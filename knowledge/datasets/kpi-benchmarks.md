# KPI Benchmarks for B2B Sales

## Overview

Key Performance Indicator (KPI) benchmarks provide the quantitative foundation for setting realistic targets, diagnosing pipeline problems, and optimizing sales performance. LeadReach uses KPI benchmarks across multiple dimensions — industry, company size, region, and channel — to power its scoring algorithms, agent recommendations, and reporting dashboards.

This document compiles 2024-2025 benchmark data from industry research firms, LeadReach's own customer base, and public company disclosures. All benchmarks represent median values unless noted; ranges indicate the interquartile range (25th-75th percentile).

```
Pipeline Velocity = (# of Opps) × (Win Rate) × (Average Deal Size) / (Average Sales Cycle Length)
```

### 3.3 Pipeline Velocity Optimization Levers

| Lever | Impact on Velocity | Typical Improvement | Time to Implement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Increase # of opportunities | Linear | +20-40% with better prospecting | 1-3 months |
