# Activity KPIs

Comprehensive KPI reference for the LeadReach platform covering activity-related performance metrics.
These benchmarks enable agents to assess prospect performance, identify improvement opportunities, and deliver data-driven recommendations.

Activity KPIs are the most granular and immediate indicators of sales execution. They measure the volume, quality, and cadence of prospecting and selling activities that ultimately produce pipeline and revenue. While activity without strategy is wasted effort, strategy without activity produces no results. The key insight is that activity KPIs must be measured both in absolute volume AND in conversion efficiency — 100 emails that produce 5 meetings are better than 200 emails that produce 2 meetings. LeadReach agents are designed to optimize the activity-to-outcome ratio, ensuring that every touch is purposeful and data-driven.

The activity KPI framework for LeadReach is organized around four interconnected measurement domains: **Outbound Activity** (volume and quality of proactive outreach), **Meeting Metrics** (volume, quality, and conversion of sales conversations), **Response Time** (speed of follow-up on inbound leads and prospect replies), and **Activity Efficiency** (the ratio of activity investment to outcome generation). Each domain contains multiple KPIs with benchmarks by role, diagnostic frameworks, and agent measurement points. Together they provide a complete picture of sales execution effectiveness.

## Outbound Activity Metrics

Outbound activity metrics track the volume and effectiveness of proactive outreach efforts. Key metrics include: emails sent per day (benchmark: 40-80 for SDRs), calls made per day (30-60 for SDRs), social touches per day (10-20 LinkedIn connections/messages), and total activities per day (80-120 combined). However, volume without quality produces diminishing returns. The activity-to-meeting conversion rate is the quality indicator: top performers achieve 3-5% email reply rates and 8-15% meeting booking rates from positive replies. Multi-channel outreach (email + phone + social) produces 2-3x higher meeting booking rates than single-channel. Activity metrics should be tracked both individually (rep performance) and by sequence/campaign (which outreach patterns produce the best results).

### Diagnostic Framework: When Activity-to-Meeting Ratios Decline

When meeting booking rates drop by 20%+ from baseline:

1. **Volume-quality tradeoff** — Has activity volume increased at the expense of personalization?
2. **Deliverability check** — Have email deliverability metrics (bounce rate, spam rate) worsened?
3. **ICP alignment** — Are activities being directed at the right personas and accounts?
4. **Message relevance** — Are subject lines and messaging resonating with current market conditions?
5. **Channel saturation** — Are prospects receiving too many touches from your team?

| Activity Pattern | Diagnosis | Action |
|-----------------|-----------|--------|
| High volume, low conversion | Quality problem | Reduce volume, increase personalization |
| Low volume, low conversion | Both quantity and quality issue | Coaching, ICP review, sequence redesign |
| High volume, declining conversion | Saturation or deliverability issue | Rotate domains, adjust sending patterns |
| Declining call connect rates | Data quality or timing issue | Verify phone numbers, adjust call times |
| Good reply rate, poor meeting rate | Discovery/booking process issue | Improve scheduling, simplify CTA |

### Leading vs. Lagging Indicators

Activity metrics are **leading indicators** for pipeline and revenue (30-90 days ahead).

- **Leading (predict meetings 1-2 weeks out):** Email open rates, reply rates, activity volume trends
- **Coincident (current activity signal):** Meeting booking rate, connect rate, positive reply rate
- **Lagging (confirmed activity outcome):** Meetings held, pipeline created, opportunities generated

## Meeting Metrics

Meeting metrics quantify the volume and quality of sales conversations. Key metrics include: meetings set per week (benchmark: 8-15 for SDRs, 6-10 for AEs), meeting show rate (75-85%), meeting-to-opportunity conversion rate (40-60%), and average meeting duration (30-45 min for discovery, 45-60 min for demo). The most predictive metric is 'meaningful meetings' — those where the prospect shared specific pain points, budget context, or timeline constraints. Top-performing teams qualify 60-70% of meetings as meaningful, while underperforming teams fall below 40%. Tracking meeting outcomes (no-show, discovery-only, qualified, advanced-to-demo, closed-lost) by source and rep reveals which activity patterns produce the highest-quality conversations.

## Response Time Metrics

Response time measures how quickly the sales team follows up on inbound leads and prospect responses. The data is unequivocal: responding within 5 minutes of an inbound inquiry increases contact rates by 900% compared to 10-minute response times (Harvard Business Review study). Benchmark response times: lead response under 5 minutes, prospect reply response under 15 minutes, meeting request response under 1 hour. Organizations using automated lead routing and real-time notifications achieve sub-5-minute response times consistently. The 'speed to lead' metric is particularly critical for inbound-heavy organizations, where the first responder wins 35-50% of deals. For outbound, response time to prospect replies should be under 15 minutes to maintain conversation momentum.

### Formula and Calculation Methodology

Median Response Time = Median value of (First Response Timestamp - Lead/Reply Timestamp)

SLA Compliance Rate = (Responses within SLA / Total Responses Required) × 100

Speed-to-Lead Score = (5 minutes / Actual First Response Time in minutes) × 100 (100 = perfect)

Average Response by Channel = Mean response time segmented by inbound channel

## Activity Cadence and Rhythm Metrics

Consistency in activity cadence is as important as absolute volume. Reps who maintain steady activity rhythms outperform those with erratic patterns, even when total volume is similar. The cadence consistency metric measures the predictability and sustainability of activity execution, which directly correlates with pipeline predictability.

### Cadence Consistency Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Benchmark | Impact |
|--------|------------|-----------|--------|
| Daily activity variance | Standard deviation of daily activity count | <20% of mean | Low variance = consistent performance |
| Week-over-week consistency | % of weeks hitting activity targets | 75-85% | Consistent weeks produce 20% more pipeline |
| Activity streaks | Consecutive days hitting activity minimums | 10-15 days avg | Streaks build momentum and habit |
| Monday/Friday effect | Activity drop on first/last day of week | <15% drop | Minimal drop = disciplined execution |
| End-of-quarter spike | Activity increase in final 2 weeks of quarter | <25% increase | Minimal spike = sustainable pace |

## Opportunity Creation Metrics

Opportunity creation tracks the translation of activities and meetings into pipeline, measuring the top-of-funnel conversion that directly feeds revenue.

|------|-------------------------------|--------------------------|----------|-------------------------|

### Opportunity Creation Rate Formulas

Opp Creation Rate = Opportunities Created / Meetings Held × 100

Opp Creation Efficiency = Opportunities Created / Total Activities × 100

Pipeline Creation Velocity = (Pipeline $ Created) / (Selling Hours Invested)
