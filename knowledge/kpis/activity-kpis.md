# Activity KPIs

Comprehensive KPI reference for the LeadReach platform covering activity-related performance metrics.
These benchmarks enable agents to assess prospect performance, identify improvement opportunities, and deliver data-driven recommendations.

## Outbound Activity Metrics

Outbound activity metrics track the volume and effectiveness of proactive outreach efforts. Key metrics include: emails sent per day (benchmark: 40-80 for SDRs), calls made per day (30-60 for SDRs), social touches per day (10-20 LinkedIn connections/messages), and total activities per day (80-120 combined). However, volume without quality produces diminishing returns. The activity-to-meeting conversion rate is the quality indicator: top performers achieve 3-5% email reply rates and 8-15% meeting booking rates from positive replies. Multi-channel outreach (email + phone + social) produces 2-3x higher meeting booking rates than single-channel. Activity metrics should be tracked both individually (rep performance) and by sequence/campaign (which outreach patterns produce the best results).

## Meeting Metrics

Meeting metrics quantify the volume and quality of sales conversations. Key metrics include: meetings set per week (benchmark: 8-15 for SDRs, 6-10 for AEs), meeting show rate (75-85%), meeting-to-opportunity conversion rate (40-60%), and average meeting duration (30-45 min for discovery, 45-60 min for demo). The most predictive metric is 'meaningful meetings' — those where the prospect shared specific pain points, budget context, or timeline constraints. Top-performing teams qualify 60-70% of meetings as meaningful, while underperforming teams fall below 40%. Tracking meeting outcomes (no-show, discovery-only, qualified, advanced-to-demo, closed-lost) by source and rep reveals which activity patterns produce the highest-quality conversations.

## Response Time Metrics

Response time measures how quickly the sales team follows up on inbound leads and prospect responses. The data is unequivocal: responding within 5 minutes of an inbound inquiry increases contact rates by 900% compared to 10-minute response times (Harvard Business Review study). Benchmark response times: lead response under 5 minutes, prospect reply response under 15 minutes, meeting request response under 1 hour. Organizations using automated lead routing and real-time notifications achieve sub-5-minute response times consistently. The 'speed to lead' metric is particularly critical for inbound-heavy organizations, where the first responder wins 35-50% of deals. For outbound, response time to prospect replies should be under 15 minutes to maintain conversation momentum.
