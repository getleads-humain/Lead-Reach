# Pricing & Packaging Guide — LeadReach Subscription Tiers and ROI Framework

## Executive Summary

LeadReach offers two product tracks—B2B Intelligence (for outbound sales teams) and B2C Setter (for appointment-setting businesses)—with a total of nine pricing tiers spanning free to enterprise custom. The B2B track includes Launchpad (free), Scout ($149/mo), Command ($399/mo), Founders' Pass ($2,497 lifetime), and Enterprise (custom). The B2C Setter track includes Setter ($97/mo), Closer ($297/mo), and Agency (custom). Each tier is designed around a specific user persona and use case, with agent count, lead volume, channel access, and team seats as the primary packaging levers.

This guide provides the complete feature comparison matrix, add-on pricing, discount structures, ROI calculation frameworks, and competitive pricing analysis. It is intended for sales teams, customer success managers, and prospects evaluating LeadReach against alternatives. All prices are in USD; annual billing provides a 17% discount equivalent to approximately 2 months free.

## ROI Calculator Framework

### Input Variables

| Variable | Description | Default | Source |
|----------|-------------|---------|--------|
| **Average Deal Size** | Revenue from closed-won deal | $25,000 | Customer input |
| **Close Rate** | % of qualified meetings that close | 20% | Customer input |
| **Meeting Booked Rate** | % of positive replies that book a meeting | 50% | LeadReach benchmark |
| **Reply Rate** | % of outreach that gets a reply | 8% | LeadReach benchmark |
| **Outreach Volume** | Emails sent per month | Tier-dependent | Plan limits |
| **Monthly Cost** | LeadReach subscription + add-ons | Tier-dependent | Pricing table |

### ROI Calculation

```
Monthly Pipeline Generated = Outreach Volume × Reply Rate × Meeting Booked Rate × Average Deal Size
Monthly Revenue = Monthly Pipeline Generated × Close Rate
Monthly ROI = (Monthly Revenue - Monthly Cost) / Monthly Cost × 100
Payback Period = Monthly Cost / Monthly Revenue × 30 (days)
```

### Example: Command Tier

```
Outreach Volume = 2,000 emails/month (conservative for 10K leads)
Reply Rate = 8% (LeadReach benchmark)
Meeting Booked Rate = 50%
Average Deal Size = $25,000
Close Rate = 20%
Monthly Cost = $399

Monthly Pipeline = 2,000 × 0.08 × 0.50 × $25,000 = $2,000,000
Monthly Revenue = $2,000,000 × 0.20 = $400,000
Monthly ROI = ($400,000 - $399) / $399 × 100 = 100,150%
Payback Period = $399 / $400,000 × 30 = 0.03 days
```

### Conservative ROI: Command Tier

Using more conservative assumptions (5% reply rate, 30% meeting rate, 10% close rate, $10K deal size):

```
Monthly Pipeline = 2,000 × 0.05 × 0.30 × $10,000 = $300,000
Monthly Revenue = $300,000 × 0.10 = $30,000
Monthly ROI = ($30,000 - $399) / $399 × 100 = 7,423%
Payback Period = $399 / $30,000 × 30 = 0.4 days
```

## Technical Details: Plan Enforcement

### Plan Gate Architecture

LeadReach enforces plan limits through a multi-layer system:

1. **API Layer**: Every API route checks the user's plan via `plan-gate.ts` before executing. If the request exceeds plan limits (leads, campaigns, seats), a 403 response is returned with a clear upgrade message.

2. **Database Layer**: Row-Level Security policies in Supabase enforce data access limits per tenant, ensuring users cannot access data beyond their subscription scope.

3. **UI Layer**: The frontend uses `use-plan-access.ts` hook to conditionally render features based on the user's plan, preventing access to locked features and displaying upgrade prompts.

4. **Agent Layer**: Agent execution checks plan limits before invoking agents that exceed the tier's agent count. Atlas respects these limits when selecting pipeline variants.

### Usage Tracking

LeadReach tracks usage metrics in real-time:
- **Leads consumed**: Count of new leads created in the current billing period
- **Agent executions**: Count of pipeline runs in the current period
- **Outreach sends**: Count of emails and messages sent
- **Enrichment operations**: Count of enrichment API calls

Usage is displayed in the billing dashboard and users receive notifications at 80% and 100% of their plan limits.

## Best Practices

1. **Start with the right tier, not the cheapest**: Under-buying leads to frustration (hit limits, missing features) and ultimately costs more in lost pipeline. Use the ROI calculator to find the tier that matches your team's capacity.

2. **Annual billing saves 17%**: If you're committed to using LeadReach for 6+ months, annual billing is the better financial decision. The savings equal approximately 2 months free.

3. **Monitor usage metrics weekly**: Track your leads consumed, agent executions, and outreach sends against your plan limits. Consistently hitting 80%+ of limits signals it's time to upgrade or add capacity.

4. **Use add-ons for spikes, upgrades for sustained growth**: If you have a one-time need (e.g., a big campaign month), add-ons are cost-effective. If you're consistently exceeding limits, upgrade to avoid per-unit costs.

5. **Leverage the Founders' Pass if available**: At $2,497 lifetime, the Founders' Pass breaks even vs. Command annual ($3,990) in approximately 7.5 months. If you plan to use LeadReach for a year or more, it's the best value.

6. **Calculate ROI with conservative assumptions**: Use the conservative ROI model (5% reply rate, 30% meeting rate, 10% close rate) to set realistic expectations. If conservative ROI is positive, actual ROI will be significantly better.

7. **Negotiate Enterprise pricing with volume data**: Come to Enterprise negotiations with data on your current lead volume, team size, and growth projections. Volume-based discounts are available but require commitment.

8. **Review plan adequacy quarterly**: As your team grows and your outbound program matures, your plan needs will change. Schedule a quarterly review with your Customer Success Manager to ensure your tier still fits.
