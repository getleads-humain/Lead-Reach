# Procurement Process Guide

Understanding the procurement process is critical for enterprise B2B sales, as procurement can either accelerate or kill deals. The typical enterprise procurement workflow includes: needs identification (business case development), vendor evaluation (RFP/RFI or informal evaluation), security and compliance review (questionnaires, certifications, penetration test results), legal review (MSA, DPA, SLA negotiation), pricing negotiation (discount tiers, payment terms), and final approval (signature authority, often requiring multiple signers). The procurement timeline ranges from 2-8 weeks for mid-market deals to 8-20 weeks for enterprise deals. Key strategies for navigating procurement include: engaging procurement early (rather than surprising them at the end), pre-completing security questionnaires using tools like Responsive or Conveyor, providing standard contract templates that reduce legal negotiation time, and understanding the prospect's procurement calendar (many companies have fiscal year-end budget deadlines that accelerate or delay purchases). LeadReach agents should: flag deals approaching procurement stage and prompt reps to engage procurement contacts, provide pre-built security documentation packages, and track procurement milestones to identify stalls that require intervention.
