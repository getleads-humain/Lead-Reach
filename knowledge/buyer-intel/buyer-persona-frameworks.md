# Buyer Persona Frameworks

Buyer personas are semi-fictional representations of ideal customers based on data and research. Effective personas go beyond demographics to include: goals and motivations (what they're trying to achieve), challenges and pain points (what's preventing achievement), information sources (where they learn about solutions), decision criteria (how they evaluate options), and buying journey stage (awareness, consideration, decision). For B2B sales, personas should be developed at two levels: the organizational persona (company size, industry, tech stack, growth stage, regulatory environment) and the individual persona (role, seniority, technical sophistication, buying authority). The most actionable personas are built from win/loss analysis and customer interviews rather than assumptions — they should be updated quarterly as market conditions and buyer preferences evolve. Each persona should map to specific: messaging frameworks (value propositions, pain point language, proof points), content assets (case studies, ROI calculators, technical documentation), outreach channels (email, LinkedIn, events, communities), and sales plays (land-and-expand, competitive displacement, expansion).
