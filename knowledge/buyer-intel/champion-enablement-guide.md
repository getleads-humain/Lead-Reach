# Champion Enablement Guide

A champion is an internal advocate who actively promotes your solution within their organization. Identifying and enabling champions is one of the highest-leverage activities in enterprise sales, as champions influence 60-70% of deal outcomes according to research. Effective champions typically share these characteristics: they personally experience the pain point your solution addresses, they have credibility and influence within their organization (often 1-2 levels below the final decision-maker), they are politically savvy and understand how decisions get made, and they are motivated by both personal gain (career advancement, solving their problem) and organizational benefit. The champion enablement playbook includes: providing them with internal selling materials (ROI calculators, competitive comparison sheets, security documentation), coaching them on how to present the solution to different stakeholders (framing value differently for CFO vs. CTO vs. end users), keeping them informed of competitive moves and new capabilities, and making them look good by delivering early wins and visible results. LeadReach agents should identify potential champions by analyzing: who engaged most actively with content, who attended multiple meetings, who asked detailed technical or process questions, and who has been at the company long enough to have organizational credibility.

## Agent Application

LeadReach agents leverage this buyer intelligence to:
- Automatically identify and classify buyer personas during prospect research
- Personalize outreach messaging based on buyer role and priorities
- Map buying committees and flag engagement gaps
- Time outreach based on buying signal detection and trigger events
- Enable champions with appropriate internal selling materials
