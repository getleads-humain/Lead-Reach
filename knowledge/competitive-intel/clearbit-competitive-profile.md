# Clearbit Competitive Profile

Clearbit (now part of HubSpot) is a data enrichment and intelligence platform that provides firmographic data, technographic signals, and intent data. Their API-first approach makes them popular for embedding data enrichment into existing workflows and tools. Clearbit's key differentiators include: real-time enrichment API that processes 250M+ API calls monthly, extensive technographic data covering 40M+ websites and technology installations, and seamless integration with marketing automation and CRM platforms. Their pricing ranges from $99/month for basic enrichment to custom enterprise plans. Key weaknesses: after the HubSpot acquisition, Clearbit's roadmap and feature development are aligned with HubSpot's ecosystem, creating potential concerns for non-HubSpot users; the contact data coverage is thinner than dedicated contact databases (ZoomInfo, Apollo); and the enrichment accuracy for small companies and startups is lower than for established enterprises. For LeadReach positioning, emphasize platform independence and agent-driven enrichment that selects the best data source for each use case, rather than being tied to a single data provider's ecosystem.

## Competitive Positioning Summary

This profile enables LeadReach agents to position against Clearbit by leveraging our advantages in AI-driven insights, real-time data freshness, and integrated agent workflows.

## Key Battle Cards

### When to Position LeadReach Over Clearbit

- When the prospect needs proactive AI agents vs. manual data lookup
- When international coverage and data accuracy are priorities
- When the buyer wants an integrated intelligence + outreach platform
- When real-time trigger events and buying signals are valued
- When compliance and data governance requirements are strict

### Anticipated Objections and Responses

- **Price objection**: LeadReach delivers higher ROI through automation and agent-driven pipeline generation
- **Incumbent comfort**: Manual processes create hidden costs in rep productivity and missed opportunities
