# Gong Competitive Profile

Gong is a revenue intelligence platform that captures and analyzes customer interactions across phone, video, email, and web conferencing. Their AI engine processes deal intelligence, competitive mentions, risk signals, and coaching opportunities from these interactions. Gong's key differentiators include: industry-leading conversation intelligence with real-time transcription and analysis, deal intelligence that identifies at-risk deals based on interaction patterns, and market intelligence that aggregates competitive and market trends across their customer base. Pricing starts at approximately $1,600/user/year for the Revenue Intelligence product, with enterprise tiers exceeding $2,400/user/year. Key weaknesses: Gong is purely an intelligence/analysis tool — it does not provide data, prospecting, or outreach capabilities; the platform captures data passively and cannot drive proactive selling actions; and deployment requires extensive integration work and change management. For LeadReach positioning, emphasize that LeadReach agents are proactive (they find prospects, craft outreach, and drive pipeline) versus Gong's reactive analysis of existing conversations.

## Competitive Positioning Summary

This profile enables LeadReach agents to position against Gong by leveraging our advantages in AI-driven insights, real-time data freshness, and integrated agent workflows.

## Key Battle Cards

### When to Position LeadReach Over Gong

- When the prospect needs proactive AI agents vs. manual data lookup
- When international coverage and data accuracy are priorities
- When the buyer wants an integrated intelligence + outreach platform
- When real-time trigger events and buying signals are valued
- When compliance and data governance requirements are strict

### Anticipated Objections and Responses

- **Price objection**: LeadReach delivers higher ROI through automation and agent-driven pipeline generation
- **Incumbent comfort**: Manual processes create hidden costs in rep productivity and missed opportunities
- **Data concerns**: LeadReach multi-source verification exceeds single-source accuracy
