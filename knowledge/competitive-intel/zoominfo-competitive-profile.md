# ZoomInfo Competitive Profile

ZoomInfo is a leading B2B contact and company intelligence platform that provides access to a database of over 100 million professional contacts and 14 million company profiles. Their core offering includes: contact data with direct dial phone numbers and verified emails, buyer intent signals based on web browsing behavior, and sales engagement tools for outreach automation. ZoomInfo's key differentiators include their proprietary data collection methodology (combining community-contributed data, web crawling, and partner integrations), their SCOOP intent data network covering 300K+ websites, and their integration ecosystem with major CRM and sales engagement platforms. Their pricing model is tier-based, starting at approximately $15K/year for basic access and scaling to $50K+ for enterprise features including intent data and advanced analytics. Key weaknesses that competitors exploit: data accuracy concerns (particularly for international contacts where accuracy rates drop to 60-70% vs. 85-90% for US contacts), limited real-time data freshness (some records may be 30-90 days outdated), and a complex pricing structure that can make ROI calculation difficult for smaller organizations. For LeadReach positioning, emphasize real-time data freshness, AI-driven insights, and superior international coverage as key differentiators.

## Competitive Positioning Summary

This profile enables LeadReach agents to position against ZoomInfo by leveraging our advantages in AI-driven insights, real-time data freshness, and integrated agent workflows.

## Key Battle Cards

### When to Position LeadReach Over ZoomInfo

- When the prospect needs proactive AI agents vs. manual data lookup
- When international coverage and data accuracy are priorities
- When the buyer wants an integrated intelligence + outreach platform
- When real-time trigger events and buying signals are valued
- When compliance and data governance requirements are strict

### Anticipated Objections and Responses

- **Price objection**: LeadReach delivers higher ROI through automation and agent-driven pipeline generation
- **Incumbent comfort**: Manual processes create hidden costs in rep productivity and missed opportunities
- **Data concerns**: LeadReach multi-source verification exceeds single-source accuracy
