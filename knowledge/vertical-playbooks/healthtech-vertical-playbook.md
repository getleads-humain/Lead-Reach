# Healthtech Vertical Playbook

The healthtech vertical playbook covers strategies for selling to healthcare technology companies, which operate under strict HIPAA regulations and have complex buying processes involving clinical, technical, and administrative stakeholders. Healthtech sub-segments include: electronic health records (EHR), telehealth platforms, digital therapeutics, clinical trial management, health data analytics, and medical device software. The healthtech buying process is among the longest in B2B (6-12 months typical) due to: HIPAA compliance requirements (business associate agreements, data encryption standards), clinical validation requirements (for products affecting patient care), and multi-stakeholder approval (clinical, IT, compliance, procurement, and often a value analysis committee). Key trigger events include: HIPAA audits or breaches (create urgency for compliance tools), new CMS regulations (change reimbursement models), funding rounds (especially for digital health startups), and hospital system mergers (create integration needs). Outreach to healthtech prospects must demonstrate: HIPAA compliance by design, understanding of clinical workflows, and references from peer healthtech organizations.
