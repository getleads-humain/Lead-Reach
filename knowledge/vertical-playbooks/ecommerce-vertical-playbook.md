# E-commerce Vertical Playbook

The e-commerce vertical playbook addresses selling to online retail and DTC companies, which have unique characteristics: high transaction volumes, seasonal demand patterns (Q4 peak), focus on customer acquisition cost and lifetime value, and technology stacks built around Shopify/Magento/commerce platforms. E-commerce sub-segments include: DTC brands (selling directly to consumers), marketplace sellers (Amazon, eBay), B2B e-commerce (wholesale platforms), and headless commerce (API-first architectures). Key selling considerations: e-commerce companies evaluate tools based on direct revenue impact (can you prove you drive sales?), integration with their commerce platform is non-negotiable, and they often prefer self-serve trials over enterprise sales processes. The buying cycle is shorter than other verticals (2-6 weeks for SMB, 4-8 weeks for enterprise e-commerce) but the bar for proving ROI is higher. Trigger events include: platform migrations (Shopify to headless), international expansion (new market entry requiring localization), and funding rounds (especially for DTC brands scaling customer acquisition).
