# Fintech Vertical Playbook

The fintech vertical playbook addresses the unique requirements of financial technology companies, which combine technology-forward cultures with heavy regulatory requirements. Fintech prospects span multiple sub-segments: payments (Stripe, Square competitors), lending (Klarna, Affirm competitors), banking-as-a-service (Marqeta, Synapse competitors), wealth management (Robinhood, Betterment competitors), and insurance (Lemonade, Hippo competitors). Each sub-segment has distinct regulatory requirements (PCI-DSS for payments, state lending licenses for lending, SEC/FINRA for wealth management) that create both barriers to entry and selling opportunities. Key selling considerations: fintech companies evaluate tools based on compliance capabilities first, then functionality and integration, and finally price; security reviews are extensive and may require 6-12 weeks; and the buying committee typically includes compliance, engineering, and business stakeholders. Trigger events unique to fintech include: regulatory changes (new compliance requirements create tool demand), funding rounds (especially Series B+ when scaling compliance operations), and partnership announcements (new distribution channels requiring additional tooling).
