# SaaS Vertical Playbook

The SaaS vertical playbook provides specialized prospecting and selling strategies for Software-as-a-Service companies. SaaS prospects share common characteristics: subscription-based revenue models with predictable ARR/MRR, product-led growth motions (for PLG companies) or sales-led motions (for enterprise SaaS), high emphasis on metrics like net revenue retention, CAC payback, and rule of 40, and technology-forward cultures that evaluate tools rigorously. Key SaaS sub-segments to target: PLG companies transitioning to sales-led (need sales tooling), late-stage startups preparing for IPO (need enterprise-grade compliance), and public SaaS companies optimizing efficiency (need analytics and automation). The SaaS buying cycle typically involves: product team evaluation (1-2 weeks), security review (2-4 weeks), procurement/legal (2-4 weeks), and executive approval (1-2 weeks). SaaS companies often have established buying processes and may require SOC 2 compliance, data processing agreements, and integration with their existing tech stack (often including Segment, Stripe, and AWS). Pricing sensitivity is moderate — SaaS companies understand subscription economics and will calculate ROI rigorously, but they also appreciate the value of good tools.
