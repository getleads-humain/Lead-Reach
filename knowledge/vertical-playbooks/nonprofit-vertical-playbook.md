# Nonprofit & NGO Vertical Playbook

The nonprofit vertical playbook addresses selling to charitable organizations, foundations, and NGOs, which have unique budget constraints, governance structures, and impact measurement requirements. Nonprofit sub-segments include: international NGOs (global operations, multiple funding sources), community-based organizations (local focus, government grants), foundations (grant-making, endowment management), and advocacy organizations (policy influence, membership management). Key selling considerations: nonprofits evaluate tools based on mission alignment and impact potential, not just ROI; budgets are often restricted by grant conditions (requiring specific allocation of funds); and the decision process involves board-level approval for significant purchases. Nonprofit technology discounts (typically 50-80% off commercial pricing through programs like TechSoup, Google for Nonprofits, and Microsoft nonprofit offers) are standard and expected. Trigger events include: new grant awards (create budget for tool purchases), leadership transitions (new executives bring new technology preferences), and audit findings (create urgency for compliance and reporting tools). Outreach to nonprofit prospects should emphasize: mission impact, affordability including nonprofit pricing, and references from peer organizations in their sub-segment.
