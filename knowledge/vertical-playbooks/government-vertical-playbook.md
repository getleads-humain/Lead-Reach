# Government & Public Sector Playbook

The government and public sector playbook addresses selling to federal, state, and local government agencies, which have unique procurement processes, compliance requirements, and budget cycles. Government procurement is characterized by: formal RFP processes (often 3-6 months from RFP to award), set-aside programs for small, women-owned, and minority-owned businesses, security requirements including FedRAMP authorization for cloud services, and budget cycles that create predictable buying windows (government fiscal years vary by entity — federal: Oct 1, most states: July 1). Key selling considerations: government buyers prioritize compliance and security above all other criteria; the sales process requires patience and relationship building over multiple procurement cycles; and contract vehicles (GSA Schedule, SEWP, CIO-SP3) provide pre-approved pathways that reduce procurement complexity. Trigger events include: new administration priorities, publicized security incidents, regulatory mandates, and budget allocations for specific initiatives. Outreach to government prospects must avoid: hard-sell tactics, unsubstantiated claims, and any appearance of impropriety. Instead, focus on: thought leadership, compliance documentation, and demonstrated understanding of their mission and challenges.
