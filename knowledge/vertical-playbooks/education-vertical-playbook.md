# Education Vertical Playbook

The education vertical playbook covers selling to higher education institutions, K-12 school districts, and edtech companies. Education institutions have unique characteristics: budget cycles aligned to academic years (July-June for most), committee-based decision-making involving faculty, IT, administration, and sometimes students, lengthy evaluation and pilot processes (often 6-12 months), and strict data privacy requirements (FERPA for student data, COPPA for children under 13). Higher education sub-segments include: research universities, liberal arts colleges, community colleges, and online learning platforms. K-12 sub-segments include: large urban districts, suburban districts, rural districts, and charter networks. Key selling considerations: education buyers respond to peer references from similar institutions, they value integrations with existing LMS systems (Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle), and they often require proof of FERPA compliance and data residency guarantees. Trigger events include: enrollment changes (growing institutions need more tools), accreditation reviews (create compliance urgency), technology refresh cycles (often tied to academic calendar), and new federal funding programs (e.g., ESSER funds during COVID). Outreach should emphasize: educational outcomes, ease of adoption for faculty and students, and cost-effectiveness given constrained budgets.
