# Manufacturing Vertical Playbook

The manufacturing vertical playbook covers strategies for selling to manufacturing companies, which are undergoing digital transformation (Industry 4.0) and represent a large but underpenetrated market for SaaS tools. Manufacturing sub-segments include: discrete manufacturing (automotive, aerospace, electronics), process manufacturing (chemicals, food & beverage, pharmaceuticals), and hybrid manufacturing. Key selling considerations: manufacturing companies are traditionally technology-laggards and may require more education about SaaS benefits; the buying process involves operations, IT, and procurement stakeholders who may have different priorities; and ROI justification often requires quantified impact on production efficiency, quality, or cost reduction. The sales cycle is typically 4-8 months with extensive evaluation periods. Key trigger events include: ERP migrations (common as legacy systems reach end-of-life), supply chain disruptions (create urgency for visibility and automation tools), and digital transformation initiatives (often announced in earnings calls or press releases). Outreach to manufacturing prospects should emphasize: practical operational improvements over technical sophistication, integration with existing ERP/MES systems, and references from similar manufacturers.
