# Discovery Call Templates

Discovery call templates provide structured frameworks for the initial sales conversation, ensuring consistent qualification while maintaining conversational flexibility. Each template includes: opening framework (rapport building and agenda setting), discovery questions organized by category (current state, pain points, desired outcomes, decision process), qualification criteria mapping (connecting answers to MEDDIC/BANT framework), and next-step proposal (suggesting appropriate next action based on qualification level). Templates are customized by segment: SMB discovery (15-20 minutes, focused on pain and budget), mid-market discovery (30-45 minutes, adds stakeholder mapping and competitive context), and enterprise discovery (45-60 minutes, includes organizational dynamics and procurement process exploration). The most effective discovery templates balance structure with adaptability — they provide a framework but allow reps to follow conversational threads while ensuring all critical information is captured. LeadReach agents use discovery templates to: prepare pre-call research briefs, generate personalized discovery questions based on prospect context, and score qualification based on discovery call outcomes.

## Template Structure

Each template includes:
- Pre-built framework with customizable variables
- Segment-specific variants (SMB, mid-market, enterprise)
- Integration with LeadReach agent workflows
- Version tracking and improvement history
