# Discovery Call Templates

Discovery call templates provide structured frameworks for the initial sales conversation, ensuring consistent qualification while maintaining conversational flexibility. Each template includes: opening framework (rapport building and agenda setting), discovery questions organized by category (current state, pain points, desired outcomes, decision process), qualification criteria mapping (connecting answers to MEDDIC/BANT framework), and next-step proposal (suggesting appropriate next action based on qualification level). Templates are customized by segment: SMB discovery (15-20 minutes, focused on pain and budget), mid-market discovery (30-45 minutes, adds stakeholder mapping and competitive context), and enterprise discovery (45-60 minutes, includes organizational dynamics and procurement process exploration). The most effective discovery templates balance structure with adaptability — they provide a framework but allow reps to follow conversational threads while ensuring all critical information is captured. LeadReach agents use discovery templates to: prepare pre-call research briefs, generate personalized discovery questions based on prospect context, and score qualification based on discovery call outcomes.

## Template Structure

Each template includes:
- Pre-built framework with customizable variables
- Segment-specific variants (SMB, mid-market, enterprise)
- Integration with LeadReach agent workflows
- Version tracking and improvement history

## Pre-Call Research Checklist

Effective discovery calls begin long before the conversation starts. Pre-call research separates average reps from top performers. The following checklist ensures consistent preparation and leverages LeadReach's agent capabilities to compile intelligence efficiently.

### Company Research (Complete Before Every Call)

- [ ] **Company overview**: Visit the company website, review the "About" page, understand the core product/service and value proposition
- [ ] **Recent news**: Search for press releases, funding announcements, product launches, and executive changes in the past 90 days
- [ ] **Financial signals**: Check for funding rounds (Crunchbase, PitchBook), revenue estimates (if public), growth indicators (job postings, office expansions)
- [ ] **Technology stack**: Use LeadReach Scout to identify the company's technology stack — CRM, marketing automation, sales engagement tools, data providers
- [ ] **Competitive landscape**: Identify top 3-5 competitors and recent competitive moves
- [ ] **Social signals**: Review the company's LinkedIn page for recent posts, employee count trends, and key hires
- [ ] **Industry context**: Understand industry-specific challenges, regulatory environment, and market dynamics
- [ ] **Trigger events**: Use LeadReach Scout to detect recent trigger events — leadership changes, funding, M&A, product launches, expansion signals

### Contact Research (Complete for Each Attendee)

- [ ] **Career trajectory**: Review LinkedIn profile — tenure at current company, previous roles, career progression pattern
- [ ] **Role and responsibilities**: Confirm current title, understand scope of role, identify likely priorities based on role
- [ ] **Content and interests**: Check for recent LinkedIn posts, articles, conference presentations, podcast appearances
- [ ] **Internal influence**: Assess likely influence level — direct decision-maker, champion, influencer, blocker?
- [ ] **Previous interactions**: Review CRM for any previous touchpoints, marketing engagement, support tickets
- [ ] **Pain point indicators**: Look for social signals suggesting challenges (e.g., posts about sales productivity, hiring for SDR roles, complaints about data quality)
- [ ] **Communication preferences**: Note preferred communication style — formal/informal, data-driven/story-driven, direct/consultative

## Discovery Call Templates by Scenario

### Template 1: First Discovery Call (Cold/Inbound Lead)

**Duration**: 20-30 minutes | **Goal**: Qualify the lead, understand initial pain, secure next meeting

**Opening Framework (3-5 minutes)**:
1. **Introduction & Rapport**: "Hi [Name], thanks for taking the time today. I'm [Your Name] with LeadReach. Before we dive in, I noticed [personalized observation from research — recent post, company news, shared connection]. How's your week been going?"
2. **Agenda Setting**: "I'd like to start by understanding what prompted our conversation and what you're hoping to get out of it. Then I'll share some context on how we've helped similar teams, and we can determine if it makes sense to continue the conversation. Does that work for you?"
3. **Permission to Take Notes**: "Mind if I take a few notes so I don't miss anything important?"

**Discovery Questions (10-15 minutes)**:
- "What prompted you to take this call today? Was there a specific challenge or event that brought this to the top of your priority list?"
- "Walk me through how your team currently handles [prospecting/lead generation/outreach]. What does that process look like day-to-day?"
- "If you could wave a magic wand and change one thing about your current process, what would it be?"
- "What have you already tried to address this? What worked and what didn't?"
- "How is this challenge impacting your team's performance? Can you quantify it — lost deals, missed targets, rep productivity?"
- "Who else on your team is feeling this pain? Are there other stakeholders who would be invested in solving this?"

**Qualification Mapping**:
| Question Category | MEDDIC Mapping | Key Signals |
|-------------------|---------------|-------------|
| Current process | Metrics | Quantifiable pain, current KPIs |
| Magic wand question | Pain | Primary pain point, desired outcome |
| Impact quantification | Metrics + Decision | Severity of problem, urgency level |
| What's been tried | Decision + Identify Process | Budget signals, evaluation timeline |
| Who else feels this | Champion + Decision | Stakeholder landscape, org dynamics |

**Next Step Proposal (3-5 minutes)**:
- **High qualification**: "This sounds like exactly what we help with. I'd like to schedule a deeper technical conversation with [relevant stakeholder] and show you how our AI agents handle this. Can we get that on the calendar for later this week?"
- **Medium qualification**: "I'm hearing some real alignment here. Let me send you a brief overview of how we've solved this for [similar company], and I'd love to schedule a 30-minute working session to map this to your specific situation. What does your calendar look like next week?"
- **Low qualification**: "I appreciate your time, [Name]. Based on what we've discussed, I'm not sure we're the best fit right now, but I'd love to stay in touch. Can I follow up in [3-6 months] when your situation may have evolved?"

### Template 2: Technical Discovery Call

**Duration**: 30-45 minutes | **Goal**: Understand technical requirements, integration needs, IT landscape, security considerations

**Opening Framework (5 minutes)**:
1. "Thanks for making time, [Name]. I understand you're the technical point of contact for evaluating new tools. I'd like to understand your current architecture and how any new solution would need to fit in. What's your focus area — more on the integration side, security, or architecture?"
2. "My goal today is to understand your technical environment and requirements so we can determine if LeadReach is technically compatible. I'll share some specifics on our architecture and integration capabilities. Sound good?"

**Technical Discovery Questions (20-30 minutes)**:
- "Walk me through your current sales tech stack. What CRM are you running, and what other tools are in the pipeline — sales engagement, data providers, marketing automation?"
- "How does data flow between your systems today? Are you using any middleware or iPaaS (like Workato, MuleSoft, Zapier)?"
- "What are your non-negotiable integration requirements? What systems absolutely must connect to any new tool?"
- "How does your team handle data governance and compliance? Are there specific regulations (GDPR, CCPA, SOC 2) that drive your requirements?"
- "What's your preferred deployment model — cloud-native SaaS, on-prem, hybrid? Any restrictions on where data can be stored or processed?"
- "How does your IT team evaluate new vendors? What's the security review process look like, and what certifications or documentation do you typically require?"
- "What's been your experience with previous tool integrations? Any lessons learned or things you'd do differently?"
- "What's your API strategy? Do you prefer native integrations, API-first tools, or custom-built connectors?"

**Technical Qualification Checklist**:

| Requirement | LeadReach Capability | Qualification Status |
|-------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| CRM Integration | Native Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach; REST API for others | ☐ Confirmed |
| Data Residency | US (default), EU (available), APAC (Enterprise) | ☐ Confirmed |
| SSO/SAML | SAML 2.0, OIDC, Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace | ☐ Confirmed |
| API Access | RESTful API v2, Webhooks, GraphQL (beta) | ☐ Confirmed |
| SOC 2 | SOC 2 Type II (annual audit) | ☐ Confirmed |
| GDPR Compliance | DPA available, data processing agreements, right to erasure | ☐ Confirmed |
| Data Encryption | AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit | ☐ Confirmed |
| Rate Limits | Standard/Professional/Enterprise tiers | ☐ Confirmed |

**Next Step**: Schedule a technical deep-dive or security review meeting with LeadReach solutions engineer. Provide security documentation package (SOC 2 report, DPA template, architecture diagram).

### Template 3: Champion Development Call

**Duration**: 30-45 minutes | **Goal**: Identify and develop an internal champion who will advocate for LeadReach

**Opening Framework (5 minutes)**:
1. "I really appreciate you carving out time, [Name]. From what I've seen, you're someone who drives real change on your team. I'm curious — what's your personal stake in improving your team's prospecting and pipeline generation?"
2. "I'd like to understand your vision for how your team could operate differently and explore whether LeadReach might be a vehicle for getting there. I'll also share how we can help you build the internal case. Fair enough?"

**Champion Discovery Questions (20-25 minutes)**:
- "What's your personal frustration with how things work today? If you could fix one thing, what would it be?"
- "What would success look like for you personally? How would your day-to-day change if this problem was solved?"
- "Who else on your team shares this frustration? Who are your natural allies in pushing for change?"
- "Who are the likely skeptics or blockers? What concerns do you think they'll raise?"
- "When you've successfully championed a new tool before, what was your approach? What worked and what didn't?"
- "What does the evaluation and purchasing process look like at your company? Who ultimately signs off?"
- "What information would you need to make a compelling internal case? ROI data? Customer references? Technical proof?"
- "What's the timeline pressure? Is there a business event or planning cycle driving urgency?"

**Champion Enablement Framework**:

| Champion Need | LeadReach Support | Resource |
|---------------|------------------|----------|
| Internal pitch deck | Co-branded presentation with their specific metrics | Bard-generated custom pitch deck |
| ROI business case | Customized ROI calculator with their inputs | ROI Calculator Template |
| Competitive comparison | Feature-by-feature battle cards vs. their current vendor | Competitive Intel Profiles |
| Technical validation | Solutions engineer for demo and proof of concept | Technical Discovery Template |
| Stakeholder alignment | Multi-threaded engagement strategy with tailored messaging | Multi-Thread Template |
| Executive sponsorship | Executive briefing with LeadReach leadership | Executive Discovery Template |
| Social proof | Customer references in their industry and segment | Customer Reference List |
| Risk mitigation | Pilot program with success criteria and exit clause | Pilot Program Template |

**Next Step**: Equip champion with internal selling materials. Schedule follow-up to review internal reaction and plan stakeholder engagement strategy.

### Template 4: Executive Discovery Call (C-Suite / VP Level)

**Duration**: 20-30 minutes | **Goal**: Understand strategic priorities, establish executive alignment, secure organizational commitment

**Opening Framework (3 minutes)**:
1. "Thank you for your time, [Name]. I know your schedule is demanding, so I'll be direct. I've been following [Company]'s growth trajectory, particularly [specific observation]. I'm curious — as you think about [relevant strategic area], what's keeping you up at night?"
2. "My objective in the next 20 minutes is to understand your strategic priorities and determine if LeadReach's approach to AI-driven pipeline generation aligns with where you're heading. If there's a fit, I'll suggest concrete next steps. If not, I'll tell you that too. Agreed?"

**Executive Discovery Questions (12-18 minutes)**:
- "What are your top 2-3 revenue priorities for this quarter/year? How does pipeline generation fit into those priorities?"
- "When you look at your sales organization's performance, where is the biggest gap between current state and target?"
- "How are you thinking about AI and automation as strategic levers for your go-to-market team?"
- "What's your current cost of customer acquisition, and how does that compare to your target?"
- "How do you benchmark your team's prospecting productivity? What does 'best-in-class' look like to you?"
- "What's the competitive landscape telling you? Are competitors gaining an edge through technology or data?"
- "If we could deliver a [specific outcome — e.g., 40% increase in qualified pipeline], how would that impact your business plan?"

**Executive Qualification Matrix**:

| Signal | Strong Positive | Neutral | Concern |
|--------|----------------|---------|---------|
| **Strategic alignment** | AI/pipeline as stated priority | Open to exploring | No strategic fit |
| **Urgency** | Active initiative underway | Planning for next quarter | No timeline |
| **Budget authority** | Can allocate budget | Influences budget | No budget visibility |
| **Measurement mindset** | Clear KPIs and benchmarks | Some metrics | Gut-driven decisions |
| **Change readiness** | Proactively seeking new solutions | Open to change | Change-averse culture |

**Next Step**: Propose executive-to-executive briefing with LeadReach leadership, or direct the opportunity to an internal sponsor for operational evaluation.

### Template 5: Demo Qualification Call

**Duration**: 20-30 minutes | **Goal**: Ensure the prospect is qualified for a full demo, understand what to show, set demo success criteria

**Opening Framework (3-5 minutes)**:
1. "Thanks for joining, [Name]. Before we schedule a full demo, I want to make sure we show you the right things at the right level. Can you help me understand where you are in your evaluation process and what you're hoping to see?"
2. "My goal is to design a demo that's relevant to your specific situation — not a generic walkthrough. To do that, I need to understand your use case, who'll be attending, and what success looks like for you."

**Demo Qualification Questions (12-18 minutes)**:
- "Where are you in your evaluation process? Are you early-stage exploration, comparing vendors, or ready to make a decision?"
- "What specific capabilities are you most interested in seeing? What problems do you want the demo to solve?"
- "Who will be attending the demo from your side? What are their roles and what will each person be evaluating?"
- "Have you seen other platforms in this space? How will you compare them to LeadReach?"
- "What would make this demo a success for you? What would you need to see to move forward?"
- "What's your decision timeline? Are there any external deadlines driving this?"
- "Is there anything you've seen or heard about LeadReach that you're particularly curious — or skeptical — about?"

**Demo Design Framework**:

| Attendee Role | Focus Area | Key Demo Points | Success Criteria |
|--------------|-----------|-----------------|------------------|
| VP Sales | Pipeline impact, ROI | Atlas orchestration, pipeline analytics | "This can increase our qualified pipeline by 30%+" |
| Sales Ops | Integration, workflow | Flow orchestration, CRM sync, automation | "This integrates cleanly and reduces manual work" |
| SDR Manager | Rep productivity | Scout discovery, Bard personalization, outreach | "My team can do 3x more with the same headcount" |
| IT/Security | Architecture, compliance | API, security, data governance | "This meets our security and compliance standards" |
| CRO/CEO | Strategic value | Executive dashboard, competitive advantage | "This gives us a sustainable edge" |

**Next Step**: Schedule tailored demo with appropriate LeadReach team (AE + SE for technical, AE alone for business-focused). Send pre-demo preparation materials and confirm attendee list.

### Template 6: Renewal Discovery Call

**Duration**: 20-30 minutes | **Goal**: Understand customer satisfaction, identify expansion opportunities, mitigate churn risk

**Opening Framework (5 minutes)**:
1. "Thanks for making time, [Name]. As we approach your renewal date, I want to make sure we're delivering real value and understand where we can do better. I'd also like to explore how we can help you get even more out of LeadReach in the next period."
2. "I'm looking for honest feedback — what's working, what's not, and where you'd like to see us invest. This is also a chance for you to tell me about any changes in your business that might affect how you use LeadReach."

**Renewal Discovery Questions (15-20 minutes)**:
- "Let's start with a temperature check — on a scale of 1-10, how well is LeadReach meeting your expectations? What would make it a 10?"
- "What's been the biggest win or 'aha moment' you've had with LeadReach? Can you quantify the impact?"
- "What's been the biggest frustration or gap? Where have we fallen short of your expectations?"
- "How has your business changed since you first signed up? Any new teams, markets, or priorities that LeadReach should support?"
- "How are your users (reps, managers, ops) actually using the platform day-to-day? Is adoption where you expected it to be?"
- "Have you evaluated any alternative solutions recently? What prompted that, if so?"
- "If you were designing the ideal next-year roadmap for LeadReach at your company, what would be on it?"

**Renewal Health Score Framework**:

| Dimension | Green (Low Risk) | Yellow (Monitor) | Red (Churn Risk) |
|-----------|------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Usage** | >80% of licensed users active weekly | 50-80% active | <50% active |
| **Value realization** | Can articulate specific ROI | Some value seen but not quantified | Cannot point to clear value |
| **Engagement** | Multiple stakeholders engaged | Single point of contact | Only CSM relationship |
| **Adoption depth** | Using 3+ agent capabilities | Using 1-2 agents | Using basic features only |
| **Satisfaction** | NPS 8-10 | NPS 6-7 | NPS 0-5 |
| **Expansion signals** | Asking about new capabilities | Mild interest in expansion | No expansion interest |
| **Competitive signals** | No competitive evaluation | Casual competitive browsing | Active vendor evaluation |

**Next Step**: Based on health score — Green: propose expansion and multi-year renewal; Yellow: create value realization plan and schedule quarterly business reviews; Red: executive escalation and custom retention plan.

### Template 8: Multi-Thread Engagement Discovery

**Duration**: 15-20 minutes per stakeholder | **Goal**: Map the full buying committee, understand each stakeholder's perspective and priorities

**Opening Framework (per stakeholder)**:
1. "Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] with LeadReach. [Champion's Name] suggested we connect because [relevant context]. I understand you're involved in [relevant area] and I'd love to get your perspective."
2. "I'm not here to sell you anything today — I genuinely want to understand how you think about [their domain] and what would make a solution valuable from your vantage point."

**Stakeholder-Specific Question Banks**:

**For the Economic Buyer (CFO, VP Finance, CRO)**:
- "How do you currently measure the ROI of your sales technology investments?"
- "What's your cost per qualified pipeline dollar today, and how does that compare to your target?"
- "When evaluating new investments, what financial metrics carry the most weight — payback period, NPV, IRR?"
- "What's your budget allocation model? Is this a new budget or would it displace an existing spend?"
- "How do you factor in opportunity cost — the cost of not investing in a solution like this?"

**For the Technical Buyer (IT Director, Sales Ops, Rev Ops)**:
- "What's your criteria for evaluating new technology? What makes a tool 'enterprise-ready' in your eyes?"
- "How do you approach integration? Do you prefer native connectors, API-first, or custom builds?"
- "What's your data governance framework? How do you evaluate vendor compliance and security posture?"
- "How do you measure the success of a technology implementation? What adoption metrics do you track?"
- "What's been your biggest challenge with previous tool rollouts? What would you do differently?"

**For the User Buyer (SDR Manager, AE Manager)**:
- "Walk me through your team's daily workflow. How much time is spent on prospecting vs. selling?"
- "What's the biggest bottleneck in your team's pipeline generation process today?"
- "How do you currently coach and develop your team's prospecting skills? What's working, what's not?"
- "If your team could get 10 more qualified conversations per week, what would that mean for your number?"
- "What tools does your team actually use day-to-day? Which ones do they love and which do they hate?"

**For the Champion (Internal Advocate)**:
- "What's your personal vision for how this team should operate? What would transformation look like?"
- "What ammunition do you need to sell this internally? What would make your case airtight?"
- "Who are your strongest allies and who needs the most convincing?"
- "What's the best way to engage each stakeholder? Any landmines I should avoid?"
- "What does the perfect outcome look like for you 6 months from now?"

### Template 9: Competitive Displacement Discovery

**Duration**: 30-40 minutes | **Goal**: Understand dissatisfaction with incumbent, identify switching triggers, assess switching costs

**Opening Framework (5 minutes)**:
1. "Thanks for your time, [Name]. I understand you're currently using [Competitor]. I'm not here to bash anyone — but I am curious about what's working and what's not with your current setup."
2. "Many of our best customers came to us after realizing their previous solution wasn't keeping up. I'd love to understand your experience and see if LeadReach's approach might address the gaps you're feeling."

**Competitive Displacement Questions (20-25 minutes)**:
- "What originally drew you to [Competitor]? What were you hoping it would do for your team?"
- "Has [Competitor] lived up to that original promise? Where has it delivered and where has it fallen short?"
- "If you could change three things about [Competitor], what would they be?"
- "How has your team's experience been with [Competitor]'s support and customer success? Responsive and helpful, or something else?"
- "What's the cost of staying with the status quo — in terms of missed opportunities, rep frustration, competitive disadvantage?"
- "What would need to be true for you to consider switching? What's your evaluation criteria?"
- "What concerns do you have about switching? Data migration, training, implementation timeline, contractual obligations?"

**Competitive Displacement Framework**:

| Switching Stage | Customer Mindset | LeadReach Strategy | Agent Support |
|----------------|------------------|-------------------|---------------|
| **Dissatisfaction** | "This isn't working as promised" | Validate pain, share similar customer stories | Sage: competitive analysis |
| **Exploration** | "There has to be something better" | Demonstrate alternative approach | Scout: show data freshness |
| **Evaluation** | "Is the switch worth the effort?" | Quantify switching ROI, minimize perceived cost | Judge: quality comparison |
| **Decision** | "I'm ready to make a change" | Offer migration support, pilot program | Flow: implementation planning |
| **Implementation** | "Make this transition painless" | White-glove onboarding, parallel run | Atlas: orchestrate migration |

**Next Step**: Provide competitive comparison materials (relevant battle card). Offer pilot program or proof of concept with clear success criteria and minimal switching risk.

## Question Banks by Persona

### C-Suite (CEO, CRO, COO)

**Strategic Questions**:
- "What are the 2-3 strategic initiatives that will define success for you this year?"
- "How does your go-to-market strategy need to evolve to hit your growth targets?"
- "What's your competitive moat? How sustainable is it, and what could erode it?"
- "How are you thinking about AI as a strategic lever — not just for efficiency, but for competitive advantage?"
- "If I asked your board how the sales organization is performing, what would they say? What would you want them to say?"

**Financial Questions**:
- "What's your current CAC (customer acquisition cost), and how has it trended over the past 12 months?"
- "What's your target pipeline-to-revenue ratio, and where are you today?"
- "How much of your sales expense is spent on activities that don't directly generate revenue (data lookup, manual research, list building)?"
- "What's the cost of a missed quarter — not just in revenue, but in market position, team morale, and board confidence?"

### VP Level (VP Sales, VP Marketing, VP Revenue)

**Operational Questions**:
- "What are your team's top 3 KPIs, and how are you performing against them right now?"
- "Where is the biggest gap between your team's current performance and your target?"
- "How do you currently balance quantity vs. quality in pipeline generation?"
- "What's your rep ramp time, and how much of that is spent learning prospecting vs. selling?"
- "How do you forecast pipeline? How confident are you in your current forecast accuracy?"

**Process Questions**:
- "Walk me through your ideal prospecting workflow from target identification to first meeting booked."
- "How does marketing-sourced pipeline compare to sales-sourced pipeline? What's the ideal mix?"
- "What's your process for identifying and prioritizing high-value accounts?"

## Objection Handling During Discovery

### Common Discovery Objections and Responses

| Objection | Root Cause | Response Framework | Example Response |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|------------------|
| **"We're not looking to change right now"** | No urgency, comfortable with status quo | Acknowledge → Create urgency → Offer value | "I hear that a lot. Out of curiosity, if you could change one thing about your current process, what would it be? [Listen] That's exactly what we hear from teams before they realize the cost of staying put..." |
| **"We already have a tool for that"** | Incumbent comfort, switching fatigue | Validate → Differentiate → Show gap | "Great — what are you using? [Listen] And how's that working for your team? Are there areas where it falls short? [Listen] That aligns with what we hear. Most teams we work with had [incumbent] but found it couldn't [specific gap]..." |
| **"I don't have budget for this"** | No allocated budget, competing priorities | Reframe → ROI → Timing | "Understood. Let me ask — if this could generate [X amount] in additional pipeline, would that change the conversation? [Listen] The teams we work with typically find that LeadReach pays for itself within [timeframe]..." |
| **"We need to involve other people"** | Not the decision-maker, committee sale | Enable → Equip → Accelerate | "Absolutely — this type of decision always involves multiple stakeholders. Who else should be in the room? [Listen] Can I help you prepare the case for them? I can provide [ROI calculator, reference customer, custom demo]..." |
| **"Your pricing is too high"** | Price sensitivity, value not established | Defend value → Compare alternatives → Show TCO | "I understand price is a factor. Let me put it in context — what's a single qualified meeting worth to your team? [Listen] If LeadReach generates even [X] additional meetings per month per rep, the ROI is [Y]x. And compared to [alternative approach — hiring more reps, manual prospecting], the TCO is significantly lower..." |
| **"We've been burned before"** | Previous bad experience with vendors | Empathize → De-risk → Offer proof | "I'm sorry to hear that. Can you share what went wrong? [Listen] That's exactly why we've designed our [implementation/pilot process] differently. We offer [proof of concept, success guarantees, exit clauses] so you can validate results before committing..." |
| **"We need to think about it"** | Indecision, missing information, hidden objection | Probe → Clarify → Next step | "Of course — what specifically do you need to think through? [Listen] Is it [concern about X, Y, or Z]? [Address specific concern]. What would help you make a decision with confidence?" |
| **"We're too small/big for this"** | Segmentation mismatch perception | Reframe → Show relevance → Customer proof | "I understand the concern. We work with companies from [size range] and tailor our approach accordingly. [For small]: Our SMB customers see [specific result] because [reason]. [For large]: Our enterprise customers choose us because [enterprise-specific capability]. Can I share a case study from a company similar to yours?" |

**A-Level (Highly Qualified) — 5-Touch, 14-Day Sequence**:

## Qualification Criteria Extraction

### From Discovery Responses to Qualification Framework

The goal of discovery is not just to have a good conversation — it's to extract specific, actionable qualification data that predicts deal outcome. Use this mapping to translate discovery responses into qualification scores.

### MEDDIC Extraction Guide

| MEDDIC Element | What to Listen For | Key Questions | Scoring Criteria |
|----------------|-------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Metrics** | Quantified pain, specific KPIs, financial impact | "How much is this costing you?" "What's the impact on your number?" | 5: Exact metrics with $ impact / 3: Directional metrics / 1: No quantification |
| **Economic Buyer** | Names and roles of budget holders, authority signals | "Who ultimately approves this investment?" "What's your approval process?" | 5: Met and engaged EB / 3: EB identified but not met / 1: EB unknown |
| **Decision Criteria** | Evaluation framework, must-haves vs. nice-to-haves | "What criteria will you use to evaluate solutions?" "What's non-negotiable?" | 5: Full criteria documented / 3: Partial criteria known / 1: Criteria undefined |
| **Decision Process** | Steps, timeline, gates, stakeholders involved | "Walk me through your evaluation and purchase process." "What are the steps and who's involved at each?" | 5: Full process mapped / 3: Process outlined / 1: Process unknown |
| **Identify Pain** | Core problem, emotional resonance, urgency | "What keeps you up at night about this?" "What happens if this doesn't get fixed?" | 5: Deep, urgent, emotional pain / 3: Stated pain but not urgent / 1: No real pain |
| **Champion** | Internal advocate, personal stake, willingness to sell | "Who on your team is most motivated to solve this?" "Would you be willing to champion this internally?" | 5: Active champion identified / 3: Potential champion / 1: No champion |
