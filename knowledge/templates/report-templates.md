# Report Templates — Master Reference

## 1. Overview

This file defines the canonical report templates used across the LeadReach platform. Reports are generated by Sage (analytics), Flow (operations), and Atlas (orchestration) to serve different stakeholders — from SDR managers tracking daily activity to CFOs reviewing quarterly pipeline.

**Report Catalog:**

| Report | Frequency | Primary Audience | Purpose |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|---------|
| Pipeline Review | Weekly | Sales managers, AEs | Assess pipeline health and deal progression |
| Weekly Forecast | Weekly | VP Sales, CRO, Finance | Predict revenue for current period |
| Monthly Revenue | Monthly | C-suite, Board | Track actual vs. target revenue |
| Campaign Performance | Weekly/Monthly | Marketing, Sales Ops | Evaluate outreach campaign effectiveness |
| Territory Analysis | Monthly | Sales Ops, VP Sales | Assess territory coverage and balance |
| Agent Performance | Weekly | Ops, Engineering | Monitor AI agent effectiveness and uptime |
| Knowledge Gap | Monthly | Knowledge Engineering | Identify missing or stale knowledge |
| Executive Dashboard | Real-time | C-suite | At-a-glance business health |

### 2.4 Risk Deal Indicators

| Risk Signal | Definition | Action | Threshold |
|-------------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Past close date | Close date has passed without close | Rep to update close date or stage | > 7 days past |
| No activity | No logged activity in 14+ days | Rep to engage or push to next quarter | > 14 days |
| Missing MEDDIC | < 50% of MEDDIC elements filled | Rep to complete qualification | < 50% |
| Stalled in stage | In same stage > 2x average | Manager review | > 2x avg days |
| Single-threaded | Only one contact on the deal | Multi-thread immediately | 1 contact |
| No next step | No next task or meeting scheduled | Rep to schedule immediately | No next step |

### 3.3 Forecast Category Definitions

| Category | Definition | Probability Range | Include In |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Commit** | Deal is done, verbal agreement, contract in process | 90–100% | Commit forecast |
| **Best Case** | Strong indication, close plan active, few obstacles | 65–89% | Best case forecast |
| **Upside** | Qualified deal with potential to close this period | 25–64% | Upside forecast |
| **Pipeline** | Early stage, unlikely to close this period | < 25% | Not in forecast |
| **Closed Won** | Deal signed and booked | 100% | Actual revenue |
| **Closed Lost** | Deal lost to competitor, no decision, or disqualification | 0% | Loss analysis |

## 10. KPI Definitions and Benchmarks

### 10.1 Pipeline KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Formula | Benchmark | Calculation Frequency |
|-----|-----------|---------|-----------|----------------------|
| Pipeline Coverage Ratio | Pipeline relative to quota | Total Pipeline / Quota | 3–4x | Weekly |
| Weighted Pipeline | Probability-adjusted pipeline value | Σ(Deal Value × Probability) | ≥ Quota | Weekly |
| Pipeline Velocity | Speed at which deals move through stages | (# Deals × Win Rate × Avg Deal Size) / Avg Sales Cycle | Increasing MoM | Monthly |
| Stage Conversion Rate | % of deals advancing from one stage to next | Deals Advanced / Deals Entered Stage | Varies by stage | Weekly |
| Average Sales Cycle | Days from first touch to close | Avg(Close Date - Created Date) | 45–90 days | Monthly |
| Pipeline Created per SDR | New pipeline generated per SDR per month | New Pipeline / # SDRs | $100K–$300K/month | Monthly |
| Pipeline per AE | Total pipeline per AE | Total Pipeline / # AEs | $500K–$2M | Weekly |

### 10.2 Revenue KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Formula | Benchmark | Calculation Frequency |
|-----|-----------|---------|-----------|----------------------|
| ARR | Annualized Recurring Revenue | MRR × 12 | — | Monthly |
| MRR | Monthly Recurring Revenue | Sum of active subscriptions / 12 | — | Monthly |
| Net New Revenue | New revenue minus churn and contraction | New Biz + Expansion - Contraction - Churn | Positive | Monthly |
| Net Revenue Retention | Revenue retention including expansion | (Starting MRR + Expansion - Contraction - Churn) / Starting MRR | > 110% | Monthly |
| Logo Retention | Customer count retention | Customers End of Period / Customers Start of Period | > 90% | Quarterly |
| ACV | Annual Contract Value | Total New ARR / Number of New Deals | $15K–$150K | Monthly |
| Average Deal Size | Mean revenue per closed deal | Total Won Revenue / Number of Won Deals | — | Monthly |
| Win Rate | % of deals that close won | Won Deals / (Won + Lost Deals) | 20–35% | Monthly |
| Revenue per Rep | Revenue per quota-carrying rep | Total Revenue / # Quota-Carrying Reps | $500K–$1.5M | Quarterly |

### 10.3 Activity KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Formula | Benchmark | Calculation Frequency |
|-----|-----------|---------|-----------|----------------------|
| Emails Sent per SDR/Week | Volume of outreach emails | Total Emails / # SDRs / Weeks | 50–100 | Weekly |
| Email Reply Rate | % of emails receiving a reply | Replies / Emails Delivered | 5–10% (cold), 15–25% (warm) | Weekly |
| Meeting Set Rate | % of conversations resulting in a meeting | Meetings / Conversations | 15–30% | Weekly |
| Calls per SDR/Day | Phone call volume | Total Calls / # SDRs / Days | 20–40 | Daily |
| Call Connect Rate | % of calls reaching a live person | Connects / Call Attempts | 12–18% | Weekly |
| LinkedIn Touches per SDR/Week | LinkedIn activity volume | LinkedIn Actions / # SDRs / Weeks | 30–60 | Weekly |
| Cadence Completion Rate | % of cadences fully executed | Completed Cadences / Started Cadences | 60–75% | Weekly |

### 10.4 Efficiency KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Formula | Benchmark | Calculation Frequency |
|-----|-----------|---------|-----------|----------------------|
| CAC | Customer Acquisition Cost | Total S&M Spend / New Customers | $500–$2,000 | Quarterly |
| CAC Payback | Months to recover acquisition cost | CAC / (MRR × Gross Margin) | < 18 months | Quarterly |
| LTV | Customer Lifetime Value | ARPU × Gross Margin / Churn Rate | 3× CAC | Quarterly |
| LTV:CAC Ratio | Lifetime value relative to acquisition cost | LTV / CAC | > 3:1 | Quarterly |
| Cost per Meeting | Cost to generate one qualified meeting | Total SDR Cost / Meetings Set | $150–$400 | Monthly |
| Cost per Pipeline Dollar | Cost to generate $1 of pipeline | Total S&D Cost / Pipeline Created | $0.05–$0.15 | Monthly |
| SDR Ramp Time | Months for new SDR to reach full productivity | Months to achieve quota | 3–6 months | Quarterly |
| Rep Productivity Index | Output relative to capacity | (Meetings + Pipeline) / (Target Meetings + Target Pipeline) | > 0.8 | Monthly |

### 10.5 Quality KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Formula | Benchmark | Calculation Frequency |
|-----|-----------|---------|-----------|----------------------|
| Lead Quality Score | Average ICP score of leads entering pipeline | Avg(ICP Score) | > 60 | Weekly |
| Forecast Accuracy | How close forecast is to actual | 1 - |Forecast - Actual| / Actual | > 85% | Weekly |
| Forecast Bias | Systematic over/under-forecasting | (Forecast - Actual) / Actual | < ±10% | Monthly |
| Data Completeness | % of required CRM fields populated | Populated Fields / Required Fields | > 90% | Weekly |
| Scoring Accuracy | How well ICP scores predict outcomes | Correlation(Score, Won/Lost) | > 0.7 | Monthly |
| Agent Uptime | System availability | (Total Time - Downtime) / Total Time | > 99.9% | Real-time |
| Agent Latency P95 | 95th percentile response time | P95(Response Time) | < 3 seconds | Real-time |
| Compliance Catch Rate | % of violations caught by Judge | Violations Caught / Total Violations | > 99% | Weekly |
