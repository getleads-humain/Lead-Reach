# Territory Planning Guide

Territory planning is the strategic allocation of accounts and geographic regions to sales representatives to maximize coverage and minimize overlap. Effective territory planning balances four factors: market potential (total addressable accounts by segment), rep capacity (number of accounts a rep can effectively manage), historical performance (which territories have produced the most revenue), and equity (fair distribution of opportunity across the team). Modern territory planning uses data-driven scoring models that assign each account a potential score based on firmographics (company size, industry, technology stack), intent signals (buying activity), and relationship signals (existing engagement, referrals). The most common territory models include: geographic territories (simple but may create unequal opportunity), named-account territories (best for enterprise with concentrated accounts), industry-vertical territories (enables deep domain expertise), and hybrid models (combining elements based on go-to-market strategy). Territory planning should be reviewed quarterly and rebalanced annually, as market conditions, rep performance, and company strategy evolve. LeadReach agents support territory planning by: scoring accounts for potential, identifying whitespace territories with high potential but low coverage, and recommending territory adjustments based on pipeline data.
