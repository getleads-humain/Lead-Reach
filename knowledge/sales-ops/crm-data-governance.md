# CRM Data Governance

CRM data governance establishes the policies, processes, and tools that ensure data quality, consistency, and compliance across the CRM system. Poor CRM data quality costs organizations 15-25% of rep productivity through: incorrect contact data leading to wasted outreach, duplicate records creating conflicting activity histories, missing fields preventing accurate lead scoring and routing, and stale data causing reps to pursue prospects who have changed roles or companies. A comprehensive data governance framework includes: data standards (naming conventions, required fields, format rules), data entry workflows (validation at point-of-entry, mandatory fields, auto-enrichment), data maintenance processes (deduplication schedules, decay monitoring, enrichment triggers), and data audit procedures (regular completeness and accuracy audits, exception reporting). The most effective data governance programs automate enforcement where possible: required fields prevent incomplete records, validation rules catch format errors, deduplication runs automatically, and enrichment fills gaps at point-of-entry. LeadReach agents support data governance by: enriching records with missing data before they enter the pipeline, flagging records that fail quality checks, and maintaining data freshness by updating records when changes are detected.
