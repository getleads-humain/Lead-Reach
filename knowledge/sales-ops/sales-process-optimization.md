# Sales Process Optimization

Sales process optimization is the continuous improvement of the defined stages, activities, and exit criteria that govern how deals progress from lead to close. A well-defined sales process increases win rates by 15-25% and reduces ramp time by 30-40% compared to organizations without a formal process. The key elements of an optimized sales process include: clearly defined stages (6-8 stages for enterprise, 3-5 for SMB), specific exit criteria for each stage (verifiable evidence required to advance), required activities at each stage (discovery call, demo, proposal, etc.), and defined handoff points between roles (SDR to AE, AE to CS). Process optimization should be data-driven: analyze historical win/loss data to identify which activities and milestones correlate most strongly with wins, then build the process around those success patterns. Common optimization opportunities include: reducing time-in-stage for bottleneck stages, implementing mandatory next-step documentation at each stage, and standardizing the qualification criteria to prevent unqualified deals from consuming AE time. LeadReach agents enforce process compliance by: flagging deals missing stage-appropriate activities, prompting reps for next-step documentation, and automatically advancing deals when exit criteria are met.
