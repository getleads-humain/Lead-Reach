# Vietnam — Business Landscape & B2B Prospecting Guide

## 1. Country Overview

Vietnam is a **$400B+ economy growing 6-7% annually**, with a population of 100M+ and a rapidly industrializing export economy. It has emerged as the **#1 alternative to China** for manufacturing diversification ("China + 1" strategy), and is one of the world's largest exporters of agriculture (coffee, rice, dragonfruit, cashews, pepper), textiles, electronics (Samsung produces ~50% of global smartphones in Vietnam), and furniture.

**Key facts**:
- **GDP**: ~$430B (2024), growing ~6-7%/year
- **Population**: ~100M, median age 32 (young, working-age population)
- **Currency**: Vietnamese đồng (VND), ~24,000 VND/USD
- **Government**: Single-party communist state; stable but controlled
- **Language**: Vietnamese (official); English common in business; Chinese, Japanese, Korean also used in trade
- **Time zone**: ICT (UTC+7)
- **Internet penetration**: ~80%; mobile-first
- **Major cities**: Ho Chi Minh City (HCMC, economic capital), Hanoi (political capital), Da Nang, Hai Phong, Can Tho

## 2. Business Registration & Identification

### The National Business Registration Portal
**Website**: `masothue.com` or `dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn`

Every Vietnamese company has a **Mã số thuế (tax code)** — a 10-digit number that uniquely identifies the business. This is the equivalent of an EIN (US) or Companies House number (UK).

**Lookup process**:
1. Search by tax code, company name (Vietnamese), or business owner name
2. Returns: legal name, English name (if any), registered address, business type, founding date, business lines (with VSIC codes), legal representative, status (active/suspended/dissolved)

### Vietnam SIC (VSIC)
Vietnam Standard Industrial Classification — 5-digit codes, modeled on ISIC rev 4. Useful for filtering companies by industry. Common codes for export-focused businesses:
- **0126** — Fruit growing (incl. dragonfruit, banana, mango)
- **1020** — Coastal fishing
- **1080** — Spinning
- **1310** — Textile finishing
- **1410** — Wearing apparel
- **1511** — Footwear
- **2410** — Iron and steel
- **2610** — Electronic components
- **3100** — Furniture

### The General Department of Taxation
**Website**: `gdt.gov.vn` — Public data on tax compliance. Companies with tax delinquencies are listed publicly.

### Vietnam Chamber of Commerce and Industry (VCCI)
**Website**: `vcci.com.vn` — Member directory; VCCI members tend to be more internationally oriented.

### VIETRADE (Vietnam Trade Promotion Agency)
**Website**: `vietrade.gov.vn` — Government agency promoting Vietnamese exports. Maintains exporter database by product category.

## 3. Key Industries & Regional Hubs

### Agriculture & Food (Strength Area)
- **Dragonfruit** (thanh long): Binh Thuan province (largest), Tien Giang, Long An
- **Coffee**: Central Highlands (Dak Lak, Lam Dong, Gia Lai) — Vietnam is #2 global producer (after Brazil)
- **Rice**: Mekong Delta (Can Tho, An Giang, Dong Thap)
- **Cashews**: Binh Phuoc, Dong Nai
- **Pepper**: Gia Lai, Dak Lak, Ba Ria-Vung Tau
- **Seafood**: Mekong Delta (shrimp), Central coast (catfish, tuna)
- **Tea**: Thai Nguyen, Lam Dong

### Manufacturing
- **Electronics**: Bac Ninh, Bac Giang (Samsung, LG, Intel facilities) — Northern Vietnam
- **Textiles/Garments**: HCMC, Binh Duong, Dong Nai — Southern Vietnam
- **Footwear**: HCMC, Binh Duong, Hai Phong
- **Furniture**: Binh Duong, Dong Nai, HCMC
- **Automotive/Motorcycles**: VinFast (Hai Phong), Honda, Yamaha plants in Northern Vietnam

### Technology & Software
- **HCMC** (District 1, District 7, Thu Thiem) — Most software firms
- **Hanoi** (Cau Giay, Nam Tu Liem) — Government tech, fintech
- **Da Nang** — Emerging tech hub, lower cost
- Notable Vietnamese tech firms: VNG (gaming, payments), FPT (IT services), Vingroup (VinBigData, VinFast), Tiki, Shopee Vietnam, MoMo (fintech), Sky Mavis (Axie Infinity)

### Tourism & Hospitality
- Major destinations: Hanoi, HCMC, Da Nang, Hoi An, Nha Trang, Phu Quoc, Ha Long Bay, Sapa
- Post-COVID recovery in progress

### Energy
- **PetroVietnam** — State oil & gas
- **EVN** — State electricity
- **Renewables**: Solar in south, wind in central coast; growing rapidly

## 4. Business Culture & Communication Norms

### Hierarchy and Face
- Vietnamese business culture is **hierarchical and face-conscious**
- Decisions flow from senior to junior; defer to the senior person in the room
- **Saving face** is critical — never publicly correct or embarrass a Vietnamese counterpart
- **"Yes"** does not always mean agreement — it may mean "I hear you" or "I will try"
- Direct refusal is impolite; "I will consider" or "maybe" often means "no"

### Relationship Before Business
- Vietnamese do business with **people they trust**, not brands
- First meetings are about establishing rapport, not closing deals
- **Drinking (bia hơi, rice wine)** and **meals** are common trust-building activities
- Expect 3-5 meetings before substantive business discussion
- Personal connections (quan hệ) are critical — warm introductions are 10× more effective than cold outreach

### Communication Channels
Vietnamese business communication is **multi-channel but mobile-first**:

1. **Zalo** — #1 messaging app in Vietnam; ~70M users; preferred for business communication
2. **WhatsApp** — Common for international trade; widely used by exporters
3. **WeChat** — Common for trade with China
4. **Email** — Used but secondary; responses are slower (24-48 hours typical)
5. **Phone** — Preferred for urgent matters
6. **LinkedIn** — Growing but limited; more common among younger professionals and tech sector
7. **Facebook** — Used for business (especially via Facebook Pages and Messenger); older demographic

### Language
- **Vietnamese** is the working language
- **English** proficiency varies by generation and industry:
  - Tech sector: high English proficiency
  - Export-oriented businesses: medium-high
  - Domestic-focused businesses: low-medium
- Always have **bilingual** materials when reaching out (Vietnamese + English)
- Use simple English; avoid idioms, slang, complex sentence structures

### Business Cards
- Still widely used; present and receive with **both hands**
- Receive cards with respect; do not immediately pocket — read and acknowledge
- Have bilingual cards (Vietnamese on one side, English on other) if doing repeat business

### Meetings
- **Punctuality** is expected for visitors; Vietnamese counterparts may be 5-15 min late
- **Small talk** before business is essential (family, weather, food, travel)
- **Tea** is served at virtually every meeting
- Decisions may be deferred — "we will discuss internally and respond" is common
- Follow up politely — do not push for immediate decisions

### Holidays
- **Tet (Lunar New Year)**: Late January or early February; **no business for 2 weeks**. Plan around this.
- **Hung Kings Festival**: April (1 day)
- **Reunification Day**: April 30
- **Labor Day**: May 1
- **Independence Day**: September 2
- **Summer vacation**: July-August (lighter business activity)

## 5. Trade Documentation & Compliance

### Export Documentation
- **Phytosanitary certificate** — Issued by Plant Protection Department (PPD); required for plant exports
- **Certificate of Origin (C/O)** — Issued by VCCI or Ministry of Industry and Trade; needed for preferential tariffs (Form D for ASEAN, Form E for China, Form AK for Korea, etc.)
- **Commercial Invoice** — In English; standard format
- **Packing List** — Detailed; required by importers
- **Bill of Lading** — Issued by shipping line; standard
- **Health Certificate** — For animal products; issued by MARD (Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development)

### Key Regulators
- **MARD** (Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development) — Agriculture exports
- **MOIT** (Ministry of Industry and International Trade) — Trade policy
- **General Department of Customs** (`customs.gov.vn`) — Import/export procedures
- **VCCI** — C/O issuance, trade documentation
- **SBV (State Bank of Vietnam)** — Banking regulation, foreign exchange

### Quality & Safety Certifications
- **VietGAP** — Vietnamese Good Agricultural Practices (basic standard)
- **GlobalG.A.P.** — International standard; required for EU/US exports
- **Organic** — EU Organic, USDA Organic, JAS (Japan)
- **HACCP** — Food safety management
- **BRC, IFS** — European food safety standards
- **Fair Trade** — For coffee, tea, cocoa
- **UTZ / Rainforest Alliance** — Sustainable agriculture

## 7. Vietnamese Business Names — Naming Conventions

Vietnamese business names typically follow patterns:
- **Công ty TNHH [Name]** — Limited Liability Company (most common)
- **Công ty Cổ phần [Name]** — Joint Stock Company
- **Công ty [Name]** — Company
- **Doanh nghiệp tư nhân [Name]** — Private enterprise

The English version often prefixes with:
- "[Name] Co., Ltd."
- "[Name] JSC"
- "[Name] Vietnam"

For dragonfruit specifically, common company names include:
- Thanh Long (means dragonfruit)
- Dragon Fruit
- Kim (golden) + Name
- Hoan (complete) + Name
- Phat (wealth) + Name

## 8. Outreach Best Practices for Vietnam

### Channel Priority
1. **WhatsApp** — best for first contact if you have a phone number
2. **Email** — best for formal inquiry; send bilingual if possible
3. **Zalo** — best if you're already in their network
4. **LinkedIn** — best for tech sector
5. **Phone** — best for urgent / established relationships

### Email Template (Export Inquiry)
```
Subject: [Product] import inquiry - [Your Country]

Dear [Name or "Sir/Madam" if name unknown],

I am [Your Name] from [Your Company] in [Your Country]. We are looking for reliable [product] suppliers and found [their company] through [source - e.g., VIETRADE / VASEP / Alibaba].

Could you share:
- Current export markets
- Available certifications (GlobalG.A.P., organic, etc.)
- MOQ and pricing FOB [Vietnam port: Cat Lai / Cai Mep / Hai Phong]
- Lead time
- Payment terms

If you have a catalog or product specifications, please share.

We typically work with [payment terms: LC at sight / 30% TT advance + 70% against BL copy] and would be ready to place a trial order of [volume] within [timeframe].

Looking forward to your reply.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Your Company]
[Phone / WhatsApp]
[Website]
```

### WhatsApp Message
```
Hello [Name], this is [Your Name] from [Your Company] in [Your Country]. We are interested in [product] and found your company online. Could you share your catalog and FOB pricing? Thank you.
```

### Cultural Tips
- Use **Mr. / Ms. + first name** (Vietnamese often use given name, not family name, in business)
- Address senior people as **"Anh"** (older brother) or **"Chị"** (older sister) for respect, or **"Ông"** (Mr.) / **"Bà"** (Mrs.) for very senior
- **Always** acknowledge Tet and major holidays
- **Don't** push for quick decisions — relationship-building takes time
- **Don't** discuss politics, the war, or human rights
- **Do** compliment Vietnamese culture, food, progress
- **Do** accept invitations to meals — these are critical for relationship-building

### Pricing & Negotiation
- Vietnamese exporters expect **negotiation** — initial quotes often have 10-20% buffer
- **Volume discounts** are significant — ask about tiered pricing
- **Payment terms**: 
  - LC at sight — most secure for buyer, common for first orders
  - 30% TT advance + 70% against B/L copy — common for established relationships
  - 100% TT advance — only for very small orders or trusted relationships
- **INCOTERMS**: FOB (most common for Vietnam exports), CIF, EXW

### Compliance Notes
- **Sanctions screening** — Vietnam itself is not sanctioned, but some Vietnamese companies have been sanctioned for trade with North Korea, Iran — screen against OFAC list
- **Anti-bribery** — Vietnam has a Corruption Perceptions Index score of 42/100 (moderate); ensure FCPA compliance
- **Labor standards** — Verify fair labor practices, especially in textiles and footwear
- **Customs compliance** — Ensure HS code accuracy; misclassification can delay clearance

## 9. Common Pitfalls in Vietnamese Prospecting

### Pitfall 1: Treating Vietnam Like China
Vietnam has different business norms, holidays (Tet, not Spring Festival), regulations, and language. Don't assume Chinese patterns transfer.

### Pitfall 2: Ignoring Provincial Differences
A dragonfruit supplier in Binh Thuan is different from one in Tien Giang — different harvest seasons, varieties, transportation logistics. Know the province.

### Pitfall 3: Email-Only Outreach
Vietnamese exporters respond to WhatsApp 5-10× faster than email. Always try WhatsApp first if you have a phone number.

### Pitfall 4: No Bilingual Materials
English-only materials limit your reach. Have Vietnamese translations ready for important documents.

### Pitfall 5: Wrong Honorifics
Vietnamese names are **Family Name + Middle Name + Given Name**. Address people by given name + title (Mr./Ms.), NOT by family name. e.g., "Mr. Nguyen Van Minh" is wrong — it's "Mr. Minh".

### Pitfall 6: Disrespecting Tet
Outreach during Tet (late Jan/Feb) will be ignored. Plan around the 2-week holiday.

### Pitfall 7: Not Verifying Companies
Many "Vietnamese suppliers" on B2B marketplaces are trading companies, not producers. Verify via tax code lookup on `masothue.com`.
