# United States — Regional Playbook

## 1. Market Shape

The United States is the largest, fastest-moving, and most competitive B2B outbound market in the world. The defining characteristics are: **high tolerance for cold outreach** (B2B cold email is generally permitted at the federal level under CAN-SPAM), **state-level privacy laws layered on top** (CCPA/CPRA in California, Colorado Privacy Act, Connecticut Data Privacy Act, Virginia VCDPA, Utah UCPA, and more), **fragmented regulation by state** for certain industries (money transmission, insurance, healthcare), and **a sophisticated buyer ecosystem** that expects personalization, multi-channel sequences, and fast follow-up.

Sales cycles are the shortest in the world for SaaS B2B (30–90 days typical). ACVs are the highest in the world. Competition is intense — buyers receive 20+ cold emails per day, so differentiation through signal-based personalization is essential.

LeadReach is headquartered in the US market and treats it as the primary GTM. This playbook covers federal + state regulations, channel preferences, and regional variants within the US.

## 2. Federal Regulations

### CAN-SPAM Act (2003)

CAN-SPAM governs commercial email in the United States. Key requirements:

- **No prior consent required** for B2B commercial email (this is the major difference from GDPR).
- **Sender identification**: Must include a valid physical postal address in every email.
- **Opt-out mechanism**: Must include a clear, working unsubscribe link; honor requests within 10 business days.
- **No deceptive subject lines**: Subject must accurately reflect the email content.
- **No false headers**: From name and email must be accurate.

Penalties: up to $51,744 per email non-compliance (FTC, 2024 adjustment).

### Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA, 1991)

TCPA governs telemarketing calls and SMS in the United States. Key requirements:

- **Prior express written consent** required for autodialed or prerecorded marketing calls/SMS to wireless numbers.
- **National Do-Not-Call Registry**: Must scrub against the registry every 31 days for manual telemarketing calls.
- **Calling time restrictions**: 8am–9pm local time of the called party.
- **Internal DNC list**: Must maintain an internal do-not-call list and honor requests for 5 years.

Penalties: $500–$1,500 per call/SMS non-compliance. Class actions are common.

> **LeadReach recommendation**: For B2B cold calls to landlines, TCPA is more permissive. For cold calls to mobile numbers, treat as if consent is required — most business mobiles are now indistinguishable from landlines.

**Practical implication**: Treat all US outreach as if CCPA applies (the strictest generally-applicable state law). Provide opt-out, honor DSARs within 30 days, do not "sell" or "share" personal data without opt-out.

### Industry-Specific Federal Regulations

- **HIPAA** (HealthTech): Required for any vendor touching PHI.
- **GLBA** (FinTech, financial services): Governs nonpublic personal information.
- **FCRA** (Consumer reporting): Governs data used for credit, insurance, employment decisions.
- **FTC Act Section 5**: Prohibits unfair or deceptive practices — applies to all commercial messaging.

## 3. Outreach Channels & Preferences

| Channel | Permission Required | Best Practice |
|---------|---------------------|---------------|
| Cold email (B2B) | No (CAN-SPAM) | Include physical address + unsubscribe; honor within 10 days |
| Cold call (B2B landline) | No ( DNC scrub required) | Scrub against National DNC every 31 days; honor internal DNC |
| Cold call (B2B mobile) | Effectively yes (TCPA risk) | Treat as if consent required; use manual dialing, not auto-dialer |
| SMS (B2B) | Yes (TCPA) | Prior express written consent required |
| LinkedIn connection | No (LinkedIn ToS) | Personalize; do not pitch in first message |
| LinkedIn InMail | No (LinkedIn ToS) | Use sparingly; high response rate when personalized |
| Direct mail | No | High-trust verticals respond well (FinTech, HealthTech, Manufacturing) |

## 4. Regional Variants Within the US

### US Northeast (NY, MA, CT, NJ, PA)

- **Industry concentration**: Financial services, media, pharma, professional services.
- **Buyer tone**: Direct, time-pressured, values brevity.
- **Channel preference**: Email + LinkedIn; cold calls answered less frequently.
- **Cycle length**: 30–60 days for SaaS B2B.

### US Midwest (IL, MI, OH, IN, WI, MN)

- **Industry concentration**: Manufacturing, insurance, agriculture, healthcare systems.
- **Buyer tone**: Relationship-driven, values trust and references.
- **Channel preference**: Cold call + email; direct mail effective.
- **Cycle length**: 60–120 days for enterprise manufacturing.

### US South (TX, FL, GA, NC, TN, AL)

- **Industry concentration**: Energy, aerospace, healthcare, FinTech (Charlotte, Austin, Miami).
- **Buyer tone**: Friendly, values face-to-face and trade shows.
- **Channel preference**: Email + LinkedIn + trade show follow-up.
- **Cycle length**: 45–90 days for SaaS B2B.

### US West (CA, WA, OR, CO, AZ)

- **Industry concentration**: SaaS, FinTech, media, ecommerce, semiconductor.
- **Buyer tone**: Tech-savvy, values innovation and case studies.
- **Channel preference**: Email + LinkedIn; cold calls rarely answered.
- **Cycle length**: 30–60 days for SaaS B2B.
- **Compliance note**: California has the strictest state privacy law (CCPA/CPRA).

## 5. Time Zones & Business Hours

The US spans 4 time zones (Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific) plus Alaska and Hawaii. Best practice for cold calls:

- **East Coast**: 9am–6pm ET.
- **Central**: 9am–5pm CT (10am–6pm ET).
- **Mountain**: 9am–5pm MT (11am–7pm ET).
- **West Coast**: 9am–5pm PT (12pm–8pm ET).

Aim to send cold emails Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am local time of the recipient. Monday mornings are saturated; Friday afternoons are ignored.

## 6. Localizing the Messaging

- **Tone**: Confident, direct, metric-heavy. Use numbers (e.g., "30% pipeline lift in 60 days") over adjectives.
- **References**: US case studies and recognizable US brand names convert best.
- **Pricing**: Quote in USD. Annual contracts are standard; month-to-month is unusual.
- **Legal**: Have an MSA + DPA template ready (US-style, mutual NDAs preferred).
- **Compliance badges**: SOC 2 Type II is the baseline. ISO 27001 is a plus. HITRUST for healthcare. PCI-DSS for fintech.

## 7. Common Pitfalls for Outbound to the US

1. **Ignoring state-level privacy laws**. CCPA applies to any company doing business in California and meeting thresholds — most B2B SaaS does.
2. **Failing to honor unsubscribes within 10 business days** (CAN-SPAM requirement).
3. **Auto-dialing mobile numbers without prior express written consent** (TCPA trap).
4. **No physical address in cold emails** (CAN-SPAM requirement).
5. **Calling outside 8am–9pm local time** (TCPA requirement).
6. **Not scrubbing against the National DNC Registry** (every 31 days).
7. **Sending on Monday morning or Friday afternoon** (lowest open rates).
8. **Not personalizing** — US buyers receive 20+ cold emails per day; generic outreach is ignored.

## 8. LeadReach Recommendations for US Outbound

- **Default channel mix**: Email + LinkedIn + Cold Call (in that order).
- **Sequence length**: 14–21 days for SaaS, 21–28 days for FinTech, 28–35 days for Manufacturing.
- **Touch count**: 6–10 touches across the sequence.
- **Personalization level**: Minimum 2 personalized lines per email (recent funding, hiring, product launch, leadership change).
- **CTA**: Soft for first 3 touches ("worth a 15-min call?"); hard for last 2 ("Tuesday at 2pm ET?").
- **Breakup email**: Always include at day 14–21; well-written breakup emails have a 5–10% reply rate.
- **Compliance**: Use a consent + opt-out platform (OneTrust, Termly, or similar). Honor unsubscribes within 10 business days.
