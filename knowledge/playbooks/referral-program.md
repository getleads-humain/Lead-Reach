# Referral Program — Playbook

This playbook is the canonical reference for building, managing, and scaling LeadReach's customer referral program. It covers referral source identification, advocate nurturing, incentive design, referral tracking, and conversion optimization. Used by Atlas (Orchestrator), Scout (Advocate Identification), Bard (Referral Outreach), Flow (Program Operations), and Echo (Program Analytics).

## 1. Why Referral Programs Matter in B2B

Referral-sourced deals close at **3-4x the rate of cold outbound** and have **25% higher ACV** than non-referral deals (Texas A&M University / RTC study, 2024). In B2B SaaS, the impact is even more pronounced because:

- **Trust is pre-established**: The referrer's endorsement transfers credibility that no marketing can buy.
- **Sales cycles are shorter**: Referred prospects skip the trust-building phase; average cycle is 40% shorter.
- **Retention is higher**: Referred customers churn at 18% lower rates because they entered with accurate expectations.
- **CAC is dramatically lower**: The cost to acquire a referred customer is 30-50% less than outbound-sourced.

Yet most B2B companies treat referrals as accidental — they happen when they happen. LeadReach treats referrals as a **systematic, measurable, scalable channel** with dedicated agents, processes, and incentives.

## 2. Referral Source Taxonomy

### Source Type 1: Customer Advocates (60-70% of referral volume)

Existing LeadReach customers who refer peers. Sub-categories:

| Sub-Category | Profile | Referral Pattern | Motivation |
|-------------|---------|-----------------|------------|
| Executive Advocate | VP/C-level at customer | Refers 1-2 per year; high quality | Social capital + incentives |
| Power User Advocate | SDR Manager / RevOps IC | Refers 3-5 per year; moderate quality | Recognition + incentives |
| Community Advocate | Active in LeadReach community | Refers 5-10 per year; variable quality | Community status + swag |

### Source Type 2: Partner Advocates (15-20% of referral volume)

Implementation partners, agencies, and technology partners who refer their clients.

| Sub-Category | Profile | Referral Pattern | Motivation |
|-------------|---------|-----------------|------------|
| Implementation Partner | RevOps consultancy | Refers 2-4 per quarter; high quality | Revenue share |
| Agency Partner | Sales enablement agency | Refers 1-3 per quarter; moderate quality | Value-add for client + revenue share |
| Technology Partner | CRM / data vendor | Refers 1-2 per quarter; variable quality | Ecosystem value |

### Source Type 3: Internal Advocates (10-15% of referral volume)

LeadReach employees (sales, CS, leadership) who refer contacts from their network.

### Source Type 4: Event/Community Advocates (5-10% of referral volume)

Non-customers who encountered LeadReach at events or in community and refer others.

## 3. The Referral Program Structure

### 3.1 Incentive Design

| Referral Type | Referrer Reward | Referee Benefit | Qualification Criteria |
|---------------|----------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Customer → Prospect | $500 Amazon gift card OR 3 months free on their account | 20% off first year | Prospect becomes paying customer |
| Partner → Client | 15% revenue share for Year 1 | 15% off first year | Client becomes paying customer |
| Employee → Contact | $250 bonus (paid in paycheck) | 10% off first year | Contact becomes paying customer |
| Community → Prospect | $250 Amazon gift card | 10% off first year | Prospect becomes paying customer |

**Double-sided incentive principle**: Both parties benefit. This creates social cover for the referrer — they're not "selling," they're "sharing a deal."

### 3.2 Referral Qualification Rules

Not all referrals deserve the full incentive. Judge agent evaluates:

| Criterion | Qualified | Unqualified |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| ICP fit | Judge score >60 | Judge score <40 |
| Company size | 50+ employees | <10 employees |
| Existing customer? | No | Yes (already a customer — upsell, not referral) |
| Already in pipeline? | No | Yes (already being worked — co-attribution) |
| Territory | Covered territory | Uncovered territory (no AE to work it) |

**Partial qualification**: If Judge score is 40-60, the referral is accepted but the referrer incentive is halved. If the prospect upgrades later, the full incentive is paid retroactively.

### 3.3 Referral Tiers (Advocate Gamification)

| Tier | Qualification | Benefits |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Bronze | 1 referral closed-won | Standard incentive + "Referral Champion" badge |
| Silver | 3 referrals closed-won | 1.5x incentive + exclusive webinar access + featured in newsletter |
| Gold | 5 referrals closed-won | 2x incentive + annual LeadReach summit invitation + executive dinner |
| Platinum | 10+ referrals closed-won | 2.5x incentive + advisory board seat + product preview access + VIP event |

## 4. The Referral Workflow (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Advocate Identification (Scout, Continuous)

Scout agent monitors for referral-ready advocates:

**Signals that a customer is ready to refer:**
- NPS score 9-10 (promoters)
- Active in community / user group
- Recently achieved a measurable outcome with LeadReach
- Mentioned LeadReach in a social post
- Gave a positive review on G2/Capterra
- Asked "do you have a referral program?" in CSM call

**Signals that a partner is ready to refer:**
- Recently completed a successful LeadReach implementation
- Asked about LeadReach partnership incentives
- Mentioned a client who needs outbound tooling

### Step 2: Advocate Nurturing (Bard, Ongoing)

Bard agent runs a referral nurturing sequence for identified advocates:

| Timing | Channel | Content |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Month 1 | Email | Thank you for being a great customer; "know anyone who'd benefit?" |
| Month 2 | Email | Share their success story; ask if peers would find it valuable |
| Month 3 | Email | Referral program announcement; incentive details; easy share link |
| Ongoing | In-app | Referral CTA in dashboard (after positive milestone: "your reply rate hit 15%! Know someone who'd love this?") |
| Quarterly | Email | Referral program update; tier status; leader board (opt-in) |

### Step 3: Referral Capture (Flow, Real-Time)

When a referral is submitted (via referral page, email, or in-app):

1. **Capture**: Referrer name, referee name + email + company, relationship context
2. **Dedup**: Forge agent checks if referee is already in CRM/pipeline
3. **Score**: Judge agent evaluates ICP fit
4. **Route**: If qualified, route to AE in referee's territory with referral tag
5. **Acknowledge**: Bard sends thank-you to referrer within 1 hour
6. **Notify**: Referee receives warm introduction email from referrer (Bard drafts; referrer approves)

### Step 4: Referee Outreach (Bard + AE, Within 2 Hours)

Bard composes a referral-specific outreach email that references the referrer:

**Template — Referral Introduction**:
**Subject**: `[referrer name] suggested we connect`

> Hi [first name],
>
> [Referrer name] from [referrer company] suggested we connect. They've been using LeadReach for [timeframe] and thought you might find it useful — especially given [specific reason, e.g., "your team's expansion into EMEA outbound"].
>
> I promise not to waste your time. 15 minutes to show you what [referrer name] is seeing — worth it?
>
> [AE name]

### Step 5: Qualification and Discovery (AE, Days 1-7)

AE runs a standard discovery but with two advantages:
1. **Pre-qualified by association**: The referrer's endorsement means the prospect starts with trust
2. **Context from referrer**: The referrer can provide background on the prospect's situation

AE should **ask the referrer for context** before the first call:
- "What's [prospect]'s biggest challenge right now?"
- "Who else is involved in the decision?"
- "Any landmines I should know about?"

### Step 6: Demo and Proposal (AE, Days 7-21)

Standard demo → proposal flow, with referral-specific enhancements:
- Reference the referrer's results in the demo
- Include the referral discount in the proposal
- Offer a reference call with the referrer (if appropriate)

### Step 7: Close and Reward (Flow, Day of Close)

When the deal closes:
1. **Referrer reward**: Flow triggers the incentive payment within 5 business days
2. **Referee benefit**: Discount applied to first invoice
3. **Public recognition** (opt-in): Referrer featured in monthly referral spotlight
4. **Tier update**: Referrer's tier status updated; new benefits unlocked if tier upgraded

### Step 8: Outcome Tracking (Echo, Continuous)

Echo agent tracks:
- Referral source → pipeline → close → retention
- Advocate activity and tier progression
- Program ROI (incentive cost vs. closed-won revenue)
- Conversion rates by referral source type

## 9. Objection Handling

| Objection | Root Cause | Response |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| "I don't want to refer and have it reflect badly on me if it doesn't work out" | Social risk aversion | "Completely understand. That's why we offer a 90-day money-back guarantee for referred customers. If it's not a fit, they get a full refund — and you still get the referral credit." |
| "I don't know anyone who needs this" | Network not activated | "No worries! We can help. Here are three ways to think about it: (1) Who in your LinkedIn network has 'SDR' or 'outbound' in their title? (2) Any former colleagues now at companies with sales teams? (3) Anyone in your industry Slack community talking about outbound challenges?" |
| "The incentive isn't worth it" | Incentive misalignment | "What would make it worth it? We've had customers ask for account credits instead of gift cards, or donate the incentive to charity. We're flexible." |
| "I don't want to seem like I'm selling for you" | Authenticity concern | "We get that. The referral link is optional — you can just make a warm intro via email and we handle the rest. Your name opens the door; we do the selling." |
| "My company has a policy against referral incentives" | Compliance barrier | "No problem. We can redirect the incentive to a charity of your choice, or offer your company a credit on their account instead. Many customers prefer this approach." |
| "I referred someone before and never got my reward" | Past program failure | "I'm sorry about that — that shouldn't happen. Our program now has automated tracking and a 5-business-day payout SLA. I'll personally make sure you're credited." |

## 11. Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

| Pitfall | Cause | Prevention |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| Low referral volume | Customers don't know the program exists | In-app CTAs, CSM mentions, quarterly program emails, event mentions |
| Low referral quality | No screening; anyone can refer anyone | Judge agent scores before routing; partial incentives for marginal leads |
| Slow reward fulfillment | Manual payment processing | Flow agent automates incentive triggers; 5-day SLA |
| Referrers feel used | Too many asks; program feels transactional | Limit referral asks to 2-3 per year per customer; make it feel exclusive |
| No attribution | Referral gets credited to outbound instead | Mandatory referral source tag in CRM; Flow agent validates source |
| Gaming the system | Fake referrals; self-referrals | Judge agent validates referee is a new contact; not in customer's org |
| Program stagnation | Same advocates referring; no new advocates | Scout continuously identifies new advocates; tier progression drives engagement |
| No executive visibility | Referral program treated as side project | Monthly referral program review in leadership meeting; Echo dashboard |

## 13. Example Scenario

**Advocate**: Sarah Chen, VP Sales Ops at CloudMatrix (LeadReach customer for 14 months)

**Signals detected**:
- NPS score: 10 (submitted 2 weeks ago)
- Active in LeadReach community (3 posts this month)
- Mentioned LeadReach in LinkedIn post about outbound benchmarks
- Achieved milestone: 5,000 meetings booked via LeadReach

**Motion executed**:
1. **Day 0**: Scout flags Sarah as referral-ready. Bard sends referral ask email.
2. **Day 2**: Sarah responds — "Actually, my former colleague just became CRO at DataPulse and they're evaluating outbound tools."
3. **Day 2**: Flow captures referral — Referee: Marcus Rivera, CRO at DataPulse (250 employees, SaaS, Judge score: 78).
4. **Day 2**: Forge deduplicates — DataPulse is not in CRM. Qualified referral.
5. **Day 2**: Bard drafts introduction email for Sarah to send to Marcus. Sarah approves and sends.
6. **Day 2 (2 hours later)**: AE receives routed referral. Bard composes follow-up email referencing Sarah.
7. **Day 3**: Marcus replies — "Sarah speaks highly of LeadReach. Let's talk."
8. **Day 5**: Discovery call. Marcus reveals: DataPulse is using Outreach, frustrated with complexity, 15 SDRs, renewal in 3 months.
9. **Day 10**: Demo tailored to DataPulse's pain points. Marcus sees the simplicity advantage immediately.
10. **Day 18**: Proposal sent — 20 seats, Growth tier, $72K ACV, 20% first-year discount (referral benefit).
11. **Day 28**: Contract signed. Sarah receives $500 gift card + tier upgrade to Silver.
12. **Day 33**: Sarah's tier update email: "You're now a Silver Referral Champion! Here's what you've unlocked..."

**Outcome**: $72K ACV deal in 28 days, 40% shorter cycle than cold outbound. Sarah makes 2 more referrals in the next quarter.

## 14. Escalation Paths

| Situation | Escalation Level | Action |
|-----------|-----------------|--------|
| Referral incentive not paid within 5 days | Operations Manager | Immediate manual payout; process review |
| Advocate refers competitor employee | Legal + Atlas | Evaluate; may be strategic intelligence opportunity |
| Referee disputes referral attribution | RevOps | Source verification; dual-credit if warranted |
| Referral quality consistently low from one advocate | Atlas | Reduce outreach frequency; recalibrate expectations |
| Partner referral conflicts with direct sales motion | VP Sales + Partnerships | Co-sell vs. partner-sell determination |
| Program fraud suspected | Legal + Security | Investigation; pause payouts pending review |
| Advocate unhappy with referral experience | VP CS | Executive outreach; service recovery |
