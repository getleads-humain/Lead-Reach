# Channel Partner Enablement Playbook

## Overview

This playbook provides a comprehensive framework for building, enabling, and scaling a channel partner ecosystem for LeadReach. Channel partnerships multiply your go-to-market reach, provide localized expertise, and create sustainable competitive advantages. However, poorly enabled partners become a liability — they misrepresent your product, create support burden, and damage your brand.

LeadReach's AI agent pipeline powers channel partner success through automated deal registration, partner-specific intelligence feeds, and co-selling coordination. This playbook establishes the operating model for a world-class partner program.

### 1.2 Partner Profile Scoring

Evaluate potential partners using a weighted scoring model:

| Dimension | Weight | Scoring Criteria (1-5) | LeadReach Data Source |
|-----------|--------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Market Alignment | 25% | 1=No overlap, 5=Perfect vertical/segment alignment | Atlas Agent market analysis |
| Technical Capability | 20% | 1=No relevant skills, 5=Deep AI/data expertise | Manual assessment + certifications |
| Sales Capacity | 20% | 1=Sole proprietor, 5=Large dedicated sales team | Forge Agent firmographic data |
| Customer Base | 15% | 1=No relevant customers, 5=Large overlapping base | Scout Agent account mapping |
| Financial Stability | 10% | 1=Startup/unproven, 5=Established, profitable | Forge Agent financial data |
| Strategic Value | 10% | 1=Commodity, 5=Unique market access/capability | Manual strategic assessment |

**Partner Score Thresholds**:
- 4.0+: Fast-track to Gold or Platinum tier
- 3.0-3.9: Silver tier with growth plan
- 2.0-2.9: Develop further before onboarding
- Below 2.0: Decline or revisit in 6 months

### 1.3 Partner Types and Ideal Profiles

#### Type 1: Value-Added Resellers (VARs)

- **Ideal Profile**: Systems integrators with AI/automation practices, established relationships with enterprise buyers
- **Value**: Pre-sale consulting, implementation services, ongoing managed services
- **Target Verticals**: Financial services, healthcare, manufacturing
- **LeadReach Fit**: High — complex product benefits from consultative selling

#### Type 2: Technology Alliance Partners

- **Ideal Profile**: CRM vendors (Salesforce, HubSpot), data platforms (Snowflake, Databricks), marketing automation (Marketo, Eloqua)
- **Value**: Integration co-selling, joint solutions, expanded use cases
- **Target Verticals**: Cross-industry
- **LeadReach Fit**: Very High — integration value is core to product proposition

#### Type 3: Managed Service Providers (MSPs)

- **Ideal Profile**: MSPs offering marketing operations or sales operations as a service
- **Value**: Ongoing management, embedded LeadReach in their service delivery
- **Target Verticals**: Mid-market, SMB
- **LeadReach Fit**: Moderate — good for volume, lower average deal size

#### Type 4: Consulting Partners

- **Ideal Profile**: Strategy consultants (McKinsey, Deloitte Digital), sales transformation consultants
- **Value**: Strategic advisory influence, large transformation deals
- **Target Verticals**: Enterprise
- **LeadReach Fit**: High — influence over large transformation budgets

#### Type 5: Referral Partners

- **Ideal Profile**: Complementary SaaS companies, industry analysts, event organizers
- **Value**: Qualified lead generation, market credibility
- **Target Verticals**: Cross-industry
- **LeadReach Fit**: Moderate — low effort, variable quality

## 2. Partner Onboarding Framework

### 2.1 Onboarding Program Structure

The partner onboarding program is a 90-day structured journey:

#### Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-30)

**Week 1: Orientation**
- Partner portal access and navigation training
- LeadReach platform overview and demo certification prep
- Partner agreement and program expectations review
- Key contacts and escalation paths introduction
- LeadReach Agent Pipeline deep-dive

**Week 2-3: Product Mastery**
- 8-Agent Pipeline technical training (Atlas, Scout, Forge, Sage, Judge, Bard, Flow, Echo)
- Hands-on lab: Build a prospecting campaign end-to-end
- Competitive positioning and differentiation training
- Pricing and packaging mastery
- ROI calculator training

**Week 4: Sales Enablement**
- LeadReach sales methodology training
- Discovery call frameworks and scripts
- Demo certification preparation
- Objection handling workshop
- First co-selling opportunity identified

#### Phase 2: Activation (Days 31-60)

**Week 5-6: Co-Selling Readiness**
- Shadow 3 LeadReach AE customer calls
- Conduct first partner-led demo (with LeadReach AE present)
- Deal registration process training and practice
- Pipeline building strategy session
- First deal registration submitted

**Week 7-8: Marketing Activation**
- Co-branded campaign development
- Joint webinar planning
- Partner-specific landing page creation
- Content syndication strategy
- Social selling playbook activation

#### Phase 3: Acceleration (Days 61-90)

**Week 9-10: Independent Execution**
- Partner independently conducts customer discovery calls
- Partner-led demos without LeadReach AE (with recording for QA)
- First closed deal support
- Customer onboarding collaboration
- Technical pre-sale support training

**Week 11-12: Optimization**
- 90-day performance review
- Certification exam scheduling
- Tier progression assessment
- QBR cadence establishment
- Growth plan development

## 3. Co-Selling Methodology

### 3.1 The Co-Sell Engagement Model

LeadReach operates a structured co-selling model with clear roles and handoffs:

#### Model A: Partner-Led, LeadReach-Supported

```
Applicable When:
- Partner has deep customer relationship
- Partner has completed demo certification
- Deal is in partner's core competency area

Roles:
┌──────────────────┬──────────────────┐
│ Partner           │ LeadReach         │
├──────────────────┼──────────────────┤
│ Lead discovery    │ Pre-sale support  │
│ Relationship mgmt │ Technical demo    │
│ Commercial terms  │ Pricing approval  │
│ Contract execution│ Implementation    │
│ Ongoing account   │ Success planning  │
└──────────────────┴──────────────────┘

Revenue Share: Partner 70% / LeadReach 30%
(For partner-sourced, partner-led deals)
```

#### Model B: LeadReach-Led, Partner-Supported

```
Applicable When:
- LeadReach has existing customer relationship
- Deal requires partner implementation services
- Customer needs industry-specific customization

Roles:
┌──────────────────┬──────────────────┐
│ LeadReach         │ Partner           │
├──────────────────┼──────────────────┤
│ Lead discovery    │ Industry context  │
│ Product demo      │ Custom solution   │
│ Commercial terms  │ SOW development   │
│ Contract execution│ Implementation    │
│ Ongoing account   │ Managed services  │
└──────────────────┴──────────────────┘

Revenue Share: LeadReach 70% / Partner 30%
(For LeadReach-sourced, partner-assisted deals)
```

#### Model C: Joint Sell

```
Applicable When:
- Strategic/enterprise opportunity
- Neither party has existing relationship
- Large deal requiring combined capabilities

Roles:
┌──────────────────┬──────────────────┐
│ LeadReach         │ Partner           │
├──────────────────┼──────────────────┤
│ Platform expertise│ Industry expertise│
│ Product demo      │ Business case     │
│ Technical validation│ Implementation  │
│ Reference calls   │ Service delivery  │
│ Contract terms    │ SOW + services    │
└──────────────────┴──────────────────┘

Revenue Share: 50/50 (Negotiable for strategic deals)
```

### 3.2 Co-Sell Engagement Rules

1. **Deal Registration is Mandatory**: Any partner engaging a prospect must register the deal within 48 hours of first meaningful contact
2. **First-to-Register Wins**: In case of overlap, the partner who registers first gets the deal, subject to validation
3. **360-Degree Protection**: Registered deals are protected for 360 days from registration date
4. **Activity Requirements**: Partners must log activity on registered deals at least every 30 days or the registration expires
5. **No End-Run Rule**: Direct sales teams may not circumvent registered partner deals
6. **Pricing Consistency**: Partners must sell at or above MSRP; discounting requires LeadReach approval
7. **Customer Experience First**: If co-selling conflicts arise, the customer experience takes priority

## 4. Partner Deal Registration

### 4.1 Deal Registration Process

```
Deal Registration Flow:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Step 1: Partner submits registration via Partner Portal
  ├── Required fields: Company name, contact, opportunity size,
  │   timeline, competing solutions, partner role
  └── Auto-populated: Partner tier, certification status, discount level

Step 2: LeadReach Scout Agent validates
  ├── Check for existing LeadReach engagement with the account
  ├── Verify firmographic data and fit with ICP
  ├── Assess competitive landscape
  └── Flag any conflicts or concerns

Step 3: LeadReach Deal Desk reviews
  ├── Tier 1 (Platinum): 48-hour SLA
  ├── Tier 2 (Gold): 5-day SLA
  └── Tier 3 (Silver): 7-day SLA

Step 4: Registration decision
  ├── Approved: Partner receives confirmation + pricing guidance
  ├── Conflict: Partner team and direct team coordinate (48-hour resolution)
  ├── Denied: Reason provided + alternative engagement model suggested
  └── Escalated: Complex situations escalated to VP Partnerships

Step 5: Ongoing management
  ├── Partner logs activity updates every 30 days
  ├── LeadReach Flow Agent tracks deal progression
  ├── Automated reminders for stale deals
  └── Co-selling support coordinated as needed
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

### 4.2 Deal Registration Requirements

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| End Customer Name | Yes | Legal entity name |
| End Customer Contact | Yes | Name, title, email, phone |
| Opportunity Amount | Yes | Estimated annual contract value |
| Close Date | Yes | Expected close date |
| Partner Role | Yes | Primary / Supporting / Referral |
| Competing Solutions | Yes | Known competitive vendors being evaluated |
| Lead Source | Yes | How the partner discovered the opportunity |
| Custom Requirements | No | Any special technical or commercial requirements |
| Services Opportunity | No | Estimated implementation + managed services revenue |

### 4.3 Deal Registration Protection Rules

1. **Registration Window**: Valid for 360 days from approval date
2. **Activity Requirement**: Must log at least one activity update per 30 days
3. **Exclusivity**: Protects against direct sales team engagement on the same opportunity
4. **Overlap Resolution**: If multiple partners register the same deal, first-to-register wins with validation
5. **Termination**: Registration revoked if no activity for 60 days, or if customer confirms no engagement

## 5. MDF Management

### 5.1 MDF Allocation Framework

Market Development Funds (MDF) are allocated quarterly based on partner tier and performance:

| Partner Tier | Base Allocation | Performance Bonus | Total Potential | Approval Process |
|-------------|----------------|-------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Platinum | $50K/quarter | Up to $25K | $75K/quarter | Auto-approved |
| Gold | $20K/quarter | Up to $10K | $30K/quarter | Partner Dev Manager |
| Silver | $5K/quarter | Up to $2.5K | $7.5K/quarter | Partner Ops review |

### 5.2 MDF-Eligible Activities

| Activity Category | Examples | MDF Coverage | Max % of Total MDF |
|-------------------|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| Events | Trade shows, conferences, partner-hosted events | 50-70% | 40% |
| Digital Marketing | Webinars, content syndication, paid search | 50-70% | 30% |
| Content Development | Case studies, whitepapers, solution briefs | 60-80% | 20% |
| Training & Certification | Sales training, technical certification, workshops | 100% | 15% |
| Demand Generation | Email campaigns, direct mail, ABM campaigns | 50-60% | 25% |

### 5.3 MDF Request and Approval Process

```
MDF Request Flow:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. Partner submits MDF request via Partner Portal (30 days before activity)
   ├── Activity description and objectives
   ├── Budget breakdown and requested MDF amount
   ├── Expected pipeline impact ($3:1 pipeline-to-MDF minimum)
   └── Timeline and measurement plan

2. LeadReach Atlas Agent evaluates
   ├── Pipeline potential analysis based on target market
   ├── ROI projection based on historical MDF performance
   ├── Brand alignment assessment
   └── Budget availability check

3. Approval decision
   ├── Auto-approved (Platinum, within guidelines)
   ├── Manager review (Gold, within guidelines)
   ├── Committee review (exceeds guidelines or new activity type)
   └── Denied with feedback

4. Execution and measurement
   ├── Partner executes activity
   ├── LeadReach Scout Agent tracks pipeline impact
   ├── Partner submits proof of execution and results
   └── MDF reconciliation within 45 days of activity completion
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|

| Conversion rate | >15% | MDF-sourced pipeline that converts to revenue |

## 8. Conflict Resolution

### 8.1 Common Conflict Scenarios

#### Scenario 1: Deal Registration Overlap

When two partners (or a partner and direct sales) register the same account:

```
Resolution Process:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. Scout Agent identifies overlap during registration validation
2. Deal Desk notifies both parties within 24 hours
3. Both parties provide evidence of engagement:
   ├── Date of first meaningful contact
   ├── Depth of relationship with customer
   ├── Investment in opportunity development
   └── Customer preference (if available)
4. Deal Desk makes determination:
   ├── Clear first-to-register: First registrant wins
   ├── Simultaneous engagement: Customer chooses
   ├── Different departments/contacts: Split opportunity
   └── Partner + direct overlap: Customer chooses; other party supports
5. Resolution communicated within 48 hours
6. Losing party receives compensation if they invested significantly
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

#### Scenario 2: Pricing Dispute

When a partner sells below approved pricing or undercuts another channel:

1. **Investigation**: Determine if pricing deviation was authorized or unauthorized
2. **Customer Impact**: Ensure customer is not disadvantaged regardless of outcome
3. **Partner Action**: Unauthorized discounting triggers tier review and potential suspension
4. **Remediation**: Partner must agree to pricing compliance going forward
5. **Repeat Offense**: Second violation results in 90-day deal registration suspension

#### Scenario 3: Customer Ownership Dispute

When a partner claims an account that LeadReach direct sales is already pursuing:

1. **CRM Validation**: Check CRM for existing opportunity records and activity history
2. **Engagement Evidence**: Both parties present evidence of customer engagement
3. **Customer Preference**: Ask the customer about their preferred engagement model
4. **Resolution**: Follow customer preference; offer supporting role to other party
5. **Prevention**: Improve account mapping and communication between direct and partner teams

### 8.2 Conflict Prevention Best Practices

- **Quarterly Account Mapping**: Align on target accounts between direct and partner teams
- **Transparent Deal Registration**: Real-time visibility into registered deals for all parties
- **Clear Rules of Engagement**: Documented and signed by all parties
- **Regular Communication**: Weekly pipeline syncs between partner and direct teams
- **Customer-Centric Resolution**: Always prioritize customer experience over internal politics

```

    ↓

```

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓
