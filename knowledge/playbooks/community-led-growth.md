# Community-Led Growth Playbook

## Overview

This playbook provides a comprehensive framework for building and leveraging a community ecosystem that drives organic growth for LeadReach. Community-Led Growth (CLG) is a go-to-market strategy where the community itself becomes a primary driver of acquisition, activation, retention, and expansion. Unlike product-led or sales-led models, CLG harnesses the collective energy, knowledge, and advocacy of your users to create sustainable competitive advantages that are nearly impossible for competitors to replicate.

LeadReach's AI agent pipeline powers CLG through community signal detection (Scout Agent), community-to-pipeline conversion (Flow Agent), and advocacy program automation (Echo Agent). This playbook translates those capabilities into a systematic community growth engine.

## 1. Community-Led Growth Framework

### 1.1 The CLG Flywheel

Community-Led Growth operates as a flywheel where each stage feeds the next:

```
CLG Flywheel:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
                    ┌──────────────┐
                    │   EXPAND     │
                    │  Community   │
                    │  members     │
                    │  advocate    │
                    │  & refer     │
                    └──────┬───────┘
                           │
    ┌──────────────┐       │       ┌──────────────┐
    │   ENGAGE     │       │       │   CONVERT    │
    │  Members     ├───────┴───────┤  Community   │
    │  participate │               │  members     │
    │  & contribute│               │  become      │
    └──────┬───────┘               │  customers   │
           │                       └──────┬───────┘
           │                              │
    ┌──────┴───────┐                      │
    │   ATTRACT    │◄─────────────────────┘
    │  Content &   │
    │  activity    │
    │  draws new   │
    │  members     │
    └──────────────┘
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

**Stage 1: Attract** — Community content, events, and member activity draw new members into the ecosystem
**Stage 2: Engage** — Members participate in discussions, contribute content, attend events, and build relationships
**Stage 3: Convert** — Engaged community members naturally progress to evaluating and purchasing LeadReach
**Stage 4: Expand** — Satisfied customers become advocates, referring new members and accelerating the flywheel

### 1.2 CLG vs. PLG vs. SLG

Understanding when and how CLG complements other growth models:

| Dimension | Community-Led | Product-Led | Sales-Led |
|-----------|-------------|------------|-----------|
| Primary driver | Peer influence + shared knowledge | Product experience | Sales relationship |
| Acquisition cost | Very low (organic) | Low (self-serve) | High (sales team) |
| Trust mechanism | Social proof from peers | Product trial experience | Sales expertise |
| Scalability | High (network effects) | High (self-serve) | Limited (headcount) |
| Time to impact | 6-12 months (build phase) | 3-6 months | Immediate |
| Best for | Technical/innovative products | Simple, intuitive products | Complex, high-ACV products |
| LeadReach fit | Complementary to PLG + SLG | Core motion for SMB | Core motion for enterprise |

**LeadReach Strategy**: CLG complements our PLG and SLG motions. Community drives top-of-funnel awareness and mid-funnel trust, while PLG handles SMB conversion and SLG closes enterprise deals.

### 1.3 The CLG Maturity Model

#### Stage 1: Community as Support (Passive)

- Community exists primarily as a support forum
- No deliberate growth strategy
- Community managers are support staff
- No measurement framework
- Community contributes minimally to pipeline

**Assessment**: Most companies start here. The goal is to move to Stage 2 within 6 months.

#### Stage 2: Community as Content (Active)

- Community generates valuable content (tutorials, templates, integrations)
- Deliberate content strategy and promotion
- Dedicated community team (1-2 people)
- Basic measurement (members, posts, engagement rate)
- Community drives some organic traffic and leads

**Assessment**: Good progress. Target Stage 3 within 12 months.

#### Stage 3: Community as Product (Integrated)

- Community feedback shapes product roadmap
- Community-built integrations and extensions expand platform value
- Community team is a cross-functional organization
- Comprehensive measurement (full-funnel attribution)
- Community drives significant pipeline contribution (>15%)

**Assessment**: Strong position. Push toward Stage 4.

#### Stage 4: Community as Competitive Advantage (Strategic)

- Community is a primary reason customers choose LeadReach over competitors
- Network effects create natural switching costs
- Community-generated content outscales marketing-produced content
- Community members actively sell on LeadReach's behalf
- Community drives >25% of pipeline contribution

**Assessment**: Ultimate goal. This creates durable, compounding competitive advantage.

### 2.3 Community Growth Tactics

#### Organic Growth Tactics

1. **SEO-optimized community content**: Community Q&A and discussions rank for long-tail search queries, driving organic traffic
2. **Social sharing incentives**: Make community content easy to share with built-in social sharing
3. **Email signature community link**: All LeadReach employees include community link in email signatures
4. **Product-to-community prompts**: In-app prompts invite users to community when they complete key milestones
5. **Referral mechanics**: Community members earn recognition for inviting peers
6. **Content syndication**: Top community content is repurposed for blog, social media, and newsletters

#### Paid Growth Tactics (Limited, Strategic)

1. **Community event sponsorship**: Sponsor industry events where your community members gather
2. **Targeted social ads**: Promote top community content to lookalike audiences
3. **Influencer partnerships**: Partner with industry influencers to join and promote the community
4. **Content amplification**: Boost top-performing community content with paid distribution

#### Partnership Growth Tactics

1. **Partner community cross-promotion**: Cross-promote with partner communities (Salesforce Trailblazers, HubSpot Community)
2. **Co-hosted events**: Host joint community events with complementary product communities
3. **Integration marketplace**: Community-built integrations attract users of other platforms
4. **Guest expert program**: Invite industry experts to host sessions in the community

### 7.3 Community Engagement Scoring

LeadReach's Sage Agent calculates a community engagement score for each member:

```
Community Engagement Score = (Activity Frequency × 30) +
                            (Activity Depth × 25) +
                            (Contribution Quality × 25) +
                            (Network Influence × 20)

Where:
- Activity Frequency: Login frequency, post frequency, event attendance
- Activity Depth: Length and depth of contributions, not just surface engagement
- Contribution Quality: Likes/reactions received, answers accepted, templates used
- Network Influence: Connections, mentions, follower count within community
```
