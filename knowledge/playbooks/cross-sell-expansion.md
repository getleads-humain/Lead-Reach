# Cross-Sell Expansion — Playbook

This playbook is the canonical reference for cross-selling additional LeadReach products, modules, and services to existing customers. It covers cross-sell opportunity identification, use-case mapping, positioning strategy, stakeholder engagement, and cross-sell measurement. Used by Atlas (Orchestrator), Echo (Signal Detection), Sage (Use-Case Research), Bard (Cross-Sell Outreach), and Flow (Pipeline Tracking).

## 1. Why Cross-Sell Expansion Matters

Cross-sell is the highest-margin revenue motion in SaaS. Unlike upsell (which sells more of the same product), cross-sell introduces a new product or module to a customer who already trusts LeadReach. The economics are compelling:

- **Near-zero CAC**: The relationship, trust, and procurement relationship already exist.
- **Higher win rate**: Cross-sell to existing customers closes at 60-70% vs. 20-30% for new-logo deals.
- **Deeper account penetration**: Each additional product increases switching costs and reduces churn.
- **Higher NRR**: Companies that cross-sell effectively achieve 130-150% NRR vs. 105-115% for companies that rely on upsell alone.
- **Customer satisfaction**: Customers who adopt multiple products report higher satisfaction because they solve more problems with a single vendor.

The challenge: cross-sell requires understanding the customer's adjacent pain points, which are often in different departments and held by different stakeholders. It's not enough to show a feature list — you need to connect a new product to a specific, validated need.

## 4. The Cross-Sell Workflow (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Signal Detection and Opportunity Identification (Echo + Scout, Continuous)

Echo and Scout monitor for cross-sell signals across all customer accounts. When a signal is detected:
- Echo logs the signal with confidence score
- Scout enriches with organizational context
- Atlas evaluates the cross-sell opportunity against customer health and ICP fit

### Step 2: Use-Case Validation (CSM, 1-2 Days)

CSM validates the cross-sell opportunity in the next regular touchpoint:
- "I noticed [signal]. Is [pain point] something your team is experiencing?"
- "Other teams at [company size] companies often ask about [product]. Is that relevant for you?"
- "Have you explored [use case]? We have a module specifically for that."

If the customer confirms the need, proceed to Step 3. If they don't, log the signal for future nurturing.

### Step 3: Stakeholder Mapping (Atlas, 1-3 Days)

Cross-sell often requires engaging new stakeholders who weren't involved in the original purchase:

| Cross-Sell Product | New Stakeholder | Role | Engagement Path |
|-------------------|----------------|------|-----------------|
| Advanced Analytics | VP Sales, CRO | Needs pipeline visibility | Champion intro; ROI of analytics |
| Data Enrichment | SDR Manager | Needs research efficiency | CSM demo; time-saved quantification |
| Multi-Channel | VP Sales | Needs higher contact rates | Champion intro; reply rate improvement |
| AI Personalization | SDR Manager | Needs personalization at scale | CSM demo; quality improvement data |
| Compliance & Governance | CISO, Legal | Needs audit and compliance | Security review; compliance module demo |
| Professional Services | SDR Manager, RevOps | Needs implementation help | CSM recommendation; package proposal |
| Marketing ABM | VP Marketing | Needs ABM coordination | Champion intro; joint Sales+Marketing demo |

Atlas agent creates a **Cross-Sell Stakeholder Map** with:
- Current champion (can make introductions)
- New stakeholder(s) (need to be engaged)
- Recommended engagement path
- Key messages for each stakeholder

### Step 4: Cross-Sell Demo (AE + CSM, Days 3-10)

A focused demo showing the cross-sell product solving the validated pain:

**Demo rules for cross-sell**:
1. **Don't re-demo the core product** — they already know it
2. **Show the new product in their existing LeadReach environment** — continuity builds trust
3. **Use their data** — their sequences, their contacts, their results
4. **Quantify the improvement** — "With Data Enrichment, your SDRs would save 2.3 hours/day on research"
5. **Show integration** — how the new product works seamlessly with what they already have

### Step 5: Proposal and Pricing (Bard + AE, Days 10-14)

Cross-sell pricing should be **incremental**, not a full re-proposal:

| Pricing Approach | Example | When to Use |
|-----------------|---------|-------------|
| Per-seat add-on | "+$20/seat/month for Data Enrichment" | Module add-ons to existing tier |
| Bundle discount | "Analytics + Data Enrichment bundle: 15% off individual pricing" | Multiple add-ons |
| Tier upgrade | "Upgrade to Enterprise: includes all modules + dedicated CSM" | Multiple add-ons + enterprise features |
| One-time service | "Onboarding package: $8,000 for 4-week structured program" | Professional services |

### Step 6: Close and Activation (AE + CSM, Days 14-21)

- Contract amendment (add modules to existing contract)
- Feature activation (turn on the new module in their account)
- Onboarding (targeted training on the new product)
- Success criteria (define what "value" looks like for the new product)

### Step 7: Value Validation (Echo, Days 30-60)

Echo monitors adoption and value of the new product:
- Feature usage: are they using the new module?
- Outcome metrics: is the new product delivering the promised improvement?
- Adoption rate: target >70% of eligible users active within 30 days

## 9. Objection Handling

| Objection | Root Cause | Response |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| "We already have a tool for that" | Competing product in adjacent category | "Interesting — which tool? We often find that using one platform for sequencing + [enrichment/analytics] reduces context-switching and improves results. Can I show you the comparison?" |
| "We don't have budget for additional modules" | Budget constraint | "The [module] pays for itself. Based on your current team size, [Data Enrichment saves X hours/week = $Y in SDR time]. The ROI is typically [3-5x] in the first quarter." |
| "I need to evaluate other options first" | Risk aversion or process requirement | "Of course. As an existing customer, you have an advantage: the [module] integrates directly with your current LeadReach setup. No migration, no new vendor, no new procurement process. Want a 2-week trial?" |
| "My team is still learning the core product" | Adoption saturation | "Smart approach — I don't want to overwhelm your team. When do you think they'll be fully ramped? I'll check back then. In the meantime, here's a 2-minute overview of the [module] so you can see what's possible." |
| "We built a custom solution for that" | Internal tooling | "Custom solutions are great until they break or the person who built them leaves. Our [module] is maintained, updated, and supported. And it works natively with LeadReach — no integration headaches. Worth a comparison?" |
| "I'm not sure the ROI justifies it" | Value not quantified | "Let me build a specific ROI model for your team. Based on your current [metric], the [module] should deliver [X] improvement, which translates to [$Y] in value. If the numbers don't work, I won't push it." |

## 11. Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

| Pitfall | Cause | Prevention |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| Selling features, not outcomes | AE lists module features without connecting to validated pain | Bard composes cross-sell outreach only after CSM validates the pain point |
| Cross-selling to unhealthy accounts | Customer is already struggling with core product | Judge validates account health before cross-sell motion; minimum health score 50 |
| Wrong stakeholder engaged | AE pitches to the champion instead of the new product's buyer | Atlas maps stakeholders per cross-sell product; engage the right persona |
| No trial or proof of value | Customer asked to buy without experiencing the new product | Offer 14-day free trial of any module for existing customers |
| Feature overload | Too many modules pitched at once | One module per conversation. Sequence cross-sell over time. |
| No post-cross-sell adoption tracking | Module sold but never used; no value realized | Echo monitors feature adoption; CSM intervenes if adoption <50% at Day 14 |
| Cross-sell creates support burden | New modules create new issues | Dedicated onboarding for each module; knowledge base updates |

## 13. Example Scenario

**Account**: Solaris Health, 500-person HealthTech company

**Current contract**: 30 seats, Growth tier, $46,440/year

**Cross-sell signals detected**:
1. SDRs manually researching contacts in LinkedIn before sequencing (Echo, Day 0) → Data Enrichment
2. VP Sales asked "which sequences actually drive closed-won deals?" in QBR (CSM note) → Advanced Analytics
3. Compliance team requested audit trail documentation (support ticket) → Compliance & Governance

**Motion executed**:
1. **Day 1**: CSM raises cross-sell opportunity in account review. Atlas prioritizes: Data Enrichment (strongest signal) first.
2. **Day 3**: CSM validates with SDR Manager: "Are your SDRs spending a lot of time on research?" Confirmed: 3+ hours/day. Pain validated.
3. **Day 5**: AE + CSM demo Data Enrichment module using Solaris Health's actual prospects. SDR Manager sees immediate value: "That would save each SDR 2 hours/day."
4. **Day 7**: Proposal for Data Enrichment add-on: +$20/seat/month × 30 seats = +$7,200/year. Incremental ACV.
5. **Day 10**: Data Enrichment contract amendment signed. Module activated. Training scheduled.
6. **Day 14**: CSM follows up on Analytics opportunity with VP Sales. Demo scheduled.
7. **Day 18**: Advanced Analytics demo. VP Sales: "This is exactly what I need for board reporting." Proposal: +$15/seat/month × 30 seats = +$5,400/year.
8. **Day 21**: Analytics amendment signed. Two modules added in 3 weeks.
9. **Day 30**: Compliance module conversation initiated. CISO engaged. Demo scheduled for next week.

**Outcome**: $12,600 incremental ACV from two modules in 21 days. Third module in pipeline. Total cross-sell ACV potential: $21,000/year (+45% of original ACV).
