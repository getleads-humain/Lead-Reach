# Outbound Cold Email — Playbook

This playbook is the canonical reference for the Bard agent's email-composition skills. It covers structure, sequencing, personalization, compliance, and measurement for B2B cold email across regions.

## 1. The Anatomy of a Cold Email

A high-converting cold email has six elements, in this order:

1. **Subject line** — 3–7 words; lowercase; no caps; no exclamation marks; promise a specific outcome, not a product.
2. **Personalization hook** — 1–2 lines referencing a recent, verifiable event in the prospect's world (funding, hiring, product launch, leadership change, earnings call).
3. **Problem framing** — 2–3 lines articulating the problem the prospect likely faces, anchored in the personalization hook.
4. **Solution positioning** — 1–2 lines positioning LeadReach as the answer, with one quantified proof point.
5. **Soft CTA** — 1 line asking for a 15-minute call (not a demo, not a "quick chat").
6. **Signature** — Name, title, company, phone, physical address (CAN-SPAM), LinkedIn profile link.

Total target length: 80–130 words. Shorter is better.

### Subject Line Patterns That Convert

| Pattern | Example | Open Rate |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Question | `quick question about [company]'s outbound` | 45–60% |
| Reference | `[peer company] / [vendor]` | 40–55% |
| Curiosity | `worth a 15-min call?` | 35–50% |
| Trigger-based | `saw your Series B — congrats` | 50–70% |
| Personal | `[first name] — quick one` | 35–45% |
| Direct | `pipeline for [company]` | 30–40% |

Avoid: ALL CAPS, exclamation marks, the word "free", product names in subject lines, emoji in B2B.

### Personalization Hook Examples

**Funding trigger:**
> "Saw the news about Acme's $40M Series B — congrats. With GTM scaling, most SDR teams at that stage struggle with research time..."

**Hiring trigger:**
> "Noticed you're hiring 3 SDRs in Austin — looks like outbound is a focus. Quick thought: most SDRs spend 60% of their day on research..."

**Product launch trigger:**
> "Saw the launch of [Product] — interesting positioning against [Competitor]. One question that came to mind: how are you sourcing the ICP accounts for the launch?"

**Leadership change trigger:**
> "Congrats on the new CRO role at Acme. Most new CROs in their first 90 days focus on pipeline coverage — quick thought..."

### Soft CTA Examples

- "Worth a 15-minute call next Tuesday or Wednesday?"
- "Open to a 15-minute conversation next week?"
- "If this resonates, would 15 minutes next Tuesday work?"
- "Mind if I send over a short case study first?"

### What NOT to Do

- **Don't pitch in the first email.** The goal of email #1 is a reply, not a sale.
- **Don't use "I hope this finds you well"** — clichéd, immediately ignored.
- **Don't ask for a 30-minute demo** — too high friction. Ask for 15 minutes.
- **Don't send attachments in cold email #1** — triggers spam filters.
- **Don't use tracking pixels in GDPR-regulated markets** without consent.
- **Don't follow up the same day** with multiple touches — feels desperate.

## 2. The 21-Day Sequence (SaaS B2B — US)

This is the canonical Bard sequence for SaaS B2B in the US. Adapt for region and industry.

| Day | Channel | Content |
|-----|---------|---------|
| 0 | LinkedIn | Connection request, 2-line personalized note (no pitch) |
| 2 | Email | Email #1 — problem-led, soft CTA |
| 5 | LinkedIn | Like + thoughtful comment on recent post |
| 7 | Email | Email #2 — case study from same industry |
| 10 | Cold Call | Reference the emails; ask for 15 minutes |
| 14 | Email | Email #3 — new case study + soft CTA |
| 17 | LinkedIn DM | Soft re-engagement |
| 21 | Email | Breakup email ("should I close your file?") |

Expected performance (with tight ICP, fresh data, well-written copy):
- **Open rate**: 50–65%
- **Reply rate**: 4–8%
- **Meeting-booked rate**: 2.5–4%
- **Positive reply rate**: 60–75% of replies

### Sequence Adaptations by Industry

| Industry | Cycle Length | Touch Count | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------------|-------|
| SaaS B2B (US) | 21 days | 8 | Canonical sequence above |
| SaaS B2B (EU) | 28 days | 7 | Email + LinkedIn only; no cold calls to mobile |
| FinTech (US) | 28 days | 9 | Add direct mail at day 14 |
| FinTech (EU) | 35 days | 8 | Email + LinkedIn + direct mail; no cold calls to Germany |
| HealthTech (US) | 28 days | 9 | Add direct mail at day 14; lead with peer reference |
| Manufacturing (US Midwest) | 35 days | 10 | Add direct mail + trade show follow-up |
| Ecommerce-DTC (US) | 14 days | 7 | Faster sequence; harder CTAs |

## 3. Compliance Checklist (Per Region)

### US (CAN-SPAM + state privacy laws)

- [ ] Physical postal address in every email
- [ ] Clear, working unsubscribe link in every email
- [ ] Honor unsubscribes within 10 business days
- [ ] No deceptive subject lines
- [ ] Accurate From name and email
- [ ] CCPA: opt-out link for "sale" or "sharing" of personal data
- [ ] DNC scrub for cold calls (every 31 days)

### EU (GDPR + ePrivacy)

- [ ] Documented Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA) for B2B outreach
- [ ] Consent for B2B cold email in Germany, Hungary (where required)
- [ ] Clear opt-out in every email
- [ ] No tracking pixels without consent
- [ ] SCCs or EU-US DPF for US data transfers
- [ ] Appoint EU representative (Article 27) if processing at scale

### UK (UK GDPR + PECR)

- [ ] Documented LIA for B2B outreach
- [ ] Clear opt-out in every email
- [ ] Sender ID not concealed
- [ ] IDTA or UK Addendum for US data transfers
- [ ] TPS scrub for cold calls

### APAC (PDPA Singapore + variants)

- [ ] Deemed consent source documented for each contact
- [ ] Clear opt-out in every email
- [ ] DNC scrub (Singapore) for cold calls
- [ ] PDPA-compliant DPA template ready

## 4. Measurement Framework

Track the following metrics for every sequence:

**Top-of-funnel:**
- **Sent volume** — emails sent per sequence per week
- **Open rate** — unique opens / delivered
- **Reply rate** — replies / delivered
- **Bounce rate** — bounces / sent (target < 2%)
- **Spam complaint rate** — complaints / delivered (target < 0.1%)

**Mid-funnel:**
- **Positive reply rate** — positive replies / total replies (target > 60%)
- **Meeting-booked rate** — meetings / delivered
- **Meeting-attended rate** — attended / booked (target > 70%)

**Bottom-funnel:**
- **SQL conversion rate** — SQLs / meetings attended
- **Win rate** — closed-won / SQLs
- **Cycle time** — days from first touch to closed-won

### Diagnostic Patterns

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Low open rate (<30%) | Subject line / sender reputation / spam filter | Test subject lines; check domain reputation; warm up new sending domains |
| High open + low reply | Email content not resonating | Rewrite copy; tighter ICP; better personalization |
| High reply + low positive reply | ICP wrong / messaging wrong | Re-examine ICP; rewrite copy |
| High positive reply + low meeting booked | CTA too soft / friction too high | Tighter CTA; reduce scheduling friction (Calendly link) |
| High meeting booked + low attended | Confirmation process weak | Add reminder emails 24h and 1h before |
| High attended + low SQL | Discovery process weak | Train AEs on discovery; tighter qualification criteria |

## 5. Bard's Email Composition Workflow

When the Bard agent is invoked to compose an email:

1. **Read the prospect context** — name, title, company, industry, region, recent triggers, peer references.
2. **Select a personalization hook** — choose the highest-signal trigger (funding > hiring > product > leadership > generic).
3. **Select a peer reference** — from the industry doc, choose a case study from the same industry, ideally same sub-vertical and similar size.
4. **Draft subject line** — 3–7 words, lowercase, no exclamation, promise outcome not product.
5. **Draft body** — 80–130 words, follow the six-element structure above.
6. **Compliance check** — verify physical address, unsubscribe link, accurate sender, opt-out language per region.
7. **Personalization review** — verify the personalization hook is accurate (e.g., funding date is correct).
8. **Length check** — under 130 words.
9. **CTA review** — single CTA, 15-minute ask, soft phrasing.
10. **Output** — return subject + body + signature, ready for the sequencing tool.
