# Healthcare & Life Sciences — Prospecting Guide

## 1. Industry Overview

Healthcare and life sciences is a **$4 trillion global industry** with strict regulation (HIPAA in US, GDPR health data in EU, PMDA in Japan, NMPA in China). The industry is dominated by a few large buyers (hospitals, pharma) and a fragmented supplier base (medical device makers, biotech, digital health).

Key characteristics:
- **Heavily regulated** — HIPAA, HITECH, FDA, EMA, plus state-level rules
- **Long clinical cycles** — Drug development 7-10 years; device approval 1-3 years
- **Privacy-first** — Personal health information (PHI) requires special handling
- **Multi-stakeholder** — Physician, hospital admin, payer, regulator, patient
- **Slow tech adoption** — Hospitals especially; legacy EHR systems dominate
- **Outcome-driven** — Clinical evidence matters more than marketing

## 2. Healthcare Sub-Segments

### Hospitals & Health Systems
- **Buyer**: CMIO (Chief Medical Information Officer), CIO, CFO, Department Chair
- **Cycle**: 12-24 months for major IT systems
- **Pain**: EHR optimization (Epic, Cerner), clinician burnout, revenue cycle, population health
- **Tools**: Epic, Cerner (Oracle Health), Athenahealth, Allscripts; CRM: Salesforce Health Cloud

### Pharma & Biotech
- **Buyer**: VP R&D, Head of Clinical Operations, Chief Medical Officer, Head of Drug Safety
- **Cycle**: 6-18 months
- **Pain**: Clinical trial recruitment, drug safety (pharmacovigilance), R&D productivity
- **Tools**: Veeva (CRM, Vault), Medidata (clinical), Oracle Health Sciences

### Medical Devices
- **Buyer**: VP R&D, VP Regulatory, VP Quality
- **Cycle**: 6-18 months
- **Pain**: FDA 510(k) / PMA approval, post-market surveillance, EU MDR compliance
- **Tools**: PLM (Windchill, Teamcenter), QMS (MasterControl, Greenlight Guru)

### Digital Health
- **Sub-types**: Telehealth (Teladoc), mental health (BetterHelp, Cerebral), chronic care (Omada), women's health (Maven)
- **Buyer**: CEO, CPO, Head of Clinical
- **Cycle**: 3-9 months
- **Pain**: Clinical validation, payer reimbursement, user acquisition
- **Tools**: Modern stack (AWS, GCP, Stripe, Twilio)

### Payers (Health Insurance)
- **Buyer**: CIO, VP Clinical Operations, Chief Actuary
- **Cycle**: 12-24 months
- **Pain**: Claims automation, prior auth, member engagement, risk adjustment
- **Tools**: Claims systems (Cognizant, DXC), Salesforce Health Cloud

### Healthcare IT / Services
- Companies that serve healthcare (e.g., healthcare-specific consultancies, RCM companies)
- **Buyer**: CEO, VP Sales
- **Cycle**: 3-9 months
- **Pain**: Client acquisition, talent shortage, compliance

## 3. Healthcare Buyer Personas

### The CMIO (Chief Medical Information Officer)
- **At**: Health system 200+ beds
- **Cares about**: Clinical workflow, EHR optimization, clinician satisfaction, patient outcomes
- **Pain**: EHR usability complaints, alert fatigue, documentation burden
- **Outreach angle**: Workflow integration with EHR, clinician satisfaction metrics
- **What works**: References from peer health systems; published clinical studies

### The VP Clinical Operations (Pharma)
- **At**: Mid-large pharma / biotech
- **Cares about**: Trial enrollment, time-to-database-lock, site performance
- **Pain**: Site activation delays, patient recruitment, data quality
- **Outreach angle**: Site activation speed, enrollment metrics, similar trial references
- **What works**: References from clinical trial sponsors; specific therapeutic area expertise

### The VP Regulatory Affairs (Medical Devices)
- **At**: Any medical device maker
- **Cares about**: Submission timeline, audit findings, post-market compliance
- **Pain**: 510(k) / PMA preparation, EU MDR transition, CAPA management
- **Outreach angle**: Submission timeline reduction, regulatory expertise
- **What works**: Ex-FDA/ex-regulator team members; specific submission experience

### The Head of Quality
- **At**: Medical device / pharma
- **Cares about**: Audit performance, CAPA cycle time, complaint handling
- **Pain**: Manual QMS, audit prep, supplier quality
- **Outreach angle**: Audit findings reduction, CAPA cycle metrics
- **What works**: ISO 13485 references, FDA inspection history

## 5. Healthcare Trigger Events

- **New drug approval** (FDA, EMA) → launch planning, marketing setup
- **Phase 3 trial readout** → commercial scaling
- **FDA warning letter / 483** → remediation spend
- **Clinical trial failure** → reorg, pipeline shift
- **EHR migration announcement** → 12-24 month transition, adjacent tools
- **Hospital M&A** → system consolidation
- **New CIO/CMIO hire** → modernization agenda
- **CMS coverage decision** → revenue cycle changes
- **EU MDR deadline** → regulatory remediation (major for device makers)
- **Telehealth policy change** → digital health market shift

## 7. HIPAA Compliance for Outreach

LeadReach agents must follow HIPAA-safe practices:

### Permitted
- Collecting **business contact information** (name, title, work email, work phone)
- Storing company information about healthcare organizations
- Outreach to healthcare professionals about B2B products/services

### Prohibited
- Collecting or storing any **Protected Health Information** (patient data, diagnoses, treatment info)
- Outreach to **patients** (B2C healthcare outreach is heavily regulated)
- Storing information about an individual's **personal medical history**
- Sending **unsecured** communications about PHI

### Required safeguards
- All healthcare prospect data must be stored encrypted
- Access logs for healthcare prospect data
- BAA (Business Associate Agreement) required if any PHI could be incidentally accessed

## 9. Outreach Best Practices for Healthcare

### Pharma/Biotech Outreach
```
Subject: [specific trial phase] site activation benchmark

Hi [Name],

Noticed [their company] recently started Phase 3 for [drug name] in 
[therapeutic area]. Site activation timelines are often the critical 
path for Phase 3 — we work with peer sponsors like [peer pharma] on 
site activation, specifically reducing activation timeline by 30-45 days.

Worth sharing the benchmark data?

Best,
[Name]
```

### Hospital Outreach
```
Subject: peer health system reference - [specific workflow pain]

Hi [Name],

We work with peer health systems like [peer 1] and [peer 2] on 
[specific pain point, e.g., alert fatigue, prior auth automation].

At [peer 1], we reduced [specific metric] by [X%] in [timeframe]. 
Happy to share the case study if relevant.

Best,
[Name]
```

### Compliance Notes for Outreach
- **Never** mention specific patient data
- **Never** claim FDA endorsement
- **Always** use peer references — healthcare buyers verify references religiously
- **Always** mention relevant certifications (HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 13485, etc.)
- **Avoid** clinical claims unless FDA-cleared

## 10. Common Healthcare Prospecting Mistakes

- **Pitching to physicians about B2B tools** — physicians don't buy hospital IT
- **Ignoring the EHR** — every solution must integrate with Epic/Cerner
- **Wrong regulatory language** — pharma vs. device vs. hospital have different rules
- **Consumer healthcare outreach** — heavily regulated; many states prohibit cold outreach to consumers about healthcare
- **No peer references** — healthcare buyers require peer references
- **Missing compliance certifications** — must mention HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 13485 as relevant
- **Phase confusion** — don't pitch commercial scaling to a pre-clinical biotech
- **Therapeutic area blindness** — pitching oncology tools to a cardiovascular device maker
