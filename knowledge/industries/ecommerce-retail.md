# E-commerce & Retail — Prospecting Guide

## 1. Industry Overview

E-commerce and retail is a **$30 trillion global industry** undergoing structural transformation. The line between "e-commerce" and "retail" has blurred — every retailer sells online; every D2C brand opens pop-ups. Key dynamics:

- **D2C explosion**: 100K+ direct-to-consumer brands globally; 1000+ new brands launched monthly
- **Marketplace dominance**: Amazon, Alibaba, Shopee, MercadoLibre command the bulk of GMV
- **Headless commerce**: Frontend/backend separation (Shopify Hydrogen, BigCommerce, commerce layer)
- **Social commerce**: TikTok Shop, Instagram Shopping, Pinduoduo — buying inside social platforms
- **Fulfillment as differentiator**: 1-day delivery, dark stores, micro-fulfillment

## 2. Sub-Segments

### D2C Brands (Direct-to-Consumer)
- **Examples**: Allbirds, Glossier, Warby Parker, Casper, Brooklinen, Outdoor Voices
- **Stage signals**: Bootstrapped / Seed (Shopify store, <$1M GMV); Series A-B ($1-50M GMV, building team); Series C+ ($50M+ GMV, scaling operations)
- **Buyer**: Founder (early), VP Marketing / Head of Growth (Series A+), VP Operations / COO (Series B+)
- **Cycle**: 30-90 days
- **Pain**: CAC (customer acquisition cost), LTV, retention, fulfillment costs, returns

### Omnichannel Retailers
- **Examples**: Target, Walmart, Sephora, Nordstrom, Best Buy
- **Buyer**: VP E-commerce, Head of Digital, VP Store Operations, CTO
- **Cycle**: 6-18 months
- **Pain**: Channel integration, BOPIS (buy online pickup in store), inventory visibility

### Marketplaces
- **Examples**: Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Wayfair, Alibaba, Shopee, MercadoLibre
- **Buyer**: VP Seller Services, Head of Marketplace, CTO
- **Cycle**: 6-12 months
- **Pain**: Seller acquisition, fraud prevention, marketplace integrity

### E-commerce Enablers (SaaS for e-commerce)
- **Examples**: Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento (Adobe), Salesforce Commerce Cloud
- **Buyer**: VP Product, CTO
- **Cycle**: 3-9 months
- **Pain**: Platform scalability, developer ecosystem, merchant success

### Logistics & Fulfillment
- **Examples**: ShipBob, Deliverr, Flexport, Quiet Logistics
- **Buyer**: COO, VP Operations
- **Cycle**: 3-9 months
- **Pain**: Capacity, technology integration, cost-per-order

## 3. Buyer Personas

### The D2C Founder (Early Stage)
- **At**: Bootstrapped / pre-seed D2C
- **Cares about**: First customers, product-market fit, cash flow
- **Pain**: CAC too high, low repeat purchase rate, manual operations
- **Outreach angle**: Specific tactics, references to similar-brand growth stories
- **What works**: Practical tactical content; tool recommendations
- **What fails**: Enterprise-speak, "scale" messaging

### The VP E-commerce (Established Brand)
- **At**: Brand with $5M+ online revenue
- **Cares about**: Conversion rate, AOV, customer LTV, channel expansion
- **Pain**: Tech stack complexity, attribution, multi-channel operations
- **Outreach angle**: Specific metrics, similar-GMV benchmarks
- **What works**: References from similar-size brands; technical specificity

### The Head of Growth
- **At**: D2C brand $1M+ revenue
- **Cares about**: CAC, LTV/CAC ratio, paid social efficiency, retention
- **Pain**: Facebook/Google ad cost increases, attribution post-iOS 14
- **Outreach angle**: Specific channel performance benchmarks, retention tactics
- **What works**: Concrete case studies with before/after metrics

### The COO / VP Operations
- **At**: Brand $10M+ revenue
- **Cares about**: Fulfillment SLA, cost-per-order, return rate, inventory turns
- **Pain**: 3PL relationships, peak season capacity, returns processing
- **Outreach angle**: Cost-per-order benchmarks, peak season preparation
- **What works**: Specific operational metrics, references from similar-category brands

## 4. E-commerce Technographic Signals

E-commerce is the **most detectable industry** — almost everything is in the HTML/JS of the website. Use PublicWWW / BuiltWith to detect:

### E-commerce Platform
- **Shopify** — Most common for D2C; detectable via `Shopify.theme` JS, `cdn.shopify.com` URLs
- **Shopify Plus** — Enterprise tier (look for plus-specific features)
- **WooCommerce** — WordPress-based; detectable via `woocommerce` JS
- **BigCommerce** — Detectable via `bigcommerce.com` URLs
- **Magento** (Adobe Commerce) — Detectable via `Magento` JS objects
- **Salesforce Commerce Cloud** — Detectable via specific URLs and JS
- **Custom / Headless** — Next.js, React, Vue with custom backend (often via API)

### Payments
- **Stripe** — Detectable via `js.stripe.com`
- **PayPal** — Standard
- **Adyen** — Enterprise
- **Braintree** — PayPal-owned, common in mid-market
- **Apple Pay / Google Pay** — Often layered on top

### Analytics & Marketing
- **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** — Universal
- **Meta Pixel** — Facebook/Instagram ads
- **TikTok Pixel** — TikTok ads
- **Klaviyo** — Email/SMS marketing (Shopify-native)
- **Segment** — CDP

### Customer Service

### Fulfillment / Logistics

### Reviews / UGC

### Subscription / Loyalty

## 5. Trigger Events

- **New product launch** — expansion play
- **Funding round** — scaling, infrastructure investment
- **New executive hire** (especially from a known brand) — modernization
- **Major ad campaign launch** — growth mode
- **Marketplace expansion** (e.g., adding Amazon, international) — operational scaling
- **Replatform announcement** — 12-month project, adjacent tool purchases
- **Earnings report** (for public retailers) — strategic priorities
- **Store closure announcement** — cost optimization
- **Returns crisis** (news about high return rates) — returns optimization
- **Peak season preparation** (Q3 for holiday readiness)
- **Trade show participation** (ShopTalk, NRF Big Show, eTail)

## 6. E-commerce Vocabulary

- **GMV** — Gross Merchandise Value
- **AOV** — Average Order Value
- **CAC** — Customer Acquisition Cost
- **LTV** — Lifetime Value
- **LTV/CAC** — Efficiency ratio (target: 3:1 or better)
- **Repeat purchase rate** — % of customers who buy again
- **Conversion rate** — % of sessions that result in purchase
- **Bounce rate** — % of sessions with one pageview
- **Cart abandonment** — % of carts not completed
- **RPU** — Revenue Per User
- **MRR / ARR** — Monthly/Annual Recurring Revenue (for subscription D2C)
- **BOPIS** — Buy Online, Pickup In Store
- **3PL** — Third-Party Logistics
- **4PL** — Fourth-Party Logistics (manages 3PLs)
- **SLA** — Service Level Agreement (e.g., shipping time)
- **DC** — Distribution Center
- **Fulfillment** — Picking, packing, shipping
- **Returns / Reverse logistics** — Handling returns
- **COD** — Cash on Delivery (common in APAC)
- **Headless commerce** — Frontend/backend separation
- **Composable commerce** — Best-of-breed stack
- **MACH** — Microservices, API-first, Cloud-native, Headless
- **Pixel** — Tracking code (Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel)
- **CDP** — Customer Data Platform (Segment, mParticle)
- **CMS** — Content Management System

## 8. Outreach Best Practices

### D2C Brand Outreach
```
Subject: noticed your [specific product] launch - retention idea

Hi [Name],

Saw [their brand] just launched [specific product] — the positioning 
around [specific detail] is sharp.

We work with similar [vertical] D2C brands like [peer 1] and [peer 2] 
on post-purchase retention. At [peer 1], we lifted 60-day repeat 
purchase rate from 18% to 27% with [specific tactic].

Worth a 15-min look? I can share the case study first if useful.

Best,
[Name]
```

### Marketplace Seller Outreach
```
Subject: amazon → D2C expansion

Hi [Name],

Noticed [their brand] is growing on Amazon but the D2C site is 
underperforming (no Shopify Plus, basic Klaviyo flows).

We help Amazon-first brands build profitable D2C channels — 
peer brand [peer name] went from $0 to $2M D2C in 8 months.

Worth a chat?

Best,
[Name]
```

## 9. Compliance Considerations

- **PCI DSS** — Don't collect payment card data in outreach
- **CCPA / CPRA** — California consumer privacy
- **GDPR** — EU data protection
- **CAN-SPAM** — Commercial email regulations
- **State privacy laws** — Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, etc.
- **FTC Endorsement Guides** — If using testimonials, must be honest
- **ADA compliance** — Don't claim websites are accessible if not verified

## 10. Common E-commerce Prospecting Mistakes

- **Pitching "more sales"** without specifics — meaningless
- **Wrong platform** — pitching Shopify apps to a Magento merchant
- **Wrong stage** — pitching enterprise tools to a $1M GMV brand
- **Ignoring the stack** — pitching Klaviyo alternative when they just installed Klaviyo
- **Asking for a meeting before showing value** — D2C founders are busy; show value first
- **No specific metrics** — "increase revenue" is useless; "lift AOV 12%" is useful
- **Wrong attribution model** — pitching attribution tools post-iOS 14 requires understanding the new landscape
