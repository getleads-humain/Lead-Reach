# Travel Technology — B2B Lead Intelligence Guide

## 1. Industry Overview

Travel technology is a **$20B+ market by 2028**, encompassing the platforms, infrastructure, and software that power the global travel industry. Post-COVID recovery compressed 5-7 years of technology adoption into 18 months, creating a permanent step-change in digital infrastructure investment. The industry is defined by **intermediary economics** (OTAs command 12-25% commissions on bookings), **seasonal and cyclical demand patterns** (Q2/Q3 peak for leisure, steady corporate travel), **legacy distribution systems** (GDS platforms dating to the 1960s still handle 70%+ of airline bookings), and **NDC (New Distribution Capability)** reshaping how airlines distribute inventory.

Key characteristics:
- **Commission-driven revenue** — OTAs, TMCs, and metasearch platforms live on margins between supplier cost and consumer price, making every efficiency gain a direct margin improvement
- **Distribution oligopoly** — Amadeus, Sabre, and Travelport control ~95% of GDS volume; NDC is the first credible challenge to this structure in 40 years
- **Seasonal cyclicality** — Booking volumes swing 40-60% between peak and off-peak seasons, creating pressure on technology that must scale elastically
- **Post-COVID acceleration** — Contactless check-in, virtual payments, AI-driven pricing, and health documentation are now table stakes, not differentiators
- **Corporate travel under pressure** — CFOs cut T&E aggressively in downturns; duty-of-care and sustainability mandates add complexity
- **Data gravity** — Travel generates enormous behavioral data (search, browse, compare, book, review); companies that own the data own the leverage

The buying motion differs by sub-segment:
- **Selling to OTAs**: Product/Engineering-led, 3-6 month cycles. Decisions by VP Product or CTO. Speed-to-market and conversion optimization are the currencies.
- **Selling to airlines**: Procurement-heavy, 9-18 month cycles. Committee-driven with IT, revenue management, and commercial teams. Legacy system integration is the gating factor.
- **Selling to corporate travel**: Finance/Operations-led, 4-8 month cycles. CFO or VP Travel runs evaluation. ROI is king — every dollar saved on travel policy compliance is measurable.
- **Selling to tour/activity operators**: Owner/operator-led, 2-8 week cycles. Fast decisions, smaller ACV, high volume of prospects.

LeadReach is best positioned for OTA mid-market, corporate travel management companies, and travel SaaS vendors. Selling to Tier-1 airlines and global hotel chains requires dedicated enterprise motions.

## 2. Sub-Segment Deep Dives

### Online Travel Agencies / OTAs

| Cycle | 3-6 months |
| ACV | $80k-$500k |

- **Market shape**: Booking Holdings and Expedia Group dominate with ~70% of global OTA bookings. Mid-market OTAs (Agoda, Traveloka, MakeMyTrip, Kiwi.com) compete regionally. - **Vocabulary & jargon**: Virtual card, lodge card, UATP, BSP (Billing and Settlement Plan), ARC (Airlines Reporting Corporation), settlement, interchange, merchant of record, PSD2, SCA (Strong Customer Authentication), FX markup, dynamic currency conversion, refund policy, chargeback ratio, authorization rate.

## 4. Buyer Personas

### VP E-Commerce / OTA
- **At**: Mid-large OTA or travel marketplace
- **Cares about**: Conversion rate, booking volume, ADR, customer acquisition cost, take rate
- **Pain**: Look-to-book ratio declining, metasearch costs rising, direct booking share stagnant, Google dominating top of funnel
- **Outreach angle**: Specific conversion lift from peer OTAs; data enrichment that improves search relevance; pricing intelligence
- **Channels**: LinkedIn, Phocuswright conference, email
- **What works**: Peer OTA case studies with specific conversion and revenue metrics; data showing ROI within 90 days
- **What fails**: Generic SaaS pitches; anything that doesn't speak to conversion, take rate, or ADR

### CTO Travel Tech
- **At**: Travel SaaS company, OTA technology team, or airline IT
- **Cares about**: System reliability (99.9%+), API performance, scalability during peak season, technical debt reduction
- **Pain**: Legacy GDS integration complexity, monolith decomposition, seasonal traffic spikes (10x), data pipeline reliability, compliance (PCI-DSS, GDPR)
- **Outreach angle**: Technical depth, integration benchmarks, API reliability, seasonal scaling case studies
- **Channels**: LinkedIn, GitHub, engineering blogs, travel tech meetups
- **What works**: Technical proof points (latency, uptime, integration time); reference architecture from peer companies
- **What fails**: Marketing-heavy pitches without technical substance; ignoring GDS integration reality

### VP Corporate Travel
- **At**: Enterprise company with $5M+ annual travel spend, or TMC
- **Cares about**: Policy compliance, traveler satisfaction, T&E cost reduction, duty of care, sustainability reporting
- **Pain**: Out-of-policy bookings (40-60%), fragmented traveler data, manual expense reconciliation, traveler safety tracking gaps, carbon reporting requirements
- **Outreach angle**: Compliance improvement metrics, duty-of-care enhancement, T&E savings from peer companies
- **Channels**: Email, GBTA conference, ACTE, LinkedIn
- **What works**: Hard ROI numbers tied to policy compliance improvement; integration with Concur/Navan; sustainability reporting capability
- **What fails**: Consumer travel language; ignoring the policy/compliance angle; not mentioning duty of care

### VP Airline IT
- **At**: Airline technology division
- **Cares about**: PSS reliability, NDC readiness, operational efficiency, revenue management accuracy
- **Pain**: PSS migration risk, GDS cost ($3-5/segment), NDC implementation complexity, ancillary revenue optimization, legacy system integration
- **Outreach angle**: NDC readiness tools, PSS migration risk mitigation, ancillary revenue uplift from peer airlines
- **Channels**: IATA events, Airline IT conferences, LinkedIn, direct executive outreach
- **What works**: Airline-specific case studies with PNR volume metrics; IATA/NDC expertise; references from same-size airline
- **What fails**: Not understanding PSS architecture; proposing cloud migration without addressing PSS migration risk; ignoring airline operational constraints

## 7. Travel Technology Vocabulary & Jargon

### Distribution & Booking
- **GDS** — Global Distribution System (Amadeus, Sabre, Travelport)
- **NDC** — New Distribution Capability (IATA standard for airline distribution)
- **PNR** — Passenger Name Record (booking reference)
- **PNR lifecycle** — Create, modify, ticket, fulfill
- **Direct connect** — Airline-to-agency connection bypassing GDS
- **EDIFACT** — Legacy messaging standard for GDS communication
- **API/XML** — Modern booking communication protocols
- **ONE Order** — IATA initiative to replace PNR/ticket/EMD with single order
- **ATPCO** — Airline Tariff Publishing Company (fare data)
- **Merchant model** — OTA buys inventory at wholesale, sells at retail
- **Agency model** — OTA earns commission on booking, passes inventory at cost

### Revenue & Operations
- **RevPAR** — Revenue Per Available Room (hotels)
- **ADR** — Average Daily Rate (hotels)
- **RBD** — Reservation Booking Designator (airline fare class code)
- **Bid price** — Minimum acceptable fare for a seat (revenue management)
- **Spill / Recapture** — Demand turned away / demand recovered from competitors
- **No-show rate** — Percentage of bookings that don't show up
- **Cancellation rate** — Percentage of bookings cancelled
- **Look-to-book ratio** — Searches to bookings (typical OTA: 30-50:1)
- **Take rate** — OTA commission percentage (12-25%)
- **Booking window** — Time between booking and travel date

### Corporate Travel
- **TMC** — Travel Management Company
- **Duty of care** — Legal obligation to track and protect traveling employees
- **Policy compliance** — % of bookings within corporate travel policy
- **NBE** — New Booking Experience (modern booking interface)
- **T&E** — Travel & Expense
- **Per diem** — Daily allowance for business travel
- **SBTi** — Science Based Targets initiative (sustainability reporting)
- **SLA** — Service Level Agreement (between company and TMC)
- **RFP season** — Annual travel program review cycle (typically Q4)

### Payments
- **BSP** — Billing and Settlement Plan (IATA, international)
- **ARC** — Airlines Reporting Corporation (US)
- **UATP** — Universal Air Travel Plan (airline-owned payment network)
- **Virtual card** — Single-use card number for specific transaction
- **Lodge card** — Centralized corporate travel payment card
- **SCA** — Strong Customer Authentication (PSD2 requirement)

## 9. Outreach Best Practices & Sequencing

### Channel Sequencing for Travel Tech

Travel tech buyers respond to **industry-specific credibility** before product pitches. Recommended sequence:

- **Day 0**: LinkedIn connection — reference a recent Phocuswright report, IATA announcement, or their company's expansion news.
- **Day 3**: Cold email #1 — 120 words, lead with a travel-specific insight (NDC adoption rate, conversion benchmark, compliance stat), no product pitch.
- **Day 7**: LinkedIn engagement — thoughtful comment on a post about distribution, pricing, or corporate travel.
- **Day 10**: Cold email #2 — case study from same travel sub-vertical, mention seasonal ROI timing.
- **Day 14**: Share a relevant Phocuswright or Skift research snippet — value before ask.
- **Day 17**: Cold call — reference the research share and the emails.
- **Day 21**: Cold email #3 — breakup email with a specific metric ("companies like yours see 30% more qualified pipeline").
- **Day 28**: Final touch — invite to a travel-industry roundtable or webinar.

Expected meeting-booked rate: **1.8-2.8%** (higher than fintech due to smaller buying committees in mid-market).

## 10. Regulatory & Compliance Notes

- **PCI-DSS**: Required for any company handling cardholder data. Most OTAs and TMCs are Level 1. Travel payments companies require strict compliance.
- **GDPR**: Travel data is personal data — PNR data, passport information, travel patterns. EU travel companies are heavily regulated under GDPR. US companies serving EU travelers must comply.
- **PSD2 / SCA**: Strong Customer Authentication for online payments in EU. Travel companies must implement 3DS2 or risk authorization rate decline.
- **IATA Resolution 787**: Governs NDC standards. NDC-certified solutions have credibility advantage.
- **EU261 / APPR / DOT rules**: Passenger compensation regulations. Airlines and OTAs must comply; non-compliance drives technology demand.
- **Duty of care legislation**: Various jurisdictions (UK Health & Safety at Work Act, EU Framework Directive) require employer tracking of traveling employees.
- **Data residency**: Some markets (China, Russia, India) require travel data to be stored locally.
- **Travel advertising regulations**: DOT full-fare advertising rule (US); EU Omnibus Directive on pricing transparency.
- **Carbon reporting**: CSRD (EU) requires Scope 3 travel emissions reporting. SBTi commitments are increasingly common for large enterprises.

LeadReach agents should NOT:
- Store PNR or passport data (GDPR risk)
- Target individual travelers for B2B products (consumer data regulations)
- Make claims about NDC certification that LeadReach doesn't hold
- Ignore seasonal booking patterns when timing outreach (off-peak outreach is less effective)
- Reference traveler-specific data in outreach (privacy violation)

## 13. Regional Variants

- **US**: Largest single travel market ($400B+). Corporate travel dominated by Concur. GDS dominated by Sabre. Strong domestic OTA market (Expedia, Priceline). NDC adoption lagging behind EU.
- **EU**: Open skies, PSD2/SCA, strong sustainability mandates. Amadeus-dominant GDS. Ryanair/easyJet LCC model drives direct distribution. NDC adoption ahead of US. GDPR applies to all travel data.
- **APAC**: Fastest-growing travel market. Diverse — Japan (JTB, Rakuten Travel), India (MakeMyTrip, Goibibo), Southeast Asia (Traveloka, Agoda). Multi-currency and multi-language complexity. GDS share varies by market.
- **China**: Ctrip (Trip.com Group) dominates. Independent GDS (TravelSky). Data residency requirements. Unique payment ecosystem (Alipay, WeChat Pay). Limited access for foreign vendors.
- **Middle East**: Emirates/Abu Dhabi/Doha hub model. Amadeus-dominant. High corporate travel spend. Luxury and premium segment focus.
- **Latin America**: Growing OTA market (Decolar, CVC Corp). B2B payments complexity (FX, local payment methods). Amadeus-dominant GDS.

## 15. Common Travel Prospecting Mistakes

- **Not knowing OTA vs. TMC** — These are fundamentally different businesses with different buyers. OTAs are tech companies; TMCs are service companies with technology.
- **Ignoring seasonal timing** — Pitching booking optimization in off-peak season gets ignored; pitch analytics and planning in off-peak, conversion tools pre-peak.
- **Wrong GDS reference** — Pitching Sabre-specific integration to an Amadeus shop (or vice versa) destroys credibility instantly.
- **Not understanding commission economics** — If you don't know that OTAs earn 12-25% commission, you can't talk about their margin improvement.
- **Consumer travel language in B2B outreach** — "Great deals on flights" language in corporate travel outreach signals you don't understand the market.
- **Underestimating PSS migration risk** — This is career-defining for airline CIOs. Don't propose rip-and-replace; propose augmentation.
- **Missing duty of care** — In corporate travel, duty of care is a legal obligation. Not mentioning it signals you don't understand the vertical.
- **Ignoring NDC transition** — NDC is the biggest distribution change in 40 years. Not mentioning it in airline outreach is a miss.
- **Generic data vendor comparison** — Travel buyers have been burned by ZoomInfo/Apollo data that doesn't understand travel distribution. LeadReach must differentiate on domain specificity.
- **Not tracking Phocuswright/Skift** — These are the industry's information gatekeepers. Not referencing their research signals outsider status.

## 16. Knowledge Gaps & Expansion Notes

- Need a dedicated sub-doc for **hotel technology** — PMS, CRS, channel management is a distinct buying motion from OTA/airline.
- Need an **NDC readiness playbook** — the biggest distribution change in 40 years deserves its own detailed guide with airline-specific NDC adoption timelines.
- Need a **Chinese travel market** specific guide — Trip.com Group, TravelSky, and Alipay/WeChat ecosystem are unique and largely inaccessible to western vendors.
- Need a **sustainability/carbon reporting** sub-doc — CSRD and SBTi requirements are creating a new category of travel technology buyer.
- **LCC technology stack** needs separate treatment — Navitaire, commercial model, and ancillary-driven distribution is different from full-service carriers.
- Need regional variants for **India (MakeMyTrip/Goibibo/UPI)** and **Southeast Asia (Traveloka/Agoda)** — both massive, fast-growing markets.
- **Travel insurance technology** is an emerging segment that could justify its own sub-doc — parametric insurance, claims automation, and embedded insurance at booking.
- Need a **vacation rental / alternative accommodation** sub-doc — Airbnb, Vrbo, and property management platforms (Guesty, Hostfully) are a distinct ecosystem.
