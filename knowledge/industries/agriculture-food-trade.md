# Agriculture & Food Trade — Prospecting Guide

## 1. Industry Overview

The agriculture and food trade industry moves **$1.5 trillion of products annually across borders**. It is fragmented (millions of smallholders and traders), relationship-driven (decades-long supplier relationships), and regulated (phytosanitary, FDA, EFSA, AQIS). Digital adoption is **lower than SaaS** but accelerating — especially in trade documentation, supply chain visibility, and B2B marketplaces.

Key characteristics:
- **Fragmented supply base** — Millions of farms, thousands of aggregators, hundreds of exporters per country
- **Relationship-driven** — Decades-long buyer-supplier relationships; trust matters more than price
- **Regulated** — Phytosanitary certs, FDA prior notice, organic certs, fair trade, food safety (HACCP, BRC, IFS)
- **Seasonal** — Harvest cycles drive supply; pricing volatility
- **Physical inspection** — Buyers inspect product before purchase; in-person relationships matter
- **Slow digital adoption** — Many exporters still use WhatsApp + email; no CRM

## 2. The Agriculture Trade Value Chain

Understanding the value chain is critical — each node has different buyers, signals, and outreach angles.

### Node 1: Smallholder Farmers
- **Count**: Millions per country (e.g., ~10M in Vietnam)
- **What they sell**: Raw agricultural product at farm gate
- **Pain**: Price discovery, access to finance, weather risk, post-harvest loss
- **Tools**: Mobile phones, basic WhatsApp, no CRM
- **Prospect?**: Usually NO — too small, too fragmented. Exception: cooperative aggregators.

### Node 2: Cooperatives & Aggregators
- **Count**: Hundreds per country
- **What they do**: Aggregate product from many smallholders, basic processing, sell to exporters
- **Pain**: Quality control, traceability, working capital, logistics
- **Tools**: Excel, basic CRM (sometimes), WhatsApp
- **Prospect?**: YES for agtech, fintech, supply chain tools

### Node 3: Exporters (The Primary Target)
- **Count**: Hundreds to thousands per country per product category
- **What they do**: Buy from aggregators, process/package, handle export logistics, sell to importers
- **Pain**: Market access, buyer discovery, trade finance, quality consistency, compliance
- **Tools**: Excel, email, WhatsApp, sometimes SAP/Oracle for larger ones
- **Prospect?**: YES for B2B marketplaces, trade finance, logistics tech

### Node 4: Importers / Distributors
- **Count**: Hundreds per country per product category
- **What they do**: Import product, hold inventory, distribute to retailers/wholesalers
- **Pain**: Sourcing diversification, supplier reliability, regulatory compliance, inventory management
- **Tools**: ERP (SAP, Oracle), CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot), sometimes EDI
- **Prospect?**: YES for procurement tech, supplier discovery, supply chain visibility

### Node 5: Retailers / Wholesalers
- **Count**: Tens per country (concentrated)
- **What they do**: Sell to end consumers (supermarkets) or foodservice
- **Pain**: Price volatility, supplier reliability, consumer trends, sustainability
- **Tools**: Sophisticated ERP, analytics, vendor management
- **Prospect?**: YES for supplier discovery, sustainability tracking, analytics

### Node 6: Food Manufacturers / Processors
- **Count**: Thousands per country
- **What they do**: Process raw agricultural product into packaged foods
- **Pain**: Raw material sourcing, quality consistency, cost optimization, ingredient traceability
- **Tools**: ERP (SAP, Oracle), manufacturing execution systems (MES), quality management
- **Prospect?**: YES for ingredient sourcing, traceability, quality management

## 3. The Canonical Use Case: Dragonfruit Suppliers in Vietnam

This is the example cited in the user's request. Walk through how LeadReach should handle this query end-to-end.

### Step 1: Query Understanding (Atlas)
The user asks: "Dragonfruit Suppliers/Distributors based in Vietnam"

Atlas must decompose this into:
- **Product**: Dragonfruit (also: pitaya, thanh long in Vietnamese)
- **Geography**: Vietnam
- **Role in value chain**: Suppliers (exporters) AND Distributors (importers in destination markets)
- **Intent**: Find companies, get contacts, identify all relevant info

### Step 2: Geographic & Product Context (Sage)
- Vietnam is the **world's largest dragonfruit exporter** (~50% of global trade)
- Production concentrated in **Binh Thuan province** (south-central coast) and **Tien Giang** (Mekong Delta)
- Harvest season: Year-round with peak May-September
- Top export markets: **China** (60%+), EU, USA, Japan, South Korea
- Vietnamese name: **Thanh Long** — search using both English and Vietnamese terms
- Common export companies named: **Kim My Phat, Hoan Chau, Dragon Fruit Co., etc.**

### Step 3: Source Discovery (Scout)
Scout must search across:

1. **Google Maps / OpenStreetMap** — Search "dragonfruit export" + "Vietnam" + city names (Binh Thuan, Tien Giang, Ho Chi Minh City)
   - Returns: Company name, address, phone, website (if listed), reviews
   - Quality: Medium — many exporters don't have websites

2. **Vietnamese Trade Directories**:
   - **Vietnam Trade Promotion Agency (VIETRADE)** — Government trade directory
   - **VCCI (Vietnam Chamber of Commerce and Industry)** — Member directory
   - **Vietnam Export Forum** — Exporter listings
   - **Alibaba** — Many Vietnamese dragonfruit exporters list here
   - **Global Sources** — Similar to Alibaba, more curated
   - **Made-in-China.com** — Also lists Vietnamese suppliers

3. **Customs & Trade Data**:
   - **Panjiva** (paid) — Bill of lading data, shows actual exporters
   - **ImportGenius** (paid) — Similar, with different data sources
   - **Vietnam Customs** — Public trade statistics
   - **UN Comtrade** — Free, country-level trade data

4. **Industry Associations**:
   - **Vietnam Dragon Fruit Association** (Hội Thanh Long Việt Nam) — Member list
   - **Vietnam Fruit and Vegetables Association** (VINAFA) — Member directory
   - **Binh Thuan Dragon Fruit Association** — Regional member list

5. **News & Press**:
   - **Vietnam News** (English-language) — Export announcements
   - **VnExpress International** — Trade news
   - **Saigon Times** — Business news
   - **Industry publications**: Asia Fruit Magazine, Fresh Plaza, Fresh Fruit Portal

6. **LinkedIn Search**:
   - Search: "dragonfruit" OR "thanh long" + "Vietnam" — find individuals
   - Filter by: Industry = "Food & Beverages" or "Import and Export"
   - Note: Many Vietnamese exporters are NOT active on LinkedIn

7. **Government Programs**:
   - **USDA GAP (Good Agricultural Practices) certified** — searchable database
   - **GlobalG.A.P. certified** — searchable database
   - **VietGAP** — Vietnamese certification program

### Step 4: Enrichment (Forge)
For each discovered company, enrich with:

- **Company name** (English + Vietnamese)
- **Address** (headquarters + processing facility)
- **Phone** (main + WhatsApp)
- **Email** (info@, sales@, or personal)
- **Website** (if any)
- **Year established**
- **Number of employees** (often <50, hard to verify)
- **Export markets** (which countries)
- **Certifications** (GlobalG.A.P., VietGAP, HACCP, BRC, IFS, organic)
- **Annual export volume** (often hard to find; estimate from news mentions)
- **Key personnel** (CEO, Export Manager, Sales Manager)
- **HS code** (typically 0810.40 for dragonfruit)
- **INCOTERMS used** (FOB, CIF, EXW)
- **Packaging specifications** (5kg carton, 10kg carton, etc.)
- **Min order quantity** (MOQ)
- **Payment terms** (LC, TT, etc.)

### Step 5: Verification (Judge)
For each enriched profile, verify:
- Phone number is reachable (call or WhatsApp check)
- Email bounces (SMTP verify)
- Address is real (geocode)
- Exporter status (check Vietnam Customs registered exporter list)
- Certifications are valid (verify with certifying body)

## 4. Agriculture Trade Buyer Personas

### The Export Manager
- **At**: Mid-size exporter (10-100 FTE)
- **Cares about**: Finding new buyers, managing logistics, ensuring quality, getting paid on time
- **Pain**: Buyer discovery, payment risk, quality complaints, logistics delays, regulatory changes
- **Outreach angle**: New market access, buyer matching, trade finance, logistics simplification
- **Channels**: WhatsApp, email, trade shows (Asia Fruit Logistica, Fruit Logistica Berlin, Gulfood)
- **What works**: Direct, relationship-focused outreach; offering specific buyer introductions
- **What fails**: Generic "transform your business with AI" pitches

### The Procurement Manager (Importer Side)
- **At**: Importer/distributor (50-500 FTE)
- **Cares about**: Supplier reliability, quality consistency, price competitiveness, compliance
- **Pain**: Supplier diversification, quality variability, regulatory compliance, traceability
- **Outreach angle**: New supplier markets, quality assurance, supply chain visibility
- **Channels**: Email, LinkedIn, trade shows
- **What works**: Specific supplier recommendations, quality data, certifications

### The Sustainability Manager
- **At**: Larger importers/retailers (200+ FTE)
- **Cares about**: ESG compliance, deforestation-free sourcing, fair labor, organic certification
- **Pain**: Traceability to farm level, audit burden, certification management
- **Outreach angle**: Traceability tech, certification management, ESG reporting
- **Channels**: LinkedIn, sustainability conferences

## 5. Agriculture Industry Vocabulary

Use the right terminology:

- **HS Code** — Harmonized System code for product classification (dragonfruit: 0810.40)
- **INCOTERMS** — International Commercial Terms (FOB, CIF, EXW, DDP)
- **Phytosanitary certificate** — Required for plant product export
- **GAP** — Good Agricultural Practices (GlobalG.A.P., VietGAP, USDA GAP)
- **HACCP** — Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point (food safety)
- **BRC** — British Retail Consortium certification
- **IFS** — International Food Standard
- **LC** — Letter of Credit (payment method)
- **TT** — Telegraphic Transfer (bank transfer)
- **MOQ** — Minimum Order Quantity
- **FOB** — Free On Board (seller delivers to port)
- **CIF** — Cost, Insurance, Freight (seller delivers to destination port)
- **Cold chain** — Temperature-controlled supply chain
- **Reefer** — Refrigerated shipping container
- **Bill of lading** — Shipping document
- **Letter of credit** — Bank-guaranteed payment

## 6. Agriculture Trade Trigger Events

- **New phytosanitary agreement** between Vietnam and a new market (e.g., Vietnam-China protocol)
- **Customs clearance issues** at a border (news coverage)
- **New GAP certification** by an exporter
- **Trade show participation** (Asia Fruit Logistica, Fruit Logistica, Gulfood, SIAL)
- **Buyer mission visits** organized by trade promotion agencies
- **Government trade missions**
- **New competitor country** entering a market (e.g., dragonfruit from Malaysia entering EU)
- **Currency fluctuations** (VND/USD/CNY) — affects competitiveness
- **Weather events** (typhoon, drought) affecting harvest
- **Pest/disease outbreak** in production region
- **New logistics route** opened (e.g., direct shipping line Vietnam-EU)

## 7. Outreach Best Practices for Agriculture Trade

### Email Pattern
Agriculture exporters respond best to short, direct, relationship-focused emails:

```
Subject: dragonfruit import inquiry - [your country]

Dear [Name],

I am [your name] from [your company] in [your country]. We are looking for reliable dragonfruit suppliers and found [their company] through [source].

Could you share:
- Current export markets
- Available certifications (GlobalG.A.P., organic)
- MOQ and pricing FOB Ho Chi Minh
- Lead time

If you have a catalog or product specifications, please share.

Best regards,
[Your name]
[Your company]
[Phone / WhatsApp]
```

This style works because:
- It's clearly a real inquiry, not marketing
- It specifies the product (dragonfruit)
- It shows you've researched them (mentions source)
- It asks specific questions (not vague)
- It includes WhatsApp (preferred channel in Vietnam)

### Channels
- **WhatsApp** is the dominant channel in Vietnamese agriculture trade
- **WeChat** for China-focused exporters
- **Email** is secondary; expect slower responses
- **LinkedIn** is rare among exporters (more common among importers)
- **Trade shows** are the highest-value touch — Asia Fruit Logistica (Sept, Hong Kong), Fruit Logistica (Feb, Berlin), Gulfood (Feb, Dubai)

### Cultural Notes
- Vietnamese business culture values **relationships and face-to-face meetings**
- **Tet (Lunar New Year, late Jan/Feb)** is a major holiday — no business for 2 weeks
- **Decision-making is consensus-oriented** — multiple stakeholders
- **English proficiency varies** — keep emails simple, avoid idioms
- **Pricing is negotiable** — expect back-and-forth
- **Honorifics matter** — use Mr./Ms. + first name (Vietnamese order: family name + middle + given; address by given name with title)

## 8. Compliance Considerations

- **GDPR** — Don't collect personal data of EU-based prospects without consent
- **Phytosanitary** — Importing countries have specific requirements (USDA APHIS for US, EFSA for EU)
- **MRL (Maximum Residue Limits)** — Pesticide residue limits vary by market
- **Customs** — HS code classification is critical for tariffs
- **Sanctions** — Some countries/regions are sanctioned (Russia, Iran, etc.)
- **Anti-bribery** — FCPA (US), UK Bribery Act, etc. apply to international trade
