# Real Estate & Construction — Prospecting Guide

## 1. Industry Overview

Real estate and construction together represent a **$15 trillion global industry** characterized by:
- **Local regulation** — every jurisdiction has zoning, building codes, licensing
- **Relationship-driven** — decades-long relationships between developers, brokers, contractors
- **Slow digital adoption** — many firms still run on Excel + email
- **Cyclical** — interest rate sensitive; boom/bust cycles
- **Project-based** — revenue is lumpy; project pipeline matters more than ARR
- **Material cost volatility** — lumber, steel, copper prices swing wildly

## 2. Sub-Segments

### Commercial Real Estate (CRE)
- **Players**: Brokerages (CBRE, JLL, Cushman & Wakefield), owners (REITs, pension funds), property managers
- **Buyer**: Head of Leasing, Property Manager, Asset Manager, Head of Marketing
- **Cycle**: 6-12 months
- **Pain**: Tenant acquisition, lease management, valuation analytics
- **Tools**: MRI, Yardi, CoStar, RealNexus

### Residential Brokerage
- **Players**: Compass, Keller Williams, RE/MAX, eXp, Sotheby's, local brokerages
- **Buyer**: Broker/Owner, Team Lead, Head of Growth
- **Cycle**: 30-90 days
- **Pain**: Agent recruitment, lead conversion, transaction management
- **Tools**: Follow Up Boss, KVCore, Top Producer, dotloop, DocuSign

### Real Estate Developers
- **Players**: Local + regional developers (land entitlement through construction)
- **Buyer**: Principal, Development Director, Construction VP
- **Cycle**: 6-18 months
- **Pain**: Project management, contractor coordination, cost tracking
- **Tools**: Procore, Autodesk Build, CoConstruct, BuilderTrend

### General Contractors (GCs)
- **Players**: Turner, Skanska, Suffolk, DPR, regional GCs
- **Buyer**: Project Executive, VP Operations, Director of Preconstruction
- **Cycle**: 6-12 months
- **Pain**: Project margin, schedule adherence, RFIs/submittals, safety
- **Tools**: Procore, Autodesk Build, Bluebeam, PlanGrid

### Architecture & Engineering (AEC)
- **Players**: Gensler, HOK, Perkins&Will, AECOM, Jacobs, Stantec
- **Buyer**: Principal, Director of Practice, BIM Manager
- **Cycle**: 3-9 months
- **Pain**: Design collaboration, project profitability, talent retention
- **Tools**: Revit, AutoCAD, Civil 3D, Rhino, Grasshopper

### PropTech / ConTech (Vendors)
- **Players**: VTS, CompStak, Engrain, Procore, OpenSpace, Hover
- **Buyer**: CTO, VP Product, VP Sales
- **Cycle**: 3-9 months
- **Pain**: Customer acquisition, integration with legacy systems, scaling

## 3. Buyer Personas

### The Broker/Owner (Residential)
- **At**: Local/regional brokerage
- **Cares about**: Agent count, transaction volume, commission splits, market share
- **Pain**: Agent turnover, lead conversion, brand differentiation
- **Outreach angle**: Specific agent productivity metrics, peer brokerage references
- **What works**: Tactical lead gen tactics, retention metrics
- **Channels**: Email, real estate conferences (NAR, Inman)

### The Property Manager (CRE)
- **At**: Building owner / property management firm
- **Cares about**: Occupancy rate, tenant satisfaction, NOI (net operating income)
- **Pain**: Manual lease administration, tenant complaints, vendor management
- **Outreach angle**: NOI improvement, tenant satisfaction scores
- **What works**: Specific building-level case studies

### The Project Executive (GC)
- **At**: Mid-large general contractor
- **Cares about**: Schedule, budget, safety record, client satisfaction
- **Pain**: RFIs (request for information), submittals, change orders, weather delays
- **Outreach angle**: Schedule compression, margin improvement
- **What works**: References from peer GCs; integrations with Procore/Autodesk

### The Director of Preconstruction
- **At**: Mid-large GC
- **Cares about**: Estimate accuracy, bid win rate, subcontractor coverage
- **Pain**: Quantity takeoff time, historical cost data, subcontractor bids
- **Outreach angle**: Bid win rate improvement, takeoff speed
- **What works**: Hard ROI numbers (hours saved per bid)

## 5. Trigger Events

- **New project award** — tools needed for new project
- **Office expansion** — scaling, new tech purchases
- **New principal / partner hire** — strategic shift
- **Layoffs** — cost optimization
- **M&A** — system consolidation
- **Trade show participation** (NAHB, World of Concrete, AIA Conference)
- **Local market shift** (interest rate change, zoning change)
- **Material price spike** — cost optimization mode
- **New regulation** (e.g., building energy codes) — compliance projects
- **Insurance premium increase** — safety/loss-control spend

## 8. Outreach Best Practices

Real estate and construction buyers value **local references** and **specific ROI numbers**. Generic outreach fails.

### Residential Broker Outreach
```
Subject: agent productivity benchmark for [market] brokerages

Hi [Name],

We work with peer brokerages in [state/region] like [peer 1] and 
[peer 2] on lead conversion — specifically moving leads to 
appointments at >25% conversion (industry avg is 8-12%).

At [peer 1], we implemented [specific system] and saw agent 
production lift 30% in 90 days.

Worth a quick call?

Best,
[Name]
```

### GC Outreach
```
Subject: RFI cycle time benchmark

Hi [Name],

We work with peer GCs like [peer GC 1] and [peer GC 2] on RFI 
and submittal workflow — typical RFI cycle time is 7-14 days; 
top performers achieve 3-5 days with [specific system].

At [peer GC], we reduced RFI cycle time by 45%, saving ~$80K 
per $50M project in overhead.

Worth sharing the case study?

Best,
[Name]
```

## 9. Compliance Considerations

- **Real estate licensing** — outreach cannot imply you're a licensed agent if you're not
- **Fair Housing Act (US)** — no discriminatory language in property-related outreach
- **RESA / RESPA** — Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (US) — kickback rules
- **TCPA** — Cold calling restrictions (especially for FSBO leads)
- **State real estate commission rules** — vary by state
- **Local building codes** — claims about compliance must be verified

## 10. Common Prospecting Mistakes

- **Wrong stakeholder** — pitching PropTech to a GC
- **Wrong region** — pitching US-specific compliance to EU firm
- **No local references** — local market credibility is essential
- **Generic "transform your business"** — meaningless
- **Wrong project stage** — pitching design tools during construction phase
- **Ignoring the GC-subcontractor relationship** — GCs may or may not influence subcontractor tool choices
- **No specific ROI** — "save time" is useless; "$80K savings per $50M project" is useful
