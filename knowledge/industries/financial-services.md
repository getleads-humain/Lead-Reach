# Financial Services Industry — Prospecting Guide

## 1. Industry Overview

Financial services is a **$22 trillion global industry** spanning banking, insurance, investment management, payments, and fintech. It is the **most heavily regulated industry on earth** — every product, vendor, and process is subject to oversight by SEC, FINRA, OCC, FDIC, CFPB (US), FCA (UK), BaFin (Germany), MAS (Singapore), and dozens of others.

Key characteristics:
- **Regulated everywhere** — Multiple regulators per country; cross-border adds complexity
- **Risk-averse** — Failed products can cost billions in fines
- **Long sales cycles** — 12-24 months for enterprise bank deals
- **Security-obsessed** — SOC 2 Type II minimum; ISO 27001; sometimes FedRAMP
- **Procurement-heavy** — RFPs, vendor risk assessments, security questionnaires
- **Talent-poor** — Bank IT teams are stretched thin; modernization backlog is huge

## 2. Financial Services Sub-Segments

### Banking (Retail & Commercial)
- **Buyer**: Head of Digital, CIO, Head of Retail Banking, Head of Commercial Banking
- **Cycle**: 12-24 months for enterprise bank
- **Pain**: Legacy core systems (Jack Henry, FIS, Fiserv), digital transformation, regulatory reporting
- **Tools**: Core banking (FIS, Fiserv, Jack Henry, Temenos), CRM (Salesforce Financial Services Cloud), digital banking platforms (Q2, Alkami)

### Fintech
- **Sub-types**: Payments (Stripe, Square), Neobanks (Chime, Revolut), Lending (SoFi, LendingClub), Wealth (Betterment, Wealthfront), B2B (Brex, Ramp)
- **Buyer**: CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Product
- **Cycle**: 1-6 months (faster than traditional finance)
- **Pain**: Scaling, compliance, fraud, customer acquisition cost
- **Tools**: Cloud-native (AWS, GCP), microservices, modern stack

### Insurance (P&C, Life, Reinsurance)
- **Buyer**: CIO, Head of Claims, Head of Underwriting, Chief Actuary
- **Cycle**: 6-18 months
- **Pain**: Legacy policy admin systems (Guidewire, Duck Creek), claims automation, fraud detection
- **Tools**: Guidewire, Duck Creek, Majesco, Salesforce Financial Services Cloud

### Wealth & Asset Management
- **Buyer**: COO, CIO, Head of Operations, Chief Compliance Officer
- **Cycle**: 6-12 months
- **Pain**: Portfolio management systems (Bloomberg, BlackRock Aladdin), regulatory reporting, client portals
- **Tools**: Bloomberg Terminal, BlackRock Aladdin, Charles River IMS, Salesforce

### Payments
- **Sub-types**: Card networks (Visa, Mastercard), processors (Stripe, Adyen, Fiserv), PSPs (Worldpay, Checkout.com), BNPL (Affirm, Klarna)
- **Buyer**: Head of Payments, CTO, VP Engineering
- **Cycle**: 3-12 months
- **Pain**: Authorization rates, fraud, cross-border, integration complexity

## 3. Financial Services Buyer Personas

### The Bank CIO
- **At**: Mid-large bank ($10B+ assets)
- **Cares about**: Modernization, cost reduction, regulatory compliance, vendor risk
- **Pain**: Legacy systems, talent shortage, board pressure on digital
- **Outreach angle**: Specific bank case studies (peer references are gold), regulatory compliance, integration with their core
- **Channels**: Email, executive events (Money20/20, Finovate), peer CIO networks
- **What works**: Hard references from similar-size banks; ROI backed by independent analyst reports
- **What fails**: "Disrupt your bank" messaging — they don't want disruption, they want evolution

### The Fintech CTO
- **At**: VC-backed fintech (Series A+)
- **Cares about**: Velocity, scalability, security posture, compliance
- **Pain**: Building vs. buying, vendor sprawl, audit prep, scaling team
- **Outreach angle**: Developer experience, integration speed, compliance out-of-the-box
- **Channels**: LinkedIn, Twitter/X, engineering blogs, fintech-specific events
- **What works**: Technical depth, references from peer fintechs, security/compliance details
- **What fails**: Sales-y pitches, missing technical specs

### The Head of Claims (Insurance)
- **At**: P&C insurer
- **Cares about**: Cycle time, leakage, customer satisfaction, fraud detection
- **Pain**: Manual processes, fraud losses, customer churn post-claim
- **Outreach angle**: Specific claims KPIs, fraud reduction %, CX improvement
- **Channels**: Email, insurance events (ITC Vegas, ACORD)
- **What works**: Concrete case studies (e.g., "reduced claim cycle time by 40% at [peer insurer]")

### The Chief Compliance Officer
- **At**: Any regulated financial institution
- **Cares about**: Regulatory exams, audit findings, AML/KYC, consumer protection
- **Pain**: Regulatory change management, audit prep, vendor risk
- **Outreach angle**: Specific regulation expertise, exam preparation, automated compliance
- **Channels**: Email, compliance officer networks, regulatory technology events

## 5. Financial Services Trigger Events

- **Regulatory change** (e.g., new CFPB rule) — creates compliance projects
- **Consent order / enforcement action** — mandatory remediation spend
- **M&A** — system consolidation
- **New product launch** (e.g., neobank launching credit product)
- **Funding round** — fintech scaling
- **Earnings call** — bank priorities revealed
- **New executive hire** from a tech-forward competitor
- **Branch closure announcement** — digital transformation
- **Partnership announcement** — integration opportunities
- **Audit failure** — remediation spend

## 6. Financial Services Vocabulary

- **AML / KYC** — Anti-Money Laundering / Know Your Customer
- **BSA** — Bank Secrecy Act
- **OFAC** — Office of Foreign Assets Control (sanctions)
- **PCI DSS** — Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard
- **SOX** — Sarbanes-Oxley (financial reporting controls)
- **CCAR / DFAST** — Federal Reserve stress tests (US banks)
- **MiFID II** — EU investment services regulation
- **GDPR** — EU data protection (financial institutions are heavily affected)
- **PSD2** — EU payment services directive (open banking)
- **Open banking** — Bank APIs for third parties
- **Core banking system** — The system of record for accounts, transactions
- **Payment rails** — ACH, wire, RTP, FedNow, SEPA
- **NPS** — Net Promoter Score (used heavily in banking)
- **Authorization rate** — % of payment attempts that succeed
- **Chargeback** — Reversed transaction
- **Leakage** — Insurance term for claim overpayment
- **Lapse rate** — Insurance term for policy non-renewal
- **AUM** — Assets Under Management
- **NAV** — Net Asset Value

## 8. Outreach Best Practices for Financial Services

### Bank Outreach
```
Subject: peer reference - [specific bank pain point]

Hi [Name],

Noticed [their bank] recently announced [trigger event]. We work with peer 
institutions like [peer bank 1] and [peer bank 2] on [specific problem].

At [peer bank], we achieved [specific outcome] in [timeframe] with [ROI]. 
I can share the case study if relevant.

Worth a 20-minute conversation? Happy to send the case study first if 
you prefer to review internally.

Best,
[Name]
```

Key elements:
- **Peer references** are mandatory — without them, no bank will engage
- **Specific outcome** with numbers
- **Soft CTA** — don't push for meeting immediately; offer to share info first
- **Compliance-aware** language (mention SOC 2, audit-ready)

### Fintech Outreach
More direct than bank outreach. Fintech buyers respond to:
- **Technical depth** — show you understand their stack
- **Velocity** — fast integration, fast time-to-value
- **Developer experience** — SDKs, API docs, sandboxes
- **References from peer fintechs** — but peer set is different (Series A-C, not banks)

## 9. Compliance Considerations for Outreach

- **FINRA** regulates broker-dealer communications — outreach to financial advisors has specific rules
- **TCPA** (Telephone Consumer Protection Act) — auto-dialers and SMS need consent
- **GLBA** — financial institution data sharing restrictions
- **State-level** — New York DFS 500 (cybersecurity), California CCPA, etc.
- **Cross-border** — different rules for EU vs US vs APAC prospects

LeadReach agents should NOT:
- Send cold emails to consumer-facing banking customers (only B2B contacts)
- Make automated calls without consent (TCPA)
- Store financial account numbers (GLBA)
- Claim FINRA/SEC registration that doesn't exist

## 10. Common Financial Services Prospecting Mistakes

- **Wrong regulator** — pitching a US bank with EU-specific compliance language
- **No peer references** — banks won't engage without them
- **Ignoring the core system** — every solution must integrate with FIS/Fiserv/Jack Henry
- **Underestimating security review** — 3-6 months for security review is normal
- **Consumer vs. commercial** — consumer banking is regulated differently; targeting retail customers is illegal in many jurisdictions
- **Not knowing the charter type** — bank vs. credit union vs. fintech (different rules)
