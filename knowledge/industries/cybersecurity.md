# Cybersecurity — Industry Intelligence

## 1. Market Shape

Cybersecurity is one of the largest and most fragmented B2B verticals, with over 3,500 vendors competing across 30+ sub-categories (network security, endpoint, identity, cloud security, data security, application security, GRC, threat intelligence, etc.). The defining characteristic is **fear-driven buying** — deals accelerate after a breach (theirs or a peer's), and stall during "peacetime" when budgets shift to growth. CISOs report "tool fatigue" as their #1 pain point, with the average enterprise running 70+ security tools from 40+ vendors.

The market is split into three motions:
1. **Enterprise direct** — long sales cycles (6–12 months), $100k+ ACV, dominated by Palo Alto, CrowdStrike, Microsoft, Zscaler. New entrants need a wedge — usually a novel threat vector or compliance mandate.
2. **SMB / mid-market SaaS** — shorter cycles (30–90 days), $5k–$50k ACV, sold to IT leads or fractional CISOs.
3. **MSSP / channel** — sold to managed security service providers who bundle and resell to SMBs. Higher upfront friction, lower CAC, longer LTV.

LeadReach is best positioned for motions #2 and #3 — vendors scaling into mid-market or building an MSSP channel.

## 5. Buying Triggers

- **Public breach disclosure** at the prospect or a named peer — highest-signal trigger, 30-day window.
- **CISO hire** announced within last 60 days — new leadership re-evaluates stack within 90 days.
- **SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / HIPAA audit scheduled** — compliance tooling budget unlocks.
- **New regulatory mandate** announced (SEC cyber disclosure, EU NIS2, DORA) — panic buying window.
- **Hiring security engineers** in last 30 days — capacity to evaluate new tools.
- **Recent funding round** — capital to invest in security posture.
- **Multi-cloud migration announced** — cloud security tooling required.

## 8. Regional Variants

- **US**: Shortest cycles, highest ACV. Federal (FedRAMP) is a separate motion entirely.
- **EU**: NIS2 (Oct 2024) and DORA (Jan 2025) are creating urgency. GDPR adds 2-4 weeks to security review.
- **UK**: NCSC guidance drives procurement. Public sector via Crown Commercial Service framework.
- **APAC**: Australia (ACSC Essential Eight) is most mature; Singapore (MAS TRM) for financial services.
- **Middle East**: Saudi (NCA) and UAE (NESA) regulations driving spend.

## 10. Compliance Notes

- **SOC 2 Type II** is the baseline for any enterprise cybersecurity deal.
- **ISO 27001** required for EU customers.
- **FedRAMP Moderate** required for US federal; "In Process" status acceptable for many agencies.
- **PCI DSS** for any vendor touching payment data.
- **HIPAA** for any vendor touching PHI.
- **StateRAMP** for US state and local government.
- **Common Criteria** for international government sales.

## 11. Outreach Ethics

- **Never exploit a breach** in cold outreach. Reference it respectfully or not at all.
- **Don't name the breached company's vendor stack** in marketing materials.
- **Don't FUD-sell** (Fear, Uncertainty, Doubt) — buyers see through it and lose trust.
- **Do reference peer best practices** and regulatory guidance.
