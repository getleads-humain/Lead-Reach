# Manufacturing & Industrial — Industry Intelligence

## 1. Market Shape

Manufacturing and industrial is a slow-moving, relationship-heavy, technical-buyer-led vertical. The defining characteristics are: **long sales cycles** (6–24 months for enterprise, 3–9 months for mid-market), **engineer-led buying** with finance sign-off, **Industry 4.0 / IIoT as the dominant transformation narrative**, **supply-chain volatility as a recurring pain point**, and **a regional manufacturing base** (US Midwest, Germany, Japan, China, Mexico) with strong local supplier networks.

Buying is consensus-driven with 4–8 stakeholders. The economic buyer is usually a Plant Manager, VP Operations, or COO. The technical buyer is a Chief Engineer, Director of Quality, or Director of IT/OT. Trust is built through plant visits, technical demos, and pilot programs — not cold outreach alone.

LeadReach is best positioned for SaaS-for-manufacturing vendors (MES, QMS, predictive maintenance, supply chain visibility) selling TO mid-market manufacturers. LeadReach is also useful for industrial distributors seeking to expand their account base.

## 6. Buying Triggers

- **ERP migration** announcement (SAP S/4HANA, Oracle Cloud, Epicor) — 90-day window for adjacent tools.
- **Plant expansion or new facility** announcement.
- **M&A activity** (acquired or acquiring).
- **Hiring VP Operations / Plant Manager** in last 60 days.
- **Hiring Director of Digital / IT/OT** in last 60 days.
- **Supply chain disruption** disclosed in earnings or press.
- **New ESG / sustainability report** published.
- **Industry 4.0 initiative** announced (press release, conference talk).
- **Recent quality issue or recall** disclosed.
- **Trade show appearance** at IMTS, Hannover Messe, FABTECH, MODEX announced.

## 8. Channels & Sequencing

Manufacturing outbound rewards **technical depth and patience**. Recommended sequence:

- **Day 0**: LinkedIn connection — reference a recent plant expansion, ERP migration, or leadership change.
- **Day 3**: Cold email #1 — 140 words, lead with a peer-manufacturer case study with operational metrics (OEE, throughput, downtime), soft CTA.
- **Day 7**: LinkedIn engagement — comment on operational / industry content.
- **Day 10**: Cold email #2 — technical white paper or industry report (not a sales pitch).
- **Day 14: Direct mail** — printed technical brief or industry report (manufacturing buyers respond well to physical mail).
- **Day 17: Cold call** — reference the mail and the emails; lead with peer reference.
- **Day 21: Cold email #3** — case study with ROI math.
- **Day 28: Breakup email**.
- **Day 35: Final email** — invite to a webinar or industry roundtable.

Expected meeting-booked rate: **1.0–2.0%** (lower than SaaS due to longer cycle and technical depth required).

## 9. Regional Variants

- **US Midwest**: Traditional manufacturing base; relationship-heavy; trade shows matter.
- **US South (Texas, Tennessee, Alabama)**: Auto and aerospace manufacturing growth; nearshoring impact.
- **Germany (Mittelstand)**: Family-owned mid-market; long cycles; technical depth essential.
- **Japan**: Slow consensus-driven buying; long cycles; quality-focused.
- **Mexico (Nearshoring)**: Rapid growth post-China+1; price-sensitive.
- **China**: Domestic vendors preferred; complex market for Western SaaS.
- **Southeast Asia (Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia)**: Emerging manufacturing; price-sensitive.
