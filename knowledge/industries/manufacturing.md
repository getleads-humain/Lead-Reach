# Manufacturing Industry — Prospecting Guide

## 1. Industry Overview

Manufacturing is a **$40 trillion global industry** encompassing everything from automotive assembly to semiconductor fabrication. It is capital-intensive, regulated, and undergoing a generational shift toward **Industry 4.0** — IoT, AI, robotics, and digital twins.

Key characteristics:
- **Capital-intensive** — Heavy investment in plants and equipment; multi-year ROI horizons
- **Long sales cycles** — 6-24 months for major equipment; 3-6 months for software
- **Risk-averse** — Failures cost millions in downtime; new vendors face scrutiny
- **Regulated** — ISO 9001, IATF 16949 (automotive), AS9100 (aerospace), FDA (medical devices)
- **Multi-stakeholder** — Engineering, procurement, operations, IT, finance all involved
- **Physical-digital blend** — IoT bridges OT (operational technology) and IT

## 2. Manufacturing Sub-Segments

### Discrete Manufacturing
Products you can count: automotive, electronics, machinery, appliances.
- **Buyer**: Engineering Manager, Plant Manager, Director of Operations
- **Cycle**: 6-18 months for major systems
- **Pain**: Production efficiency, quality control, supply chain visibility
- **Tools**: ERP (SAP, Oracle), MES (Siemens Opcenter, Rockwell), PLM (PTC, Dassault), SCADA

### Process Manufacturing
Products made in batches: chemicals, pharma, food, beverage, cosmetics.
- **Buyer**: Process Engineer, Quality Director, Plant Manager
- **Cycle**: 6-12 months
- **Pain**: Batch consistency, regulatory compliance, yield optimization
- **Tools**: ERP with process modules, LIMS (lab info), batch records, MES

### Industrial Machinery / Equipment Makers
Companies that make the machines other manufacturers use (OEMs).
- **Buyer**: VP Engineering, CTO, Product Manager
- **Cycle**: 12-24 months for major equipment
- **Pain**: Service revenue growth, predictive maintenance, connected products
- **Tools**: CAD (SolidWorks, AutoCAD), PLM, IoT platforms (PTC ThingWorx, Siemens MindSphere)

### Contract Manufacturers (CMs / EMS / CDMO)
Companies that manufacture for others: Foxconn, Flex, Jabil, Wistron (electronics); Catalent, Lonza (pharma).
- **Buyer**: Business Development, Operations VP
- **Cycle**: 6-18 months to land a new customer
- **Pain**: Customer acquisition, capacity utilization, NPI (new product introduction)
- **Tools**: ERP, MES, customer portals

## 3. Manufacturing Buyer Personas

### The Plant Manager
- **At**: Any manufacturer with a physical plant
- **Cares about**: OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness), uptime, throughput, safety, cost-per-unit
- **Pain**: Unplanned downtime, scrap/rework, labor shortage, energy costs
- **Outreach angle**: Specific OEE benchmarks, downtime reduction, similar-plant case studies
- **Channels**: Email, industry events, peer networks
- **What works**: Hard numbers (downtime %, OEE), references from similar plants

### The VP of Operations / COO
- **At**: Mid-large manufacturer (200+ FTE)
- **Cares about**: Cost reduction, capacity expansion, automation, multi-plant coordination
- **Pain**: Multi-plant visibility, labor costs, capex decisions
- **Outreach angle**: Network-wide optimization, automation ROI, expansion support
- **Channels**: Email, executive events, peer COO networks

### The Director of Quality
- **At**: Any regulated manufacturer
- **Cares about**: Defect rates, audit performance, CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Action), compliance
- **Pain**: Manual inspection, audit prep, supplier quality, traceability
- **Outreach angle**: Defect reduction %, audit prep automation, traceability
- **Channels**: Quality-specific events (ASQ), LinkedIn

### The Director of Engineering / CTO
- **At**: Industrial OEMs, tech-forward manufacturers
- **Cares about**: Innovation, product development velocity, IoT connectivity, AI adoption
- **Pain**: Legacy systems, integration complexity, talent shortage, vendor lock-in
- **Outreach angle**: Modernization, integration, talent multiplier

## 5. Manufacturing Trigger Events

- **New product launch** → NPI (New Product Introduction) process starting
- **Plant expansion / new facility** → capex cycle, tooling purchases
- **Layoff / restructuring** → cost optimization mode
- **New ERP/MES announcement** → 12-month integration cycle, adjacent tool purchases
- **Quality issue / recall** → quality system overhaul
- **Regulatory change** → compliance project
- **M&A** → integration, possible system consolidation
- **New executive hire** (especially from a tech-forward competitor) → modernization agenda
- **Sustainability announcement** → energy efficiency, carbon tracking tools
- **Trade show participation** (IMTS, Hannover Messe, FABTECH) → evaluation mode

## 6. Manufacturing Industry Vocabulary

- **OEE** — Overall Equipment Effectiveness (Availability × Performance × Quality)
- **MTBF / MTTR** — Mean Time Between Failures / Mean Time To Repair
- **Six Sigma / Lean** — Quality methodologies
- **5S** — Sort, Set in order, Shine, Standardize, Sustain
- **Kaizen** — Continuous improvement
- **CAPA** — Corrective and Preventive Action
- **NPI** — New Product Introduction
- **BOM** — Bill of Materials
- **MRP** — Material Requirements Planning
- **CAD / CAM / CAE** — Computer-Aided Design / Manufacturing / Engineering
- **SCADA** — Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition
- **PLC** — Programmable Logic Controller
- **CNC** — Computer Numerical Control
- **OT / IT** — Operational Technology / Information Technology
- **Industry 4.0** — Fourth industrial revolution (digital)
- **Digital twin** — Virtual model of physical asset
- **Predictive maintenance** — IoT-enabled maintenance prediction

## 7. Outreach Best Practices for Manufacturing

Manufacturing buyers are **skeptical of marketing hype**. Outreach must be:
- **Specific**: Reference their plant, product, or recent announcement
- **Quantified**: ROI calculations, benchmark comparisons
- **Patient**: Don't push for quick meetings; offer to send info first
- **Credible**: References from similar manufacturers are essential
- **Compliance-aware**: Mention relevant certifications (ISO 9001, IATF 16949)

### Email Template
```
Subject: OEE benchmark for [product type] plants

Hi [Name],

I noticed [their company] recently expanded the [specific plant] — congrats. 
We work with similar [vertical] manufacturers (e.g., [reference customer]) 
on OEE improvement, specifically reducing unplanned downtime on [equipment type].

Our typical impact: 8-15% OEE improvement in 90 days, with ROI in 4-6 months.

Worth a brief call? I can share the benchmark data even if there's no fit.

Best,
[Name]
```

## 9. Common Manufacturing Prospecting Mistakes

- **Pitching "digital transformation"** without specifics — meaningless to most manufacturers
- **Wrong stakeholder** — emailing the CEO about a plant floor tool
- **Ignoring OT/IT divide** — many plant systems report to Operations, not IT
- **Underestimating sales cycle** — 6-12 months is normal, not slow
- **Compliance blindness** — not mentioning relevant ISO/IATF certifications
- **No references** — manufacturing buyers always check references from similar companies
