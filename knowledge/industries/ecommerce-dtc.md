# E-Commerce & DTC — Industry Intelligence

## 1. Market Shape

E-Commerce and Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) is a fast-moving, performance-marketing-heavy, margin-pressured vertical. The defining characteristics are: **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) as the dominant financial metric**, **platform dependency** (Shopify, Amazon, Meta, Google), **post-iOS 14 attribution crisis** that has driven brands toward first-party data and retention, and **a bifurcated buyer landscape** between emerging DTC brands ($1M–$50M GMV), scaling brands ($50M–$500M GMV), and enterprise retailers ($500M+ GMV).

Sales cycles are short (2–8 weeks for SaaS-for-ecommerce tools, 1–4 weeks for agency services). Buying is consensus-driven but lean (2–4 stakeholders). The buyer values proof-of-revenue-impact above all else — case studies must show specific ROAS (Return on Ad Spend), AOV (Average Order Value), or LTV (Lifetime Value) improvements.

LeadReach is best positioned for the SaaS-for-ecommerce vendor segment selling TO ecommerce brands (i.e., LeadReach helps these vendors find their next 100 customers). LeadReach is not directly useful to a DTC brand selling to consumers — that requires a different motion (paid social, influencer, marketplace).

## 8. Channels & Sequencing

Ecommerce outbound rewards **speed and personalization**. Recommended sequence:

- **Day 0**: LinkedIn connection — reference a recent funding, product, or hiring announcement.
- **Day 1**: Cold email #1 — 80 words, lead with a vertical-specific case study, hard CTA ("15-min call Tuesday at 2pm ET?").
- **Day 3**: LinkedIn engagement — like + comment on recent post.
- **Day 5**: Cold email #2 — new case study + social proof.
- **Day 7: Cold call** — reference the emails, lead with case study.
- **Day 10**: Cold email #3 — breakup email.
- **Day 14: Final email** — value-add resource (no ask).

Expected meeting-booked rate: **2.5–4.0%** (higher than SaaS due to shorter cycle and personalization appetite).

## 9. Regional Variants

- **US**: Largest market, highest ACV, most competitive.
- **EU**: GDPR affects outreach to ecommerce brands; consent-first messaging.
- **UK**: Mature ecommerce market; Brexit affects payments + logistics messaging.
- **APAC**: Rapid growth; marketplaces (Shopee, Lazada, Tokopedia) dominate.
- **LATAM (Mercado Libre)**: Fast-growing; price-sensitive.
- **India (Flipkart, Meesho)**: Massive volume; very price-sensitive.
